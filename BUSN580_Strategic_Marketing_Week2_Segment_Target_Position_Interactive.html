Week 2 Interactive: Market Assessment

Week 2 objective: Classify the bases used to segment a market and match each company to the target-market strategy it uses.

Four segmentation bases

BehavioralBenefits sought, usage rate, usage situation, loyalty status.
DemographicAge, income, gender, family life cycle, education, occupation.
GeographicRegion, city size, population density, climate.
PsychographicActivities, interests, opinions, values, attitudes, lifestyles.

Practice 1: which segmentation base?

Ridgeway Roasters, a fictional coffee company, is profiling its customers. Sort each variable it is considering.

Practice 2: which targeting strategy?

Match each fictional company to the target-market strategy it is using.

Takeaway: Segment with more than one base, target the segments you can serve profitably and that fit your mission, and position your offering where competitors are not clustered. Microtargeting individuals with combined personal data raises ethical and privacy questions, so use customer data with care.
