Week 2 Interactive: Reliability and Validity of Relationship Assessments

Week 2 objective: Identify the kinds of evidence that show whether a couples assessment is reliable and valid before you recommend it.

Two questions for any assessment

Psychologists do not assume a measure works; they collect data to show that it does. They ask two questions:

  • Reliability: Is it consistent? Over time (test-retest), across its items (internal consistency), and across observers (inter-rater). A correlation of +.80 or higher is generally considered good.
  • Validity: Does it measure what it claims? Face validity (it looks right) is weak evidence; content validity asks whether the items cover the whole construct; criterion validity asks whether scores relate to outcomes they should predict.
