Week 2 Interactive: Descriptive or Predictive?

Week 2 objective: Evaluate business situations with descriptive analytics and identify where predictive analytics and other data sources add value (CLOs 2 and 3).

Two kinds of analytics

Describe: what is happening?
Query and reporting tools: canned reports, ad hoc reports, dashboards, and OLAP. Managers use them to see and explore what is happening inside the organization.
Predict: what is likely next?
Data mining uses computers to find hidden patterns and build models from large data sets: customer segmentation, promotion targeting, market basket analysis, collaborative filtering, and churn.

Practice 1: describe or predict?

A fictional clothing retailer, Harbor & Pine, uses analytics across the business. Label each use.

Practice 2: which reporting tool?

Match each request at Harbor & Pine to the reporting tool that fits it best.

Practice 3: where would the data come from?

Harbor & Pine wants to know more about its customers. Choose the best source for each question.

Takeaway: Descriptive tools show what is happening; data mining finds patterns that point to what is likely next. Both depend on choosing the right data source.
