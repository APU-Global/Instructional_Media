Governance, risk, and compliance

GRC is “a strategic approach to balancing enterprise risk, compliance with regulation, and organizational governance.”

Governance
Processes, policies, and accountability for who decides: aligning IS with strategy and prioritizing work.
Risk
What do we need to protect, what could happen, and how do we protect it? Controls such as separation of duties and audit trails.
Compliance
Legal or professionally binding steps (HIPAA, PCI). Audits check them, but compliance does not equal security.
