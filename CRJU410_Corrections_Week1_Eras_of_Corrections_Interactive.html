Practice: Name the approach

Each description is of a fictional town or facility. Choose the approach it reflects.

Takeaway: Each era answered the question what is punishment for? differently: fear and retribution in the colonies, penitence in the penitentiary, reform in the reformatory, and treatment and reintegration in the 20th century. Each design had strengths, and each harmed someone, which is why corrections still debates punishment versus treatment.
