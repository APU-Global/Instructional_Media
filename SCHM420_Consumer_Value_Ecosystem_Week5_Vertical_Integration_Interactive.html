Week 5 Interactive: Vertical Integration Moves

Week 5 objective: Classify vertical integration moves as backward or forward and separate the reasons to integrate from the risks.

Key terms

Vertical integration means getting involved in new portions of the value chain. Backward integration enters a supplier's business; forward integration enters a buyer's business. Firms can integrate on their own (opening company-owned stores) or by acquisition.

Integration reduces the leverage of powerful suppliers or buyers and can secure stable, high-quality inputs, but it can also pull a firm into businesses that need very different skills or breed complacency in an owned unit.
