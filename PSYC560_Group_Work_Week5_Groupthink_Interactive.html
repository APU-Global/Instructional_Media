Part 3: Which trap is it?

Groupthink is not the only decision trap. Name the one each fictional group fell into.

Takeaway: Groupthink happens when members’ strivings for unanimity override realistic appraisal of alternatives. Its warning signs include pressure, self-censorship, and an illusion of unanimity, and its causes are cohesion, isolation, biased leadership, and stress. Leaders protect decisions with impartiality, devil’s advocates, outside views, structured techniques, and premortems.
