Week 1 Interactive: Theories of Emotion

Week 1 objective: Distinguish the Cannon-Bard, James-Lange, and two-factor theories of emotion by the role each gives to bodily arousal.

Three ways to order the same moment

Cannon-Bard
Event → emotion and arousal together. You feel afraid as your heart starts racing.
James-Lange
Event → arousal → emotion. "We feel sorry because we cry, angry because we strike, afraid because we tremble" (William James).
Two-factor
Arousal + cognitive appraisal = emotion. Arousal sets the strength; your interpretation of the situation decides which emotion it is.
