Week 6 Interactive: Depression Prevalence

Week objective: Calculate prevalence rates by gender and interpret differences in depression prevalence between groups.

Before you compare two states

  • Same definition? A major depressive episode (NSDUH) and a PHQ-9 score of 10 or more (NHANES) give different rates.
  • Same population? Age, sex, and income all shift prevalence.
  • Combine counts, not percentages. Overall prevalence = all cases ÷ all people.

Questions use national figures from NIMH and NCHS.

Takeaway: Calculate prevalence from counts, compare only like with like (same definition, ages, and years), and let the gaps in the data (sex, age, income, and who receives care) point to the initiatives a community needs.
