Week 5 Interactive: Evidence From Stakeholders

Week 5 objective: Identify stakeholders, analyze their interest and influence, and gather their evidence ethically.

Who has a stake?

Stakeholders are people, groups, organizations, or communities that may be affected by an effort, may affect its outcome, or have a significant responsibility or interest in it. A common map places them by influence and interest, but influence alone should not decide importance (Center for Community Health and Development, n.d.). Stakeholder evidence is one of the four sources of evidence in EBMgt (Hasanpoor et al., 2018).
