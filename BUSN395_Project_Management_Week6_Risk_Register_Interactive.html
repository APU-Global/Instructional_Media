Week 6 Interactive: Risk Planning and Management

Week 6 objective: Evaluate risks by probability and impact, find the critical few, and choose a response for each.

The case: Harborview Food Festival

A fictional city nonprofit runs a one-day outdoor food festival on the waterfront, with 30 food vendors, two stages, and a lead sponsor. You are building its risk register. Watt describes a risk as "any uncertain event or condition that might affect your project," evaluated by how likely it is and how much damage it would do.

Step 1: Rate each risk

Read the cues, then rate probability and impact as Low, Medium, or High. This activity multiplies the two (1–3 each) into a simple rating score.

Step 2: See the matrix

Your ratings place each risk on a probability and impact grid. The mitigation plan focuses on the risks rated high on both.

Probability (rows) by impact (columns)

Step 3: Name the response

Chapter 16 gives four basic ways to handle a risk (avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept) and also describes risk sharing with a partner. Classify each planned action.

Takeaway: Not all risks are equal. Rate each one for probability and impact, focus the mitigation plan on the critical few that are high on both, and record a response for every risk: avoid, mitigate, transfer, share, or accept. Back the biggest ones with a contingency plan.
