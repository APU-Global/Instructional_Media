Week 2 Interactive: Motivating an Underperforming Team

Week 2 objective: Diagnose why a team is struggling and match the situation to a motivation theory, as Discussion 2 asks you to do.

Lenses from the readings

Team barriersFloundering (unclear goals), dominating members, poor performance of some members, and poorly managed conflict.
Expectancy theoryEffort leads to performance (expectancy), performance leads to rewards (instrumentality), and the rewards are valued (valence).
Equity theoryPeople compare their inputs and outcomes with others'; perceived unfairness lowers effort.
Two-factor theoryHygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators such as achievement, recognition, and responsibility drive effort.
Goal settingSMART goals (specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, timely) give direction and energy.
Job characteristics modelSkill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, and feedback make work motivating.

Part 2: Which motivation lens fits best?

Choose the theory that best explains each employee's low effort.

Takeaway: Before you try to motivate a team, diagnose it. Unclear goals, a dominating voice, a skill gap, or a feud each need a different fix. When motivation is the issue, choose the lens that fits: expectancy, equity, hygiene versus motivators, goal setting, or job design.
