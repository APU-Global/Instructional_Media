Week 5 Interactive: Stop, Think, Listen, Communicate

Week 5 objective: Apply the STLC model and recognize integrative, distributive, and avoidant tactics so you can give specific advice in Discussion 5.

From this week's reading

  • Stop: take a break to move from a reactive stance to a proactive one.
  • Think: what is the conflict really about? Options: do nothing, change yourself, change the other person, or change the situation.
  • Listen: suspend judgment and try to see the conflict through the other person's eyes.
  • Communicate: be assertive without verbal aggression, and watch your nonverbal behavior.
  • Tactics: integrative (win-win), distributive (win-lose), and avoidant. Strategies are often reciprocated.
