PSYC 450 · Family Systems · 3 credits · 8 weeks. Explore the course below, then build your own success plan.

This course gives you an overview of the development of the family system, including dynamic family relationships, cultural factors, and Christian worldview implications. You will learn major family systems theories, apply them to real and fictional families, and explore psychological interventions through an ethical framework.
