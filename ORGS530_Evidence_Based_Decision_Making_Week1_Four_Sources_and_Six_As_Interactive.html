Week 1 objective: Identify the four sources of evidence and the six steps of evidence-based management.

Evidence from multiple sources

Evidence-based practice means making decisions “through the conscientious, explicit and judicious use of the best available evidence from multiple sources to increase the likelihood of a favorable outcome” (Barends et al., 2017). A systematic review of health care managers describes four sources (scientific, organizational, experiential, and stakeholder evidence) and a six-step decision cycle (Hasanpoor et al., 2018). Your course calls the steps the six A’s.

Practice 1: which source of evidence is it?

Harbor Point Logistics (fictional) is deciding whether to adopt a new performance-review practice. Choose the source each piece of evidence comes from.

Practice 2: which of the six A’s?

Takeaway: Good decisions draw on all four sources and move through Ask, Acquire, Appraise, Aggregate, Apply, Assess. Use both lists as the outline for your Evaluating the Strength of the Evidence project.
