Executive Directive

Finance Manager Briefing & Instructions

When balancing budgets, both revenues and expenses have to be considered as well as how different decisions may affect process, finances, and patient outcomes.

A clinical laboratory is a healthcare entity that receives revenues by providing laboratory testing and services as well as incurring operational expenses. The number of laboratory tests is growing and now accounts for billions of dollars in annual medical care costs.

The Challenge

You are the finance manager for the laboratory service area. The healthcare organization has reduced your budget by 5% and now you need to recommend costs to reduce or ways to increase revenue. Use the Budget Decision Simulation to make your decision and determine the outcome.

The goal is to make budget decisions that will contribute to reducing and balancing the budget.

Summary/Conclusion Feedback :

There is no right or wrong way to approach budgeting. Finance professionals have many variables to consider when making budget adjustments that affect more than just balancing a budget. Some non-financial factors to consider are impacts on maintaining or improving staff morale, matching industry standards and practices, maintaining positive relationships with suppliers and customers and improving your business reputation with the local community.
