Week 7 Interactive: Safety Sweep

Week 7 objective: Apply California's supervision and safe-environment principles to common PE situations, for every learner.

Safety by design

Sight and sound. Stay close to every student and scan constantly.
Know your students. Be aware of each student’s health status and needs.
Space. Buffer zones, no shared boundary lines, hazards reported.
Equipment. Teach safe use first; use it only as intended.
Matching. Pair students by size and ability for contact.
Conditions. Plan for weather and air quality.

Practice: what would you do?

Situations are fictional. Choose the best response, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: A safe PE environment is both physical and psychological. Plan rules and supervision into the lesson, check the space and equipment before students arrive, and know each student well enough to keep everyone safe and included.

Principles from the California Department of Education (2009), Physical Education Framework for California Public Schools: Chapter 6 “Establishing a Safe Environment,” Chapter 7 “Establishing an Inclusive Environment,” and Chapter 8 facility safety. Situations are fictional.
