Week 4 Interactive: Build a Training Program Framework

Week 4 focus: Chapter 8 lays out a training program framework that starts with a needs assessment and ends with measuring effectiveness. Practice each piece here before you begin Develop a Group Training Program | Part 1 - Analysis and Topic.

Part 1: Which level of needs assessment?

The text names three levels: organizational (what the company needs to meet its strategic objectives), occupational or task (the tasks, skills, knowledge, and abilities a job requires), and individual (what one employee needs). Classify each fictional finding.

Part 2: Is it a good learning objective?

Good learning objectives are performance based and clear, and the result can be observed or measured. Decide whether each draft objective for a fictional customer-service training is ready to use.

Part 3: Which Kirkpatrick level does it measure?

Kirkpatrick’s model has four levels: Reaction (how participants reacted), Learning (how much knowledge and skill improved), Behavior (whether behavior changed), and Results (what benefits the organization gained). Classify each evaluation method.

Part 4: Your framework checklist

The text lists ten considerations for developing a training program. Check each one as you plan your Group Training Program; your progress is not saved or sent anywhere.

Takeaway: Begin with a needs assessment at the organizational, task, and individual levels; write objectives a trainee can demonstrate; match delivery mode, style, budget, and timelines to the audience; and plan how you will measure reaction, learning, behavior, and results before the training starts.
