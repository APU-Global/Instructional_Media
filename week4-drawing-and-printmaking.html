Drawing is seeing. Contour lines wrap around a form and show its shape. Shading shows which way it faces the light. Practice at least one shape technique and one shading technique in every drawing.

Drag the red dot

That is the vanishing point. Receding lines (orthogonals) all aim at it. It sits on the horizon line, at the viewer’s eye level.
