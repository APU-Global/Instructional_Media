Week 4 Interactive: Reading Cues, Responding Well

Week 4 objective: Recognize the main types of nonverbal communication and practice active listening responses that bridge communication barriers.

No foolproof dictionary

Nonverbal communication conveys a message without words, and it can complement, repeat, replace, mask, or contradict what someone says. There is no foolproof dictionary for it, and norms for space, time, eye contact, and silence vary by culture. Active listening means focusing on the message rather than your internal monologue, restating it in your own words, and asking clarifying questions.

Takeaway: Treat every cue as information to check, not a verdict. Restate, ask, and wait before you conclude.
