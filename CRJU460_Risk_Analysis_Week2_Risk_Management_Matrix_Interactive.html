Week 2 Interactive: Retain, Reduce, Transfer, or Avoid

Week 2 objective: Apply risk assessment fundamentals: describe risk, follow the risk management process, and choose a response.

The risk management matrix

The matrix places one risk by how often losses occur (frequency) and how bad they are (severity). Select each cell.

Select a card to see the details.

Practice 1: Choose the response

Each risk facing a fictional sheriff’s office is described by frequency and severity. Choose the response the matrix suggests.

Practice 2: Prevention or reduction?

Loss prevention lowers the probability of a loss. Loss reduction lowers its severity. Classify each control.

Practice 3: Order the risk management process

Arrange the steps in the order a risk manager follows them.

    Takeaway: Risk is a consequence of uncertainty. Measure how often losses happen and how severe they are, then retain, reduce, transfer, or avoid. When a risk cannot be avoided, as with answering emergencies, the work is to reduce and plan for it.

    Source: Saylor Academy (2012), Risk Management for Enterprises and Individuals, Sections 1.2, 4.1, and 4.4 (saylordotorg.github.io). Scenarios are fictional.
