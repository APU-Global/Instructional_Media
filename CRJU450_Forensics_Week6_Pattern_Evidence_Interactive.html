Practice 1: Within the science or overstated?

Each statement is fictional testimony. Decide whether it stays within what the evidence and research support.

Practice 2: Match the error rate

NIJ-funded research reviewed examinations in wrongful conviction cases. Choose the share of each discipline’s examinations that contained an incorrect identification or classification (Type 2 error).

Takeaway: Pattern evidence depends on careful collection and an examiner’s trained judgment. Its accuracy varies by discipline, so honest testimony states limits (“consistent with,” secondary transfer, shared sole designs). A juror weighs what the evidence proves, what it only suggests, and what was never tested.
