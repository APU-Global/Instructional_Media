Week 3 Interactive: The Physical-Emotional Connection

Week 3 objective: Distinguish sympathetic and parasympathetic responses and recognize the four emotional intelligence skills.

The body and the skills

The autonomic nervous system works with little voluntary control. Its sympathetic division energizes the heart, muscles, and adrenal glands for fight or flight ("go, go, go"). Its parasympathetic division curbs that mobilization ("stop, stop, stop").

The Mayer and Salovey four-branch model of emotional intelligence names four skills: perceiving emotion in faces, voices, and body language; using emotion to support thinking; understanding emotions, their causes, and how they combine or escalate; and managing emotion with strategies that prevent unwanted behavior.

Part 2: Which emotion skill?

Takeaway: Arousal arrives before reflection. The sympathetic system says go; the parasympathetic system says stop. Emotional intelligence adds four learnable skills, perceiving, using, understanding, and managing emotion, that let thinking guide what you do next.
