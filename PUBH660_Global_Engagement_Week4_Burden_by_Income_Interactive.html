Week 4 objective: Use WHO’s 2023 data on the leading causes of death to recognize how the burden of disease differs by country income group, a first step in setting global health priorities and choosing a focus for your Global Health Intervention Strategy.

Three categories, four income groups

WHO groups causes of death into three categories: communicable (infectious and parasitic diseases and maternal, perinatal and nutritional conditions), noncommunicable (chronic), and injuries. The World Bank classifies economies into four income groups by gross national income: low, lower-middle, upper-middle, and high.
