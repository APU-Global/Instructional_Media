Week 6 Interactive: Organizational Justice and Ethical Climate

Week 6 objective: Distinguish distributive, procedural, and interactional justice, and identify leadership practices that strengthen or weaken an ethical, inclusive climate.

Three kinds of fairness

  • Distributive justice: are the outcomes people receive fair?
  • Procedural justice: are decisions made fairly? Advance notice, voice, explanations, and consistent rules help.
  • Interactional justice: are people treated with respect, kindness, and dignity?

Climate matters too: without a culture of integrity, honesty, and trust, mandatory ethics training is often doomed to fail, and a commitment to a respectful workplace must be backed by accountability, not a compliance mindset.

Practice 1: Which kind of justice is at stake?

Each comment comes from a fictional employee survey at Pinecrest Health Partners. Choose the type of justice the comment raises.

Practice 2: Strengthens or weakens the climate?

Decide whether each fictional practice is likely to strengthen or weaken an ethical, inclusive climate.

Takeaway: An ethical, inclusive climate is built on fair outcomes, fair processes, and respectful treatment, modeled by leaders and backed by accountability and resources. Integrity, not mere compliance, is what makes the climate last.
