Week 3 Interactive: Piaget Tasks

Week 3 objective: Recognize the thinking behind children’s answers to classic Piaget tasks, as you will in your Observation Case Studies.

Two stages to watch for

Preoperational (about ages 2-7): children think symbolically and love pretend play, but they show egocentrism, precausal thinking (animism, artificialism), centration, and trouble with conservation and class inclusion. Concrete operational (about ages 7-11): children use logic about concrete things: they understand reversibility, reciprocity (a change in one quality is balanced by another), and classify objects in several ways. Ages vary from child to child.

Practice: name the thinking

Each child is fictional. Choose the concept that best explains the answer, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: When you observe a child for your case study, record the child’s exact words. The reason a child gives (“the tall one is taller” versus “it’s wider, so it’s the same”) tells you more about cognitive development than whether the answer is right.
