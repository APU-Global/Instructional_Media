Week 6 Interactive: Building KPIs

Week 6 objective: Write KPIs that are specific, measurable, and time-bound, and balance financial and nonfinancial measures across the four Balanced Scorecard areas.

Make it SMART

SpecificWho, what, where, when, which, and why.
MeasurableHow much? How many? How will I know?
AttainableYou can plan steps to reach it.
RealisticYou are willing and able to work toward it.
TimelyAnchored to a time frame.

Practice 1: fix the KPI

Each statement is a draft personal or team KPI from a fictional clinic. Choose what is missing, or mark it SMART as written.

Practice 2: balance the scorecard

The Balanced Scorecard sets goals in four areas so managers do not rely on financial results alone. Place each healthcare measure in its area.

Takeaway: Keep your measures few and tied to what drives success. Make each one SMART, and balance customer, learning and growth, internal process, and financial measures so you see where performance is heading, not just where it has been.
