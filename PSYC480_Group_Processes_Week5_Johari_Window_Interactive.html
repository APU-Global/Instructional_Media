Week 5 Interactive: The Johari Window and the Working Stage

Week 5 objective: Use the Johari Window to explain how self-disclosure and feedback help a group reach and use the working stage.

The four areas

Joseph Luft and Harrington Ingham developed the Johari Window in 1955. It crosses what is known or not known to the self with what is known or not known to others:

  • Open: known to self and to others.
  • Blind: known to others but not to self.
  • Hidden: known to self but not to others.
  • Unknown: known to neither.

Self-disclosure moves something from hidden to open; feedback from others moves something from blind to open.
