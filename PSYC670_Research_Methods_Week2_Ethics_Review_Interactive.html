Week 2 Interactive: Ethics Review Desk

Week 2 objective: Apply the APA Ethics Code (Standard 8) to decide what consent a study needs and what an informed consent form must say.

Three questions a reviewer asks

(1) Could the study reasonably create distress or harm? If not, and it fits one of the situations in Standard 8.05 (normal educational practices; only anonymous questionnaires, naturalistic observation, or archival research that put no one at risk; certain organizational studies), consent may be dispensed with. (2) Otherwise, informed consent is required (8.02), including for recording voices or images (8.03). (3) Are participants vulnerable or dependent, such as children, adults with diminished capacity, or the researcher's own clients or students (8.04)? Then they need added protections.

Consent form elements

Takeaway: Ask whether a study could cause distress or harm, whether it fits a Standard 8.05 situation, and who the participants are. Consent must cover purpose, procedures, the right to withdraw, risks, benefits, confidentiality limits, incentives, and contacts. The research question itself may wait for debriefing, as long as procedures, risks, and benefits are described up front.
