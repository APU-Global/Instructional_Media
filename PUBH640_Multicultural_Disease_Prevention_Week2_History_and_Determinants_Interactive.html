Week 2 objective: Connect documented episodes of exclusion in U.S. public health to the concepts and social determinants that explain today’s disparities.

The five SDOH domains (Healthy People 2030)

Social determinants of health are the conditions in the environments where people are born, live, learn, work, play, worship, and age.

Economic Stability
Income, jobs, and the resources to meet basic needs.
Education Access and Quality
Schools, education, and the opportunities they open.
Health Care Access and Quality
Whether people can get the care they need, when they need it.
Neighborhood and Built Environment
Housing, transportation, safety, and the air and water around people.
Social and Community Context
Relationships, community ties, and experiences such as discrimination.
