Week 3 Interactive: Outsourcing Decisions

Week 3 objective: Distinguish outsourcing from offshoring, weigh the benefits and risks of outsourcing, and identify which capabilities to keep in-house.

Key terms

In outsourcing, a company delegates an entire process to a vendor, which runs it as it sees fit; the company pays for the end result. Contract manufacturing is a form of outsourcing in which a partner produces to the hiring firm's design. In offshoring, the firm sets up its own operation in a low-cost country, keeps control, and hires the employees directly.

A strategic resource is valuable, rare, difficult to imitate, and nonsubstitutable. Identify your core competencies before deciding what to hand to a partner.
