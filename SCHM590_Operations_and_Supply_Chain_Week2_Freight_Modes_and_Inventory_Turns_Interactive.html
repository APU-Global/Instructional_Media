Practice 2: Inventory turnover calculator

Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold ÷ average aggregate inventory value (raw materials, work in process, and finished goods, all at cost). Higher turns mean inventory is replenished and sold more times in a period.

Inventory turnover

Your turn. Calder Brook Foodservice (fictional) had cost of goods sold of $9,600,000 and average aggregate inventory of $1,200,000 last year. This year: $10,200,000 and $1,020,000. How many turns did it achieve this year?

Takeaway: Freight cost is driven by decisions: own or outsource, which carriers, which mode and route, and the negotiated rate. Faster modes and more stocking points buy responsiveness at a price, so pair cost measures such as inventory turns with service measures such as on-time delivery.
