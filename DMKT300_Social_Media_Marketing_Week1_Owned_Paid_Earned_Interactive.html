Week 1 Interactive: The Social Media Mix

Week 1 objective: Examine how social media changed the connection between consumers and businesses by sorting a brand’s social media into owned, paid, and earned media, and the work into strategy, implementation, and analytics.

Three kinds of media

Owned: the pages, profiles, and channels a brand runs itself. Paid: any placement the brand pays a third party for, such as promoted or boosted posts, display ads, and branded content. Earned: what other people create and share about the brand. Social media began mostly as earned media and is now a mix of all three. The work runs in a cycle of strategy, implementation (content creation and community engagement), and analytics, with results feeding back into strategy.

Takeaway: Plan owned, paid, and earned media together, and keep the work cycling: strategy guides implementation, analytics show what worked, and the results reshape the strategy. Marketers can lead the conversation, but they cannot control it.
