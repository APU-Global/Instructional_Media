Week 5 Interactive: Reinforcement and Habits Lab

Week 5 objective: Apply behaviorist, social learning, cognitive, self-regulated, and habit-formation approaches to learning new behaviors.

Reading the four quadrants

Reinforcement makes a behavior more likely; punishment makes it less likely. Positive means something is added; negative means something is removed. Ask two questions: Is the behavior going up or down? Was something added or taken away?

Takeaway: To change a behavior, reinforce it (positive reinforcement usually works better than punishment), model it, give learners strategies and goals to track, and repeat it long enough to become a habit. Use these tools in your Week 5 learning plan.
