Week 3 Interactive: Auditing an Organization Against the Five Principles

Week 3 objective: Use the Community Tool Box’s five essential principles as a practical standard to assess a health organization, and separate the four terms that are often confused.

The five essential principles

1. Valuing diversity
Recognizing and respecting differences between and within communities.
2. Conducting cultural self-assessment
Regularly examining the organization’s own policies, norms, communication, staffing, and decisions.
3. Understanding the dynamics of difference
Recognizing how past and present discrimination and experiences with institutions shape trust.
4. Institutionalizing cultural knowledge
Building competence into policies, services, budgets, and decisions, not occasional training.
5. Adapting to diversity
Changing practices when existing approaches create barriers or fail to meet needs.

Takeaway: Knowledge, awareness, and sensitivity support competence but do not equal it. Competence is organizational: valuing diversity, assessing yourself, understanding the dynamics of difference, institutionalizing knowledge, and adapting practice. Use the five principles as your checklist when you assess a campaign in the multi-part assignment.
