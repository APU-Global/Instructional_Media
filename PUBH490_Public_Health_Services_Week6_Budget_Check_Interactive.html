Week 6 Interactive: Estimating and Monitoring a Budget

Week 6 objective: Recognize top-down, bottom-up, and parametric estimates, and use earned value thinking to judge whether a program is really over budget.

Key ideas

Top-down

Start with a total and assign percentages to phases based on experienced people's judgment.

Bottom-up

Estimate each task with the people who will do it, then roll the estimates up. More effort, more likely to be accurate.

Parametric

Use a statistical relationship from historical data, such as cost per unit.

Earned value

Compare what was spent with the planned cost of the work actually done (budget), and the work done with the work scheduled (schedule).

Practice 1: which estimating method?

A fictional health department is budgeting a six-month diabetes outreach program. Choose the estimating technique each step uses.

Takeaway: A spending total alone cannot tell you whether a program is in trouble. Compare actual costs with the work completed, and the work completed with the work scheduled, before you decide to cut, add, or re-plan.
