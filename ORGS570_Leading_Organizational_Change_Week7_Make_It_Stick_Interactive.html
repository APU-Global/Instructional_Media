Week 7 Interactive: Make It Stick

Week 7 objective: Identify the moves that anchor a change in the culture, as Discussion 7 asks whether a turnaround strategy is sustainable and Part 3 asks you to recommend a successful second change process.

Part 1: Which culture-change lever?

A fictional airline, Coastline Air, is moving from a rule-bound culture to one that empowers frontline staff to solve customer problems. Name the lever behind each action.

Part 2: Refreeze the change

After the first year, which refreezing move is each action?

Part 3: Surface change or culture change?

The text warns that change in superficial elements alone is not success. Which is each?

Takeaway: A change lasts when it reaches assumptions, not just procedures. Align urgency, leaders, modeling, training, rewards, and symbols, then refreeze. “Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind…” (Romans 12:2, ESV).
