Week 5 Interactive: Stem-Changing Verbs

Week 5 objective: Conjugate stem-changing verbs and verbs with an irregular yo form in the present tense.

The rule

The last stem vowel changes where it is stressed: in every form except nosotros and vosotros. Endings stay regular.

Three stem-changing patterns
e → ie: pensaro → ue: volvere → i: pedir
pienso, piensas, piensavuelvo, vuelves, vuelvepido, pides, pide
pensamos, pensáis, piensanvolvemos, volvéis, vuelvenpedimos, pedís, piden

Irregular yo forms: hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, oigo, veo; tengo, vengo.
