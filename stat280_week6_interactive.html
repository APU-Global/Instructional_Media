What Does “95% Confident” Mean?

A confidence level describes the reliability of a method, not a 95% chance that one particular interval is correct. Build intervals from random samples and count how often they capture the truth.

Part 1 · Watch the intervals

The true population mean is μ = 50 (the dashed line). Each bar is a confidence interval built from its own random sample of size n, using the t-distribution because σ is unknown. Teal bars capture μ; red bars miss it.

Part 2 · Plan a sample size

You can choose a sample size to achieve a desired margin of error: n = (z·σ / E)². A poll or a quality check of cereal boxes needs enough data, but more precision costs more data.
