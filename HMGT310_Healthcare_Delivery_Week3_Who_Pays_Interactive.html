Week 3 Interactive: Public and Private Payers

Week 3 objective: Compare the public and private payers in the US healthcare system and explain how government acts as purchaser and regulator.

Why this matters

The US system is pluralistic: public payers fund about 48% of health spending, private payers 40%, and patients pay 12% out of pocket. Which program covers a person depends on age, disability, income, military service, tribal membership, and employment. Administrators need to know the payer mix because it shapes revenue, rules, and access. This practice also prepares you for the ACA paper.

Practice 1: match the payer

Each fictional patient is described in one or two sentences. Choose the payer that most likely covers their care.

Practice 2: true or false?

Decide whether each statement about the government's roles is true or false according to this week's sources.

Takeaway: Government is the largest single purchaser of US healthcare, a provider through programs such as Veterans Affairs, and a regulator that sets the rules for private insurance and protects conscience. Each payer brings different rules, so knowing the payer mix is basic to managing any healthcare organization.
