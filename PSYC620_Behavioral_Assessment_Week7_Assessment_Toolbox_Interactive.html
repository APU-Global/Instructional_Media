Week 7 Interactive: The Behavior Assessment Toolbox

Week 7 objective: Classify behavior assessment methods, weigh what each can and cannot tell you, and read functional analysis data to identify what maintains a behavior.

Three families of assessment

Indirect
Interviews, questionnaires, and rating scales completed by people who know the person well, such as the 16-item Functional Analysis Screening Tool (FAST).
Observational
Directly recording the behavior where it happens with ABC recording or time charts, often across 5 or more days.
Functional analysis
Experimentally testing attention, demand, tangible, and alone conditions against a play control condition.
