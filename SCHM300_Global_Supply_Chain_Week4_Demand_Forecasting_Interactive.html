Week 4 Interactive: Forecasting the Demand Your Customers Create

Week 4 objective: Calculate naive, moving-average, weighted moving-average, and exponential-smoothing forecasts, compare their accuracy with the mean absolute deviation (MAD), and recognize common demand patterns. Good CRM promises depend on forecasts like these.

Key terms

  • Naive: next period's forecast = last period's actual demand.
  • 3-period moving average: the average of the last three periods.
  • Weighted moving average: 0.5 × last period + 0.3 × the one before + 0.2 × the one before that.
  • Exponential smoothing: Ft = (1 − α) Ft−1 + α Dt−1, starting with the first forecast equal to the first period's demand.
  • MAD: the average of the absolute errors |actual − forecast|. Lower is better.

Takeaway: Averages smooth out noise but lag behind a trend. Compare methods with an error measure such as MAD, expect some error in every forecast, and update forecasts as conditions change.
