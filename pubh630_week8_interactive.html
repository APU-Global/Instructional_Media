Findings Only Matter If They’re Used

Tailor one set of findings to three audiences, tie a recommendation to evidence, and review an AI-drafted paragraph the way an accountable professional would.

The evaluation findings

Neighborhood Walking Program, 12-month results (invented for practice).
  • 62% of participants walked weekly; the target was 70%.
  • Participation was lowest among residents over 65 and people who work evenings.
  • The program cost $48,000 and served 120 residents.
  • Participants reported higher confidence on a follow-up survey. Data were self-reported from a small sample.

Part 1 · Know your audience

The same finding may need to be shared differently with funders, community partners, and policymakers. Choose one option in each group for each audience, then check.

Part 2 · Turn conclusions into recommendations

Findings should lead to decisions. Choose what the program should do, then cite the evidence that supports it.

Part 3 · Review the AI draft

AI tools can help draft messages, but accountability remains with the professional. Tap every phrase that you would revise before sharing, then check your review.
