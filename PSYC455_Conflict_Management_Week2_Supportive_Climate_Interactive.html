Week 2 objective: Recognize Gibb's defensive behaviors and choose supportive alternatives, as you will when you advise the friend in Discussion 2.

Gibb's six pairs (supportive versus defensive)

  • Description versus evaluation: neutral facts and I-messages, not blame.
  • Collaboration versus control: working toward a win-win, not "my way or nothing."
  • Straightforwardness versus manipulation: direct and honest, no hidden intentions.
  • Empathy versus indifference: relating to the other person, not "everyone's got problems."
  • Equality versus superiority: fairness, not an upper hand.
  • Flexibility versus certainty: open to something better, not "I'm right, you're wrong."

Takeaway: Defensiveness tends to spread, and so does support. Describe instead of judging, invite collaboration, and stay open: the other person is more likely to answer in kind.
