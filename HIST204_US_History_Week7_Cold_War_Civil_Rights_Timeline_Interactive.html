Week 7 Interactive: Cold War, Civil Rights, and Vietnam Timeline

Week 7 objective: Place major events of the Cold War, the civil rights movement, and the Vietnam War in order, and see how the three stories ran side by side (course outcome 1: timing, sequence, and significance).

How to play

Use the ▲ and ▼ buttons to move each event until the list runs from earliest (top) to latest (bottom). Then select Check my order. Each event is tagged Cold WarCivil RightsVietnam.

      Takeaway: These stories happened at the same time. While the United States built a policy of containment abroad, African Americans pressed the nation to live up to its ideals at home, and the same president who signed the Voting Rights Act escalated the war in Vietnam.
