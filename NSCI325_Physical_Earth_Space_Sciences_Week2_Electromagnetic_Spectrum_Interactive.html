Week 2 Interactive: The Electromagnetic Spectrum

Week 2 objective: Describe the bands of the electromagnetic spectrum, how wavelength, frequency, and energy relate, and where each band appears in everyday life.

The key relationship

Every form of electromagnetic radiation travels at the speed of light, c ≈ 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s, so c = λν: a longer wavelength (λ) means a lower frequency (ν). Energy rises with frequency, so the shortest waves carry the most energy.
