Takeaway: A meeting of the minds is judged by what the parties said and did, not by secret intentions. Assent obtained by force or fraud in the execution is no assent at all; assent induced by an improper threat or a misrepresentation can be undone. Honest dealing is not only ethical, it is what keeps a negotiated contract standing.
