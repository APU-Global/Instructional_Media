Week 4 Interactive: CRM Implementation

Week 4 objective: Sequence the steps of a CRM implementation, match business problems to CRM tool types, and identify common implementation errors.

Strategy before software

Stokes gives five steps: (1) conduct a business needs analysis and write a problem statement; (2) understand customer needs; (3) set objectives and measures of success (KPIs); (4) develop the strategy and how you will implement it (channels, touchpoints, data); (5) choose the right tools. Tools are useless without a clear strategy. Tool types: collaborative (customer data combined across departments), social (social listening and sentiment), operational (front end and customer service), sales and marketing automation (leads, sales cycles, behaviour-driven emails), and analytical (studying behaviour to segment customers and predict purchases).

Practice 1: which step is it?

Rivera Home Services, a fictional HVAC company, is adopting CRM. Name the step each activity belongs to.

Practice 2: which tool type fits?

Match each need at Rivera Home Services to the CRM tool type that addresses it.

Practice 3: name the error

Each fictional company stumbled. Choose the error the readings warn about.

Takeaway: Count the cost before you build: analyze needs, listen to customers, set KPIs, plan the strategy, and only then choose the tool type that fits. Most failures trace back to missing buy-in, siloed or stale data, or rewards nobody costed.
