Week 1 Interactive: Use, Abuse, or Addiction?

Week 1 objective: Differentiate drug and alcohol use, abuse, and addiction using the Three C's, DSM-5 severity levels, and the brain disease model.

Why this matters

Use, abuse, and addiction sit on a continuum, and not all drug use leads to addiction. The Three C's (compulsion, loss of control, and consequences) help mark where use becomes addictive, and DSM-5 now rates one disorder, substance use disorder, by severity.

Practice 1: name the C

Each fictional scenario shows one of the Three C's most clearly. Choose the C it shows.

Practice 2: rate the severity

DSM-5 counts how many of the 11 symptoms a person shows. Choose the severity level for each fictional case.

Practice 3: true or false?

Decide whether each statement matches this week's sources.

Takeaway: Ask three questions: Is there compulsion? Is there loss of control? Are there consequences? Then count symptoms for severity. Addiction is a chronic brain disease, not a moral failing, and relapse is common, which calls for patience and long-term care.
