HSCI 110 · Week 3 Interactive

Guide a Patient Along the Continuum of Care

A patient's journey crosses many settings, and each transition can keep or lose information. Make the coordination choices and watch what the next team knows.

Part 1 · Manage the transitions

Scenario (fictional): Mr. Alvarez, 78, breaks his hip in a fall. He moves from the emergency department to the hospital, to rehabilitation, home, and back to his primary care clinic. At each handoff, choose the action that best supports coordination. The meter shows how much of his story each next team still has. This is a care-coordination exercise, not medical advice.

Start with the first handoff.

Part 2 · Settings of care

Care is delivered in inpatient, outpatient, post-acute, long-term, palliative, and hospice settings. Each serves different needs.

Part 3 · Roles, coordination, and navigation

Takeaways
