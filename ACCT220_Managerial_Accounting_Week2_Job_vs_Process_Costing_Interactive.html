Week 2 Interactive: Job Costing or Process Costing?

Week 2 objective: Distinguish between job costing and process costing, and explain which system fits a product or service.

Sort each product or service

Read each example and choose the costing system that fits. Then select Check my answers. Each answer explains why.

Takeaway: Job costing tracks costs for each unique product or job on a job cost sheet. Process costing tracks costs by department for identical units made in batches. Both use direct materials, direct labor, and overhead flowing through the same three inventory accounts.
