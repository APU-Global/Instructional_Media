Part 2: Find the root cause with the 4Ms

A fishbone diagram groups possible causes by category. Choose the 4M category for each root cause found at Bayview.

Part 3: Calculate process cycle efficiency

Process cycle efficiency (PCE) = value-added time ÷ total lead time, where total lead time = value-added time + non-value-added time. In the case warehouse, put-away of mixed pallets averaged 7,200 seconds of value-added time and 10,800 seconds of non-value-added time.

Try your own numbers. Enter value-added and non-value-added seconds for any step.



Part 4: Slot to cut travel

Castier and Martínez-Toro (2023) tested storage layouts in a 191-product warehouse. Choose the best answer.

Takeaway: Lean starts with value and makes waste visible. Walk the floor for waiting, motion, transportation, overproduction, defects, and excess inventory; trace each problem to man, machine, method, or material; and measure progress with process cycle efficiency. Slotting fast movers near the pick start point is a simple way to cut travel.
