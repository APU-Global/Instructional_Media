Week 4 Interactive: Bias and Pain Assessment

Week 4 objective: Recognize how bias and culture affect pain assessment and choose culturally responsive approaches.

Pain is individual

Chapter 4 reminds us that pain assessment is not one-size-fits-all. Social conditions (finances, living alone, depression, rural or low-income areas), provider myths and unconscious bias, and cultural factors (stoicism, beliefs about the meaning of pain, fear of side effects or addiction, metaphors lost in translation, mistrust from past experiences) all shape how pain is expressed, heard, and treated.

Takeaway: Believe the report, question the myths, and ask before you assume. Fair pain care combines the patient's own words, probing questions, objective signs, family insight, and respect for the patient's beliefs and choices.
