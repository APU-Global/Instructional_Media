Week 2 Interactive: Gustar and the Preterite

Week 2 objective: Use gustar with indirect object pronouns, and choose the right preterite form to tell about a trip.

Quick reference

Gustar agrees with the thing liked: gusta for one thing or an infinitive, gustan for plural things. The pronoun names the person: me, te, le, nos, os, les (A mí me gusta la playa. A mis padres les gustan las montañas.)

Takeaway: Gustar agrees with what is liked (gusta / gustan), and the pronoun names who likes it. In the preterite, regular yo and él/ella forms carry an accent (viajé, viajó), but di, dio, hice, hizo, fui, fue do not.
