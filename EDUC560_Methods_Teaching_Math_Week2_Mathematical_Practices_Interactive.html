Week 2 Interactive: Standards for Mathematical Practice

Week 2 objective: Recognize the eight Standards for Mathematical Practice in students' problem solving so you can design problem-based lessons that call for them.

Eight habits of a mathematical thinker

The California Common Core State Standards for Mathematics include eight Standards for Mathematical Practice, the same at every grade. They are "not a checklist; they are the basis of mathematics instruction and learning," and several should be evident in each lesson. Proficient problem solvers start by explaining the meaning of a problem to themselves, look for entry points, and keep asking, "Does this make sense?"

Practice: which practice is this?

Each classroom is fictional. Choose the practice the students show most clearly, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Design problem-based lessons that require the practices: a problem with more than one entry point (SMP 1), a context to model (SMP 4), a choice of tools (SMP 5), and time for students to explain and critique (SMP 3). Then listen for the practices as students work, and name them aloud.

Practice descriptions and examples from the California Department of Education, California Common Core State Standards: Mathematics (2013), "Standards for Mathematical Practice" (cde.ca.gov). Classroom scenarios are fictional.
