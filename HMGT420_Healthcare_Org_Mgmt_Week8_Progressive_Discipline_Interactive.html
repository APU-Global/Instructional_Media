Week 8 Interactive: Misconduct and Progressive Discipline

Week 8 objective: Classify employee performance issues, put the progressive discipline steps in order, and check a disciplinary decision against the tests of just cause.

Four kinds of performance issue

Mandated issueSerious and often legal (safety, privacy, harassment). Handle swiftly by written policy.
Single incidentA one-time misstep. Coach in a private conversation.
Behavior patternRepeated, not yet corrected. Restate expectations.
Persistent patternContinues after correction. Make the seriousness clear.

    Before you act: the seven tests of just cause

    Notice of the rule · a reasonable rule · a timely investigation · a fair investigation · proof · equal treatment · a fair penalty that considers length of service and prior record. Document every step, meet in private, and let the employee explain.

    Takeaway: Treat safety and privacy violations as mandated issues and act swiftly. For everything else, investigate first, coach early, and escalate through progressive discipline with documentation at every step. Consistent, fair treatment protects patients, staff, and the organization.
