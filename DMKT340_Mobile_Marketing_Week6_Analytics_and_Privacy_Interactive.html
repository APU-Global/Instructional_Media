Week 6 Interactive: Mobile Analytics and Privacy

Week 6 objective: Match campaign questions to useful mobile metrics, and judge which data practices keep people's trust.

Useful metrics and the three parts of trust

Useful mobile metrics include device type, operating system, and screen size; how visitors arrived; engagement such as page views, time on site, and bounce rate; and for apps, active users, retention rate, session length, exit screen, and crash analytics. Downloads alone mislead. Trust needs security (protect data from being hacked or stolen), privacy (follow the law on what is collected and how it is used, with an accessible privacy policy), and transparency (show people how their data is used).

Practice 2: does this practice keep trust?

Leaders suggest ways to use data during the campaign. Judge each one.

Takeaway: Measure real engagement, not vanity numbers, and answer most questions with aggregate counts. Collect personal data only with consent, keep it secure, and be open about how it is used.
