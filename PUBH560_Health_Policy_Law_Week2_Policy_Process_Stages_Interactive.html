Week 2 Interactive: Stages of the Policy Process

Week 2 objective: Identify the stages of the policy-making process and what happens in each.

The five stages

Embrett and Randall (2014) describe the policy process as phases: problem definition (an issue is framed as a public problem policy can address), agenda setting (it is placed on the agenda for possible government action), formulation and decision making (options are designed, debated, and chosen), implementation (the decision is put into operation), and evaluation (results are measured). Real policymaking is not always linear, but the stages help you see where a policy stands.
