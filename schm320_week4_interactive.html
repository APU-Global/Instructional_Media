Step 3 · Weigh the criteria

Modes are compared on cost, transit time, reliability, capability, and accessibility. Faster modes usually cost more. Change the importance of each criterion, and compare modes on the radar chart (select a mode to show or hide it).

    Think total cost. A cheap freight rate can still cost more once inventory, speed, and service effects are included.
    Intermodal combines two or more modes in one journey, with containers transferring between them. It can balance cost and service. Compare it to the single modes above.

    Scores (1 to 5, higher is better) are illustrative generalizations for practice. Real performance varies with distance, topography, capacity, and loading procedures.
