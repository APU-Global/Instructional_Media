What inclusive leaders do

Seek out and value different viewpoints. Invite quieter voices, acknowledge contributions, and adapt communication to the team.
Name bias and barriers. Everyone holds biases, and structures and norms can create barriers even without intent. Awareness is the first step.
Handle conflict across differences early and respectfully. Look for shared goals and common ground.
Diversity is a strength. Diverse teams offer more perspectives, which fuels innovation and creativity, if leaders do the work of inclusion.
