Week 6 Interactive: Strategic Planning for Marketing Campaigns

Week 6 objective: Organize the parts of a strategic marketing plan and separate internal from external factors in a SWOT analysis.

The plan in brief

A strategic marketing plan starts with a value proposition and an honest situation analysis (SWOT: strengths and weaknesses are internal; opportunities and threats are external). The written plan then moves through the executive summary, business challenge, market (customers, company analysis, collaborators, competitors, business climate), strategy (offering, communication plan, distribution), and budget.

Practice 1: Where does it go in the plan?

Harbor Family Health is fictional. It is planning to launch a mobile screening van. Choose the section of the marketing plan where each piece belongs.

Practice 2: SWOT for Harbor Family Health

Sort each factor. Tip: take Harbor out of the picture. If the factor still exists, it is external (an opportunity or a threat).

Takeaway: A persuasive plan answers "Why should I invest?" in order: summary, challenge, market, strategy, budget. Keep the SWOT honest and keep its quadrants straight: an opportunity lies in the market, not in a strength.
