Week 3 Interactive: Percents and Their Base

Week 3 objective: Convert among fractions, decimals, and percents, and solve percent and percent-change problems by identifying the base.

The key relationships

Percent means "per hundred": 40% = 40/100 = 0.40. If a part is some percent of a whole, then percent = part ÷ whole, or percent × whole = part (write the percent as a decimal). For a change, absolute change = |ending − starting| and relative change = absolute change ÷ starting quantity. The starting quantity is the base of the percent change.

Takeaway: Before you compute, ask "percent of what?" Discounts and gains that follow one another use a new base each time, a change between two percents is measured in percentage points, and percents with different bases cannot simply be averaged.
