Week 6 Interactive: Treatment Toolkit

Week 6 objective: Differentiate assessment, therapy, and medication approaches to treating substance use disorders.

Why this matters

Effective treatment has several parts: screening and assessment to plan care, counseling and behavioral therapies, medication-assisted treatment where it fits, and integrated care for co-occurring mental illness. Long-term or repeated care is often needed.

Practice 1: choose the tool

Each fictional moment comes from Dana's path through treatment. Choose the tool being used.

Practice 2: true or false?

Decide whether each statement matches this week's sources.

Takeaway: Screen, then assess, then match care to the person: therapy to change thinking and habits, medication where it helps, and integrated care for co-occurring conditions. Recovery often takes long-term or repeated care, and faith-based support can complement it.
