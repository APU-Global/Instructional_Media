A literature review starts with a focused problem statement and a specific, measurable research question. Narrow a broad interest step by step, sort statements from questions, and spot the gaps that point to new research.

Part 2 · Statement or question?

The problem statement describes an issue that needs further research. The research question follows from it and is specific and measurable. Quantitative questions ask “how much?” or about the impact of one variable on another; qualitative questions ask “how did that occur?” or about lived experience. Classify each sentence.

Part 3 · Find the gap
