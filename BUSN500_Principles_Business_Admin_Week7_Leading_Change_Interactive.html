Week 7 Interactive: Leading Change and Working With Resistance

Week 7 objective: Identify why people resist a change and sort change-management actions into Lewin's unfreezing, changing, and refreezing stages, as Discussion 7 asks.

Terms from the readings

Why people resistDisrupted habits, personality, uncertainty, fear of failure, personal impact, too-frequent change, and perceived loss of power.
UnfreezingPrepare people: communicate the plan and vision, create urgency, build a coalition, provide support, and invite participation.
ChangingExecute the plan: keep providing support, create small wins, and eliminate obstacles.
RefreezingMake it stick: publicize success and reward people who adopt the change.

Part 1: Why is this person resisting?

Harborline Airways, a fictional regional airline, is moving to a new self-service check-in system and a team-based structure. Choose the main reason for each reaction.

Part 2: Unfreeze, change, or refreeze?

Choose the stage of Lewin's model each action belongs to.

Takeaway: Resistance has reasons, and those reasons point to the response. Prepare people before the change, support them during it, and reinforce the new way afterward so it becomes routine.
