Week 6 Interactive: Global Sourcing, Offshoring, and Reshoring

Week 6 objective: Calculate a make-or-buy break-even between domestic and offshore production, and distinguish global sourcing strategies, outsourcing, and offshoring.

Key terms

Global sourcing is importing: buying goods and services from foreign sources. Offshoring sets up operations in a low-cost country with directly hired workers, so the firm keeps control. Outsourcing hands an entire process to a vendor, which runs it as it sees fit.

Break-even volume = difference in fixed costs ÷ difference in unit costs. Below it, the option with lower fixed costs wins; above it, the option with lower unit costs wins.
