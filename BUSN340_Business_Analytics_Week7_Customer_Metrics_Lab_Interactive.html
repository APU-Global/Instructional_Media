Week 7 Interactive: Customer Metrics Lab

Week 7 objective: Evaluate customer behavior with conversion rate, acquisition cost, and customer lifetime value, and apply ethical considerations to data collection (CLOs 3 and 5).

Three metrics

Conversion rate
Conversions divided by visitors, as a percentage. Funnel analysis checks each step.
Cost per acquisition
Campaign spending divided by the new customers it produced.
Customer lifetime value
The profitability of a customer over the entire relationship.

Practice 3: respect or overreach?

Customer behavior data raises ethical questions. Decide whether each practice respects the customer or crosses a line.

Takeaway: Conversion rate shows where customers drop away, cost per acquisition shows what growth costs, and lifetime value shows what a customer is worth over time. The data behind these metrics belongs to real people, so collect it with consent, transparency, and care. Use these metrics in Discussion 7 and in your Board Report.
