Week 3 Interactive: Stages of Group Development

Week 3 objective: Identify a group's stage of development from its behavior and choose the leader move that helps it advance.

Five stages

Forming: polite, guarded, conflict avoidant; members ask "Will I be accepted? What is my role?" Storming: facades drop; members argue over tasks and roles, form cliques, and question the leader. Norming: "We survived!"; members set ground rules and ask each other for help and feedback. Performing: interdependent and focused on how they work, not only what they produce. Adjourning: the group ends; members feel victory, grief, and insecurity. Groups can also slip back to an earlier stage after a disruption.

Practice 1: Name the stage

Each group is fictional. Choose the stage that best fits, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: What should the leader do?

Choose the leader move the reading recommends for each stage.

Takeaway: Read the signals (politeness, conflict, ground rules, self-review, endings) to place a group in a stage, and change your leadership style as the group matures: directive, then coach, then delegator. Storming is normal; handled well, it releases creative energy.
