Week 6 Interactive: Professional Communication

Week 6 objective: Recognize the four communication styles, adapt your message to each, and use active listening to prevent and repair communication breakdowns.

Four styles, one team

Human Relations describes four styles. Expressers get excited, rely on hunches, and dislike long explanations. Drivers are decisive, want results, and like to be in charge. Relaters value warm relationships and want to know you care. Analyticals ask many questions, want data, and dislike being rushed. No one uses one style all the time, but naming a colleague's dominant style helps you frame a message they will hear.

Practice 1: Which style is speaking?

A fictional community health team is planning a vaccination outreach event. Choose each member's dominant style.

Practice 2: Choose the best reply

Each situation is fictional. Choose the reply that best follows active listening and the communicator's responsibilities.

Takeaway: Prepare a clear, concise message, listen actively and restate what you heard, adapt to each person's style and culture, and speak without blaming when things go wrong.
