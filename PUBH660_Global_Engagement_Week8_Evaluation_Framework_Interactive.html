Week 8 Interactive: Plan an Evaluation

Week 8 objective: Apply CDC’s Program Evaluation Framework (six steps and five standards) to a global health program, as you will in Part 4: Evaluation and Dissemination Plan.

CDC Program Evaluation Framework

CDC calls the framework “a practical, nonprescriptive tool.” Three cross-cutting actions run through every step: engage collaboratively, promote fair and just evaluation practices and outcomes, and learn from and use insights. CDC calls the people with a stake in an evaluation “interest holders.” The steps are often shown in order, but evaluation is iterative.

Takeaway: A good evaluation plan names its questions (Step 3), its indicators and data (Step 4), and how findings will reach each interest holder (Step 6), and it meets the five standards throughout. That is the core of your Part 4 plan.
