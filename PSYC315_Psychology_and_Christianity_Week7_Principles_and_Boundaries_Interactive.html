Week 7 Interactive: Ethical Decision-Making

Week 7 objective: Identify the ethical principles at stake in a situation and recognize professional boundary warning signs.

Six principles and professional boundaries

Open RN lists foundational ethical principles: autonomy (self-determination), beneficence (do good), nonmaleficence (do no harm), justice (fairness), fidelity (keep promises), and veracity (tell the truth). Professionals also keep boundaries: they limit self-disclosure, avoid personal or business relationships with clients, and openly acknowledge a dual relationship when roles overlap. These scenarios are different from the Discussion 7 case, so you can practice before you work through it.

Takeaway: Name the principles in tension, consult the codes, and guard the boundaries that protect clients.
