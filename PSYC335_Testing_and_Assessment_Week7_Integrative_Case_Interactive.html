Week 7 Interactive: Integrative Case Analysis

Week 7 objective: Classify scores with each instrument's rules, separate supported conclusions from unsupported ones, and choose scope-appropriate wording for a referral summary.

How to read across instruments

Line the results up, look for where they converge (point the same way) and diverge, and name what the data cannot tell you. Screeners are self-report snapshots; symptoms overlap across conditions; diagnosis requires a qualified clinician using interviews and observation. Functional measures like the WHODAS 2.0 describe how difficulties affect daily life, which is often the clearest way to communicate.
