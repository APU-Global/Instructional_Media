Week 5 Interactive: Your Digital Reputation

Week 5 objective: Evaluate whether a post supports or harms a health science professional's reputation, using the rules of netiquette and reputation management.

Three questions before you post

1. Is it permanent-proof? Anything posted online can stay there indefinitely (Human Relations). 2. Would I say it in person? Netiquette asks you to remember the human on the other side, hold to the same standards online as in real life, and respect other people's privacy (Business Communication for Success). 3. Would I want an employer to see it? Your online profile works like a public résumé, and an employer who finds your posts should see you representing the organization in a positive light.

Practice: share, revise, or don't post?

Each post is fictional. Decide what a careful health science professional should do.

Quick audit of your own presence

Try these reputation-management steps from Human Relations. Check each one off as you finish. Nothing is recorded.

Takeaway: Treat every post as permanent and public. Apply the same standards online as in person, protect other people's privacy, and share what builds others up.
