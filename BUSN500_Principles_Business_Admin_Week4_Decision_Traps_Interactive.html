Week 4 Interactive: Deciding Under Uncertainty

Week 4 objective: Recognize common decision-making traps and match decision approaches to the situation, as Discussion 4 asks you to evaluate.

Terms from the readings

Overconfidence biasOverestimating your ability to predict future events.
AnchoringRelying too heavily on a single piece of information.
Framing biasBeing swayed by how a problem or choice is presented.
Escalation of commitmentContinuing a failing course of action because of what has already been invested.
GroupthinkGroup pressure that reduces reality testing and moral judgment; signs include self-censorship and illusions of unanimity.
Analysis paralysisSpending so long gathering and studying information that no decision gets made.
