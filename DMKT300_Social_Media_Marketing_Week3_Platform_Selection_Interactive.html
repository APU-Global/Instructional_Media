Week 3 Interactive: Choosing Platforms

Week 3 objective: Evaluate social media platforms for customer engagement by answering the three platform-selection questions and matching business needs to each network’s purpose.

Three questions

Who is your audience? Research them, including their digital habits. How can you reach them? Compare the demographics and psychographics of each platform’s users and go where your audience spends the most time. What are your goals? Awareness, relationships and expertise, or sales and traffic each favor different platforms. Large platforms offer reach; niche platforms such as LinkedIn may bring more engagement with a refined audience.

Practice 1: which question does it answer?

A fictional garden center, Fernwood Gardens, is planning its social media. Choose which selection question each planning note answers: Audience (who is the audience?), Reach (how can we reach them?), or Goals (what are our goals?).

Practice 2: match the need to the network

Platforms were built with distinct purposes. Choose the best fit for each fictional organization’s need.

Takeaway: There is no single best platform. Choose the network that fits your audience, your reach, and your goals, secure your brand name everywhere, and keep testing: platforms rise and fall.
