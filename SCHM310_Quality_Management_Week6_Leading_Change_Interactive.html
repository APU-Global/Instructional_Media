Week 6 Interactive: Implementation

Week 6 objective: Plan the people side of a QMS implementation using Lewin's three-stage model of change.

Lewin's three stages of planned change

1 · UNFREEZE
Make sure people are ready for and receptive to change: communicate the plan and vision, build urgency, win over opinion leaders, provide support, and invite participation.
2 · CHANGE
Execute the planned change: keep supporting people, create small wins, and remove obstacles.
3 · REFREEZE
Make the change permanent: publicize success and reward the people who adopt the new ways.

Practice 1: Which stage is the leader in?

Mesa Valley Foods (fictional) is rolling out a new quality management system at its packaging plant. Choose the stage each action belongs to.

Practice 2: Why are they resisting?

Some employees push back. Choose the reason for resistance each comment shows best.

Takeaway: Most failed quality strategies fail with people, not tools. Unfreeze by explaining why and inviting participation, change with support and small wins, and refreeze by publicizing results and rewarding the new behaviors. Listen for the reason behind resistance so you can answer it.
