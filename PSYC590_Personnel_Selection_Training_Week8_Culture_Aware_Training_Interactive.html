Week 8 Interactive: Culture-Aware Training Design

Week 8 focus: Discussion 8 asks what global HR managers must consider when creating a training program, using Hofstede’s research. Practice reading the five dimensions, then plan training for an overseas assignment.

Part 1: Name the dimension

Match each description to one of Hofstede’s five value dimensions.

Part 2: Plan a session for a fictional training group

Set where a fictional participant group leans on three dimensions. The planner suggests questions to ask as you design. Dimensions describe tendencies in a culture, not every individual, so check with local colleagues before you decide.

Part 3: Which component of overseas-assignment training?

Your HRM text names five components of training for an overseas assignment. Match each fictional need to one of them.

Takeaway: Use Hofstede’s dimensions as questions, not labels: How will this group relate to the trainer? How much structure will they expect? Will they prefer individual or group work? Then train the whole assignment: language, culture, goals, family and stress, and the return home, with input from host-country colleagues.
