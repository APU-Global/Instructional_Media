Why memes spread, and how they go wrong

Viral content spreads by word of mouth, and people trust “a person like me.” The strongest reason to share is social currency: people like to look resourceful, caring, or humorous to their friends. Humor reduces counterarguing, but people must get the joke and connect it to the brand, and jokes built on stereotypes can offend. Stokes adds: research trends and hashtags before joining them, keep a consistent visual identity, label marketing messages, and use only images you own or have permission to use.
