Week 3 Interactive: Spot the Required Emphases

Week 3 objective: Identify print concepts, letters of the alphabet, phonological awareness, and phonemic awareness in a recorded lesson, the same skill you use to build the Required Emphases chart for Intern Teaching Observation Part 1.

Activity 1: Sort by level of sound

Phonological awareness runs along a continuum, from whole words and rhyme to individual phonemes. Choose the level each oral task works on.

Activity 2: Build the chart from a fictional lesson

An intern’s kindergarten lesson, “Sam’s Map,” was recorded in full. For each timestamped moment, choose the foundational skill it shows, then check the chart. Part 1 asks for at least 2–3 instances of each emphasis and at least 4–6 chart entries.

Takeaway: Plan every emphasis on purpose, at least two or three times each, and teach it explicitly: model, guide practice with feedback, then check independent use. Those moments become the timestamps in your chart, each with the skill named, why it matters, and the TPE met.

Definitions from the California Department of Education, California Dyslexia Guidelines (2017: phonological awareness continuum, phonemic awareness, direct and explicit instruction) and California Common Core State Standards for ELA/Literacy (RF.K.1 Print Concepts, RF.K.2 Phonological Awareness); required emphases and chart from the EDUC 592 assignment “Intern Teaching Observation | Part 1: TPE 7.5.” The lesson is fictional.
