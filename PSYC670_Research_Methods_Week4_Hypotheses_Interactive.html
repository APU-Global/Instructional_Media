Week 4 Interactive: Hypothesis Workshop

Week 4 objective: Match a research question to a method, state null and alternative hypotheses, and make a decision from a p value.

Part 1: Which approach fits the question?

Each question is fictional unless noted. Choose the approach that best answers it, then select Check my answers.

Part 2: Write the hypotheses

Research question: Does a six-week mindfulness program reduce test anxiety more than a study-skills program? Choose the best statement for each.

Part 3: Reject or retain?

Alpha is set at .05, as it almost always is. Decide for each fictional result.

Takeaway: Let the question choose the method: experiences call for qualitative designs, causal questions for experiments, and relationships among measured variables for correlational designs. A quantitative proposal states its research question with a null and an alternative hypothesis. A p value below .05 leads you to reject H0; a p value above it leads you to retain H0, never to prove it.

Concepts from Price, Jhangiani, & Chiang, Research Methods in Psychology (2nd Canadian ed., Lumen Learning): Generating Good Research Questions; Experiment Basics; Correlational Research; Qualitative Research; Understanding Null Hypothesis Testing.
