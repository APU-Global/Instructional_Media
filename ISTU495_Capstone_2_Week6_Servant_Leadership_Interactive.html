What servant leaders do

Servant leadership “defines the leader’s role as serving the needs of others.” Its primary mission is to develop people and help them reach their goals. Specific habits include asking what you can do for your people, learning their goals, being humble, being open, and serving the wider community. The Week 6 Overview quotes Max De Pree: “In between, the leader is a servant.”

Takeaway: Servant leadership is not passivity and not self-promotion. It shows up in specific, observable actions that develop people, build trust, and serve the community, following the One who “came not to be served but to serve” (Mark 10:45, ESV).
