Week 7 Interactive: Child Custody Evaluations

Week 7 objective: Identify sound practices and role problems in a child custody evaluation, and translate a court's referral question into the psychological issue to assess.

Practice 1: Sound practice or a problem?

Each step in a fictional custody evaluation is either sound practice or a role or method problem. Choose, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: What is the judge asking?

Before evaluating, translate the court's legal question into a psychological one. Match each fictional referral question to its theme.

Takeaway: Custody evaluations serve the best interests of the child. Stay neutral, never become the family's therapist, explain the limits of confidentiality, ask about daily caretaking and co-parenting attitudes, assess how a parent's illness affects the child, and turn the judge's question into specific psychological issues before you start.

Sources: Chung, D. S., Moon, D. S., Lee, M. H., & Kwack, Y. S. (2020), Journal of the Korean Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, 31(2), 58–65; Zumbach, J., & Volbert, R. (2021), Frontiers in Psychology, 12, 603597. Practice situations are fictional.
