Week 4 Interactive: Interview Question Clinic

Week 4 focus: Writing open-ended interview questions, probing without pressure, and coding what people say, before your Discussion 4 interview with the Public Health Research Interview Chatbot.

Key ideas

Select each card to open it.

Activity 1: Diagnose the question

A fictional researcher is interviewing residents about access to nutritious food in a food desert. Decide what kind of question each one is.

Activity 2: Choose the best follow-up

The participant has just answered. Pick the probe a skilled qualitative interviewer would use.

0 / 3

Activity 3: Focused coding

After open coding, the researcher merged her notes into three themes. Assign each fictional excerpt to the theme it fits best.

Takeaway: Use an interview guide, not a script. Ask open-ended, non-leading questions, swap “why?” for “Could you tell me a little more about that?”, and read your transcript more than once: open coding first, then focused coding into named themes.
