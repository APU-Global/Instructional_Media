Practice 2: What does the research say?

Decide whether each statement is supported by this week's research on expert credibility and bias.

Takeaway: Credibility rests on knowledge, trustworthiness, likeability, and confidence, but most of all on honest, well-supported findings. Prepare by reviewing the whole file, slow down on the stand, never read unfamiliar documents cold, and remember that who hires you and your own views can pull your conclusions.
