Week 7 objective: Distinguish content from contextual adaptations of evidence-based interventions and match adaptations from published studies to a family's needs.

Two kinds of adaptation

A systematic review of 42 studies (Lange et al., 2022) found that evidence-based interventions (EBIs) for children who have experienced trauma, most often Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT) and Cognitive Behavioral Intervention for Trauma in Schools (CBITS), were adapted mainly to improve fit with a new population and to address cultural factors.

  • Content adaptations change what is delivered: adding elements, or tailoring language, examples, analogies, songs, and stories.
  • Contextual adaptations change how, to whom, or by whom it is delivered: the population, the format (such as group sessions), or the personnel (such as trained lay counselors).

The authors add that most EBIs work with diverse populations, so an existing EBI with minor tailoring that keeps its core components is usually the first choice.
