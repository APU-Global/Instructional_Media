Practice 1: Name the model

Each city is fictional. Choose the oversight model each description shows.

Practice 2: Internal or external iron?

The course's Iron Sharpens Iron resource describes accountability coming from inside and outside an agency. Sort each mechanism.

Takeaway: Oversight models divide investigation, review, and discipline differently, so a charter must also decide who receives complaints, who disciplines, what rights officers have, and what is made public. Strong agencies combine internal checks, such as a duty to intervene and use-of-force reviews, with external ones, such as civilian boards and courts.
