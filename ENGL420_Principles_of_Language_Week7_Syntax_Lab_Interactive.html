Week 7 Interactive: Grammar and Syntax

Week 7 objective: Distinguish mental grammar from prescriptive rules and use grammaticality judgments and replacement tests to analyze sentence structure.

Key terms

* ungrammatical: does not fit the speaker's mental grammar# grammatical, but the meaning is oddConstituent: a group of words that acts as a unitSVO: Subject-Verb-Object, the basic order of English

Practice 1: Make a grammaticality judgment

Judge the sentence by the shape of English, not by school rules. Made-up words are allowed.

Practice 2: Find the noun phrase with a replacement test

Which group of words can a pronoun (it, they, she) replace while keeping the meaning? That group is a constituent: a noun phrase.

Practice 3: Which kind of grammar?

Decide whether each statement is about the mental grammar every speaker has or about a prescriptive rule for a standardized variety.

Takeaway: In linguistics, ungrammatical does not mean "bad grammar": it means the sentence does not fit a speaker's mental grammar. Grammaticality judgments and replacement tests turn grammar into something students can investigate, not something to fear.
