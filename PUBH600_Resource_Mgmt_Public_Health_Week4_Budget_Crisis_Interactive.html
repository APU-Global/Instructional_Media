Week 4 Interactive: Resource Allocation and Budget Deficits

Week 4 objective: Analyze a budget shortfall and choose deficit responses that protect mission, people, and essential services.

The scenario

Steady Steps is a fictional county fall-prevention program for older adults. Its annual budget is $240,000 and it serves 800 people a year. Next year a funder cuts support by 15%, local hardship raises demand by 10%, and two coach positions are vacant.

Practice 1: run the numbers

Work out the size of the crisis. Choose the correct figure.

Practice 2: balanced, surplus, or deficit?

Classify each draft budget using the Community Tool Box definitions.

Practice 3: a responsible response?

The Tool Box says the goal is not simply to cut until the numbers balance. Decide whether each proposal is a reasonable response or a red flag.

Takeaway: A 15% cut plus a 10% rise in demand shrank funding per person by about 23%. Before cutting, ask whether the deficit is temporary or structural, protect mission, safety, and people, and look for reserves, partners, and new revenue alongside careful reductions.

Budget elements, definitions, and deficit responses from Rabinowitz, Section 1. Planning and Writing an Annual Budget (Community Tool Box, Chapter 43, University of Kansas). Steady Steps and its figures are fictional.
