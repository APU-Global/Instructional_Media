Week 5 focus: identify the stakeholders your Capstone needs and plan how to communicate with each (CLO 5).

Stakeholders and decision levels

Stakeholders care about or have a vested interest in a project; they have something to gain or lose. Health care decisions happen at three levels:

Micro
Factors at the individual level, such as patients, residents, and their families.
Meso
Factors at an institutional level, such as a facility's managers and administrative staff.
Macro
Structural, legal, regulatory, or economic conditions often beyond the influence of one person or organization.

Takeaway: A strong stakeholder matrix names people at every level, from patients to regulators; records how important each one is, how much they support you, and what they care about; and plans regular, honest, two-way communication. Plans succeed with many advisers.
