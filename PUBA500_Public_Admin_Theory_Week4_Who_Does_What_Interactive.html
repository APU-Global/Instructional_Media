Week 4 Interactive: Functions and Structures of Public Organizations

Week 4 objective: Identify the five functions public bodies perform and the vertical, horizontal, and divisional structures they use.

Five functions of public bodies

  • Policy: strategic planning, legal drafting, minimum standards, policy analysis and evaluation, forecasting.
  • Co-ordination, supervision, and performance monitoring: coordinating relationships between bodies and monitoring and enabling subsidiary bodies to reach their targets.
  • Service delivery: providing products or services to internal or external customers, often by contract or by subnational government.
  • Support: financial management, human resources, information systems, infrastructure, staff training.
  • Regulatory: licensing, certification, permissions, accreditation, inspection, compliance, financial audit.

Three structures

  • Vertical: few people at the top, many below; few make policy decisions and many enact them. The classic bureaucracy, rooted in military command.
  • Horizontal: very few supervisors and many peers; staff work together cooperatively, as in some very small city or county programs.
  • Divisional: work divided by specialty or geography, as with court circuits and police or fire precincts.
