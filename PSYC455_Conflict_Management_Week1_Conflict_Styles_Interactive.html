Week 1 Interactive: Conflict Style Sorter

Week 1 objective: Identify the five conflict management styles and when each can fit, so you can name your own style in Discussion 1 and the Conflict Management Style Reflection.

Five styles from this week's reading

  • Competing: high concern for self, low for the other; striving to win.
  • Avoiding: often low concern for both; little direct communication about the conflict.
  • Accommodating: low concern for self, high for the other; going along without giving input.
  • Compromising: moderate concern for both; a partial win/lose.
  • Collaborating: high concern for both; takes the most work and aims for win/win.

Practice 1: name the style

Each situation is fictional. Choose the style it shows, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: when can it fit?

The reading says no style is right for every situation. Choose the style the text says can be appropriate here.

Practice 3: match the survey animal

The Conflict Management Style Survey uses animals. Choose the style each animal describes.

Takeaway: Your style is learned and contextual, not fixed. Know your default, ask for information before you react, and choose the style that fits the situation and the relationship.
