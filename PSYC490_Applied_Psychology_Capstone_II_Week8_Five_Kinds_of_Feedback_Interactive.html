Week 8 Interactive: Peer Learning and Feedback

Week 8 objective: Identify Carl Rogers's five types of feedback in peer comments and choose improvement comments that address the lesson, not the person.

Five kinds of feedback

  • Evaluative: a judgment of quality (the most common type).
  • Interpretive: paraphrasing or restating to confirm or clarify what you understood, often as a question.
  • Supportive: encouragement in response to the message.
  • Probing: a targeted request for specific information.
  • Understanding: sympathy and empathy for the person behind the message.

A behavioral evaluation assesses the action, not the actor.

Practice 1: name the feedback

Each comment responds to a fictional presentation by Sam on helping middle schoolers manage stress. Choose the type of feedback. Then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: the lesson, not the person

For each pair of improvement comments, choose the one that assesses the action and gives Sam something to use. Then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Mix the types: pair a specific evaluation with a probing question and sincere support. Aim every improvement comment at the lesson, not the person, and suggest something the presenter can try.
