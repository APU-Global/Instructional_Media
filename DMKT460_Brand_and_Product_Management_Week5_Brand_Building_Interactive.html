Week 5 Interactive: Elements of a Brand

Week 5 objective: Distinguish brand awareness, brand equity, brand personality, and brand positioning, and draft a positioning statement.

Four terms Discussion 5 asks about

  • Brand awareness: how familiar customers are with a brand and how much they know about it.
  • Brand equity: the value of a well-known brand that brings up positive (or negative) mental and emotional associations; it can support a price premium and loyalty.
  • Brand personality: the distinctive, emotional, self-expressive character associated with a brand, often defined with "is / is never" statements.
  • Brand positioning: how customers see the brand relative to competitors, and why it is different and better for its target.

Takeaway: A brand is much more than a logo: it is the sum of what people know, feel, and experience. Awareness gets you recognized, personality makes you distinct, positioning tells customers why you are different and better, and equity is the value all of that creates over time.
