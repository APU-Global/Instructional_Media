Week 3 Interactive: Money Illusion Lab

Week 3 objective: Calculate an inflation rate, convert nominal pay into real buying power, and identify who gains and who loses when inflation rises unexpectedly.

Two tools

Inflation rate

The percentage change in the cost of a fixed basket of goods:
(new cost − old cost) ÷ old cost × 100

Nominal to real

Divide by the price index (expressed as a decimal, so 105 becomes 1.05):
real value = nominal value ÷ (index ÷ 100)

Money illusion is treating a change in nominal dollars as if it were a change in real value.

Practice 3: Who wins, who loses?

Inflation unexpectedly jumps from 2% to 7%. Is each fictional person or organization helped or hurt?

Takeaway: Always ask what a number buys. Convert nominal pay, prices, and returns into real terms before you compare them. Unexpected inflation helps those who owe fixed amounts and hurts those who receive fixed amounts or whose pay adjusts late.
