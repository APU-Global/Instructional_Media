Week 6 Interactive: Employee Assessment

Week 6 focus: Chapter 11 explains how to design a performance evaluation system that encourages good work, tells employees how they are doing, develops them, and supports fair pay and promotion decisions.

Part 1: Match the appraisal method

Each fictional manager describes how they want to evaluate people. Choose the method from your text that fits best.

Part 2: Spot the rating error

Your text names several errors that weaken an appraisal system. Identify the problem in each fictional case.

Part 3: Which standard is measurable?

Reliability improves when two raters would give close ratings, which is easier when standards are measurable. Pick the standard two raters could score the same way.

Part 4: What does a review cycle cost a manager?

The text estimates the time a manager spends on formal reviews: employees × (time to write + time to discuss) + time to schedule. Adjust the sliders to see how span of control changes the load.

Takeaway: No single appraisal method is best, so combine methods that fit the job, write measurable standards tied to the job description, rate against the standard rather than against coworkers, train every rater, and budget real time for the conversation.
