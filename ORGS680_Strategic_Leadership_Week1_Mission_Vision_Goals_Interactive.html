Week 1 Interactive: Mission, Vision, and SMART Goals

Week 1 objective: Distinguish an organization's mission from its vision and turn a vision into goals people can act on.

Three statements, three jobs

MissionWho are we? States the organization's reason for being and how it serves its key stakeholders. It captures the past and present.
VisionWhere are we going? A brief, future-oriented declaration of what the organization hopes to become.
GoalsNarrower aims that guide daily work toward the vision. The most effective goals are SMART: specific, measurable, aggressive, realistic, and time-bound.
Why it mattersIn one survey, 98% of executives named a strong sense of vision as the top trait of strategic leaders, yet 90% doubted their own ability to create one.

Practice 1: Mission, vision, or goal?

Practice 2: Is the goal SMART?

Takeaway: Write the vision first, check that it points the same direction as the mission, then translate it into SMART goals. People support what they can see, measure, and reach.
