Week 2 Interactive: SWOT and SMART Goals

Week 2 objective: Use SWOT analysis to identify a performance gap and write a SMART goal that closes it (CLO 1).

From gap to goal in four moves

1. Mission and vision
Why the organization exists and what it hopes to become.
2. SWOT
Internal strengths and weaknesses; external opportunities and threats.
3. The gap
What the plan promises versus what is actually happening.
4. SMART goal
Specific, measurable, achievable, realistic (or relevant), time-bound.
