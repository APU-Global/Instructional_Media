Week 3 Interactive: Financial Condition Check

Week 3 objective: Compute and interpret ratios that describe a government's liquidity, profitability, and solvency, then explain the result in plain language for citizens.

Three questions about financial position

LIQUIDITY
Does the organization have liquid resources, especially cash, to cover its near-term liabilities?
PROFITABILITY
Do its revenues cover its operating expenses?
SOLVENCY
Can it generate enough resources to cover its near-term and long-term liabilities?

Takeaway: Ratios turn financial statements into answers about liquidity, profitability, and solvency, and they mean most when compared with peer governments, as the Ten Point Test does. Citizens can only be real partners in budget decisions when that information is objective, complete, relevant, easy to find, and easy to understand.
