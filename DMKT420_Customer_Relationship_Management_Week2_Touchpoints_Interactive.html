Week 2 Interactive: Customer Touchpoints

Week 2 objective: Classify customer touchpoints by purchase phase and by who starts them, and explain the job each one does in the relationship.

Every contact is part of the relationship

Touchpoints are all the points where a brand touches a customer's life, from an ad to a call-centre conversation to a monthly statement. They fall into three phases. Pre-purchase touchpoints build awareness, educate, and ignite the possibility of a relationship. Purchase touchpoints instil confidence, make buying easy, reinforce the decision, and reduce post-purchase dissonance. Post-purchase touchpoints deepen the relationship, deliver on the brand promise, and invite repeat purchases. A touchpoint can be brand-initiated (the brand reaches out) or customer-initiated (the customer reaches in).

Practice 1: which phase?

Hearth & Grain is a fictional neighborhood bakery. Choose the phase each touchpoint belongs to, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: who starts it?

Decide whether each touchpoint is started by the bakery or by the customer.

Takeaway: Map every touchpoint in all three phases, ask what job each one should do, and make sure they all speak with one voice. CRM data can remember the details, but trust grows when the business keeps its promises at every touchpoint.
