About the course

Opportunity for the graduate student to demonstrate applied and integrated knowledge of psychological concepts, theories, and practices. Students demonstrate proficiency of learning through experiences or research in psychology settings including business, government, education, or social organizations. Instructor permission to remain registered will be granted upon formal approval of capstone proposal by instructor.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Four discussions are graded, 50 points each (Weeks 1, 3, 5, and 7). Post an initial response and respond to at least three colleagues. The forums in Weeks 2, 4, 6, and 8 are optional, non-graded places to post updates, ask questions, or encourage your peers.

INITIAL POST
250–300 words
addressing every prompt, with a minimum of one APA formatted source where required.
COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
150–250 words each
respond to a minimum of three posts.
SUBSTANTIVE DIALOGUE
Suggest resources, ask clarifying questions, or add context and insight. If you agree, explain why and further the conversation.
Connect experience and academic knowledge, avoid simple agreement, and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Engagement (10), Critical Thinking (20), Contributions to Discussion (15), and Communication and Collaboration (5) = 50 points. Forums without an initial post and all required responses receive a zero.
