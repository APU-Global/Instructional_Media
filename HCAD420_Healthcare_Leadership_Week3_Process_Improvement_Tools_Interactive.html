Week 3 Interactive: Process Improvement Frameworks and Tools

Week 3 objective: Apply DMAIC, Lean, and SIPOC to propose administrative processes that support quality care (CLO 2).

Four tools, four questions

SIPOC
Who and what feed the process, what are its steps, and who receives its results?
DMAIC
How do we move from a defined problem to a controlled improvement?
Lean
Which activities consume resources but add no value for the patient?
Root cause
Why does the problem happen? (Fishbone diagrams sort causes by people, methods, materials, machines, and environment.)

Practice 1: which DMAIC phase?

A fictional emergency department works through a Six Sigma project. Choose the phase for each activity.

Practice 2: name the waste

Lean defines waste as any activity that consumes resources but creates no value for the customer. Name the waste in each fictional example.

Practice 3: build a SIPOC

An outpatient blood-draw process at a fictional clinic. Place each item in the SIPOC.

Takeaway: Look at the system before blaming people. Map the process with a SIPOC, follow Define-Measure-Analyze-Improve-Control, remove the seven wastes, and use a fishbone diagram and data to find root causes.
