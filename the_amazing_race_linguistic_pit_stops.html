📍 Pit Stop 1: The Rapid Naming & Fluency Sprint

⏱ 7 Minutes

Focus: Fluency (TPE U7.5e), Rapid Automatized Naming (RAN) & Dyslexia (D3.I), Oral Language for English Learners (U1.6).

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Examine the provided Rapid Automatized Naming (RAN) grid alongside a 2nd-grade student fluency reading sample.
  2. Diagnose the underlying processing barrier: Differentiate between a decoding failure and a rapid retrieval bottleneck (RAN deficit), noting how slow serial naming speed impacts working memory.
  3. Design a 60-second oral language rehearsal strategy (e.g., echo reading) that lowers cognitive load for English Learners while building automaticity.

📍 Pit Stop 2: The Syllabication Detour

⏱ 7 Minutes

Focus: Syllabication, Background Knowledge, and Fisher Modules 5-7.

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Review your assigned stack of complex, multisyllabic content-area vocabulary terms.
  2. Apply explicit syllable-division rules (VCCV and VCV patterns) to chunk each word (e.g., e/co/sys/tem).
  3. Formulate a 30-second background knowledge schema hook (visual, real-world connection, analogy) to anchor meaning.
Example: Ecosystem

Chunks: e/co/sys/tem (Closed / Open mix)

Hook: Show a visual of an aquarium. Ask how water, plants, and animals rely on each other.

📍 Pit Stop 3: The Vocabulary Knowledge Roadblock

⏱ 8 Minutes

Focus: Vocabulary Knowledge (TPE U7.7), IRIS Module 2 (Meaning Making & Foundational Skills).

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Select one Tier-Two or Tier-Three academic word from your team's thematic unit context.
  2. Construct an interactive semantic word web below including definition, visual anchor, sentence frame, and translanguaging assets.
Example: "Migration"
  • Definition: Moving from one home region or country to another to live or find better food/weather.
  • Visual Anchor: A flock of geese flying in a V-formation or a dotted map line crossing borders.
  • Frame: "Many animals and people experience ______________ during changing seasons or times of hardship."
  • Translanguaging Asset: Migración (Spanish Cognate) - explicitly connect the root word to honor Spanish-speaking English Learners' linguistic capital.
