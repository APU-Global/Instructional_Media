The Endocrine System: Feedback and Control

Blood glucose feedback loop

Disturb the system, then watch negative feedback pull glucose back to its set point.

Glucose is in the set-point range. Insulin and glucagon are both resting.
  • Receptor: islet cells sense blood glucose.
  • Control center & effector: the pancreas releases insulin or glucagon.
  • Targets: liver, muscle and fat cells change what they do with glucose.
  • Negative feedback: the response reverses the change that started it.
