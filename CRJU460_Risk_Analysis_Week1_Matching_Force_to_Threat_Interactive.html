    Practice 3: Would deadly force be reasonable under Garner?

    Each scenario is fictional. Decide whether deadly force would be constitutionally reasonable.

    Takeaway: Force is an officer’s last option, scaled to the threat. Under Tennessee v. Garner, deadly force is a Fourth Amendment seizure and is reasonable only when there is probable cause that a suspect poses a significant threat of death or serious physical injury to the officer or others.
