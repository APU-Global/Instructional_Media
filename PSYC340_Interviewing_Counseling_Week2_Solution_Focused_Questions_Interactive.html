Week 2 Interactive: Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

Week 2 objective: Recognize the core questions of solution-focused brief therapy (SFBT) and choose responses that build on what is already working, as you will in Discussion 2 and AI Roleplay #1.

Quick guide

Miracle question
Imagine the problem is solved overnight. What would be different in the morning?
Exception question
When is the problem not present? What was different then?
Scaling question
On a scale from 1 to 10, where are you now? Used to set goals and track progress.
Presession change
What has already changed since the client decided to come?

SFBT assumes the client has the resources to reach a preferred future. The counselor leads from one step behind and focuses on the present and the future rather than on the causes of the problem.

Score: 0

Takeaway: SFBT builds on what is already working. Ask about the preferred future, look for exceptions, scale progress, and let the client be the expert on their own life.
