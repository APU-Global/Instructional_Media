Week 6 Interactive: Functional Fixedness and Insight

Week 6 objective: Recognize functional fixedness and related problem-solving traps, and match each one with a research-based way to get unstuck.

The candle problem

Karl Duncker (1945) gave people a candle, a box of thumbtacks, and a book of matches and asked them to attach the candle to the wall so it would not drip onto the table below. Before you reveal the answer, decide: which object did most people fail to use in a new way?

Practice 2: Choose a way through

Each person is stuck. Choose the strategy from this week’s research that fits best.

Takeaway: Our schemas tell us what things are “for,” and that can hide a solution (functional fixedness). To get unstuck, list many possible uses (divergent thinking), test ideas that could prove your first guess wrong, step back after trial and error to make room for insight, and work with people who support and challenge you.
