1. Biological Foundation

Subcategory: Brain & Body

Core Question: How does activity in your brain and body give rise to your thoughts, feelings, and actions?

2. Cognitive Foundation

Core Question: How do your mental activities affect your thoughts, feelings, and actions?

3. Developmental Foundation

Core Question: How do you change over your life—from birth through old age—in terms of your thoughts, feelings, and actions?

4. Social & Personality Foundation

Core Question: How do social factors and your personal characteristics impact your thoughts, feelings, and actions?

5. Mental & Physical Health Foundation

Core Question: What affects your mental and physical health, and how can you develop healthy behaviors?
