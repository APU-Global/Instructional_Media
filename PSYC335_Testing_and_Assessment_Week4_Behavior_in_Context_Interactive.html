Week 4 Interactive: Behavioral Assessment

Week 4 objective: Separate observable behavior from interpretation, apply PSC-17 cutoffs, and interpret informant disagreement responsibly.

Three ideas to use

Operational definitions: a behavioral measure records what a person actually does, defined precisely enough to count. Screener cutoffs: on the PSC-17 a parent rates 17 items (Never 0, Sometimes 1, Often 2); a total of 15 or more suggests significant problems that call for comprehensive assessment, and the subscale cutoffs are Internalising 5+ (items 1-5), Attention 7+ (items 6-10), and Externalising 7+ (items 11-17). Informants: when a parent and a teacher disagree, the difference shows how behavior shifts across settings; it is not error to average away.
