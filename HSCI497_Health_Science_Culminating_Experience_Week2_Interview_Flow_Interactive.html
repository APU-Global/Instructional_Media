Week 2 Interactive: Interviewing with Confidence

Week 2 objective: Recognize the five stages of an interview conversation and choose responses that prepare well, answer the purpose behind a question, recover when things go off track, and follow up professionally.

The interview is a conversation

Business Communication for Success describes five stages of conversation and applies them to the employment interview: initiation (signal openness), preview (what the conversation is about), talking points (getting down to business), feedback (clarify and restate to reach understanding), and closing (wrap up, often naming when you will talk next). Preparation comes first, and follow-up comes after.
