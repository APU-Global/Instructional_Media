Week 5 Interactive: CBT and REBT

Week 5 objective: Use Ellis’s ABC model to separate an event from the belief about it, tell rational from irrational beliefs, and recognize functional, empirical, and logical disputes for Discussion 5 and Partner Roleplay #2.

Quick guide

A, B, C
Antecedent event, Belief about the event, and the emotional and behavioral Consequence.
Irrational belief
Rigid, illogical, and inconsistent with reality. It often sounds like an absolute demand (“must,” “should”).
Rational belief
Flexible, logical, and consistent with reality. It sounds like a preference.
Disputes
Functional: Is this belief helping? Empirical: What evidence supports it? Logical: Does a demand follow from a preference?

Score: 0

Takeaway: In CBT and REBT, the event does not cause the feeling by itself; the belief about it matters. Map the ABC, listen for rigid demands, and help the client test the belief for helpfulness, evidence, and logic. The aim is healthy negative emotion, not no emotion.
