Week 5 Interactive: Making a Differential Diagnosis

Week 5 objective: Distinguish disorders with overlapping symptoms by duration, triggers, and context, and recognize when no diagnosis fits.

Clues that separate similar disorders

  • Trauma timeline: symptoms from 3 days to 1 month after a trauma suggest acute stress disorder; at least one month, across all four symptom categories, suggests PTSD. Adjustment disorder follows an identifiable stressor within the past 3 months. Prolonged grief disorder applies only when the death was at least 12 months ago.
  • Anxiety triggers: worry about many things for 6+ months with 3+ somatic symptoms (GAD); unexpected, recurrent panic attacks (panic disorder); fear of being judged (social anxiety); fear of a specific object or situation (specific phobia).
  • A medical cause, such as hyperthyroidism, should be ruled out with a physical exam.
