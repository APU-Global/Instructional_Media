Week 7 Interactive: Evaluating Marketing Campaign Effectiveness

Week 7 objective: Choose KPIs that fit a campaign's objectives, calculate funnel conversion, and interpret a change in results.

Measures follow objectives

Decide what the campaign must accomplish, then choose measures that show whether it did. Reach is the number of people exposed to a message; frequency is how often they are exposed. Conversion ratios track movement through the funnel. Satisfaction surveys and complaints capture the patient's experience. Before acting on a change, ask what caused it.

Practice 1: Match the KPI to the objective

Harbor Family Health (fictional) is promoting its mobile screening van. Choose the measure that best shows whether each objective was met.

Practice 2: Funnel calculator

Enter one month of (fictional) campaign results. The calculator shows each stage's conversion ratio (how many people at one stage it takes to move one person to the next) and the percentage who move on.

Practice 3: Separate the usual from the new

For five years, Harbor's screenings have dropped an average of 20 percent each summer. This summer they dropped 28 percent. About how many percentage points of this year's drop likely came from something new?

Takeaway: Start with the objective, pick the KPI that shows it, follow people through the whole funnel, listen to patient feedback, and check causality before you change course.
