Branches and shared roots

Two major branches. Sunni and Shia communities differ historically over leadership after Muhammad. Both share the core beliefs and the Pillars.
Shared roots. Islam shares prophets and themes with Judaism and Christianity, which is one reason careful study of one tradition helps in understanding the others.
