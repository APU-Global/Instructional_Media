About the course

Effective internal and external communications strategies. The course covers appropriate use of written, verbal, electronic, and other technology-based communications. Students practice ethical use of messaging for public communications. Coursework requires practical application and demonstration of skills. Throughout the course you weigh communication choices through a Christian worldview.

Projected total

Initial post by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight weekly discussions is worth 50 points (Week 8: initial post by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here is what every discussion asks of you.

INITIAL POST
Answer every question in the prompt
Review the week’s assigned reading and resources first, then respond substantively to each part of the prompt.
COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
At least two substantive responses
Suggest further resources, ask questions of clarification, or add context and insight. Avoid simple posts of agreement; if you agree, explain why and further the conversation.
SOURCES
Cite scholarly sources where required
Several prompts ask you to use references from the reading to support your answer. Discussions are checked for plagiarism and AI-generated content.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus’s graduate discussion rubric: Assignment Requirements (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10, including APA formatting), for 50 points.
