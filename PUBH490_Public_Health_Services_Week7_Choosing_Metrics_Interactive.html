Week 7 Interactive: Process and Outcome Measures

Week 7 objective: Distinguish process measures from outcome measures, match evaluation questions to evaluation types, and read an indicator tracking table.

Key ideas

Monitoring

Tracks the program's operational work continuously at the activity and output levels: did we do what we planned?

Evaluation

Looks at outcomes and goals, less often and more formally: is the program achieving what it set out to do?

Indicators

Activity indicators describe implementation, output indicators the products of activities, and outcome indicators whether effects were achieved.

Evaluation types

Formative, process/implementation, outcome, impact, and economic evaluations answer different questions.

Practice 1: process or outcome?

A fictional high school runs a vaping-prevention program. Decide whether each measure tracks the process or the outcome.

Practice 2: which type of evaluation?

Match each evaluation question about the same program to the type of evaluation that answers it.
