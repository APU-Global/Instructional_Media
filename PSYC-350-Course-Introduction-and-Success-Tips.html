Welcome to PSYC 350: Social Psychology

A comprehensive look at how the thoughts, feelings, and behavior of individuals are shaped by actual, imagined, or implied social interactions.

What you will do
Explore pertinent research along with the ethical and cultural aspects of social psychology, and reflect on how social issues connect to Christian principles and to the people they affect.
