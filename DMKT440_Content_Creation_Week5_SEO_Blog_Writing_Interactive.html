Week 5 Interactive: Writing for Readers and Search Engines

Week 5 objective: Apply digital copywriting and SEO principles to review and improve a blog post, including one drafted with an AI tool.

Write for people, then for search engines

Stokes says the human audience always comes first: research who you are writing for, put the main idea at the top, and make copy easy to scan with headings, lists, short paragraphs, and the active voice. Then help search engines understand the page: the key phrase near the beginning of a page title under 60 characters, a meta description of about 150 to 160 characters with a clear call to action, at least 250 words of on-page copy, descriptive alt text, and synonyms instead of keyword stuffing. The course infographic adds: target several related keywords, use images, and check engagement such as bounce rate and time on site.
