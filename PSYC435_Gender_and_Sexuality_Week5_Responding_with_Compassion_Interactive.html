Week 5 Interactive: Compassion Skills

Week 5 objective: Recognize active listening, validation, emotional support, and non-judgment, and choose responses that convey compassion and hospitality.

Key terms

  • Active listening: full attention, no interrupting, noticing nonverbal cues, paraphrasing, and open-ended questions.
  • Validation: acknowledging emotions, which does not mean you necessarily agree with the person's perspective.
  • Emotional support: being present and offering a safe space; comfort does not always require fixing the problem.
  • Non-judgment: approaching others with an open mind, without assumptions.

Practice 1: Name the skill

A counselor is meeting with Riley, a fictional college student. Choose the skill each counselor action shows most clearly.

Practice 2: Compassionate or not?

Riley says: "Since I told my roommate I'm bisexual, she barely talks to me. I feel so alone here." Label each possible response.

Takeaway: Compassion is a skill you can practice: listen, validate, stay present, and set assumptions aside. Validating a feeling honors the person without asking you to give up your own convictions.
