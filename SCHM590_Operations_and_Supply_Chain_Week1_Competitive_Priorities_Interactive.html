Week 1 Interactive: Competitive Priorities and Customer Value

Week 1 objective: Connect what customers value to the operations and supply chain priority a firm must emphasize.

The four competitive priorities

Customers weigh key purchasing criteria such as price, quality, variety, and timeliness. Operations answers with four competitive priorities: cost (minimize cost and waste), quality (superior, durable, defect-free products), flexibility (adapt designs and output quickly), and delivery (reliable, speedy delivery). No firm can excel at all four at once, so each choice is a trade-off.

Practice 2: Order qualifier or order winner?

Order qualifiers are the nonnegotiable requirements that get you considered. An order winner is the characteristic that wins the sale.

Takeaway: A customer-focused supply chain starts with the customer’s key purchasing criteria. Meet every order qualifier, invest in the order winners, and choose your trade-offs among cost, quality, flexibility, and delivery deliberately, because they change over time.
