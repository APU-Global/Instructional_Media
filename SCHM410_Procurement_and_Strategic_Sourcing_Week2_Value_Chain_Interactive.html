Week 2 Interactive: Primary, Support, and Supplier Criteria

Week 2 objective: Distinguish primary from support activities in Porter's value chain, and match supplier evaluation criteria to potential or existing suppliers.

Key terms

Primary activities directly create and distribute the product: inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, and service.

Support activities give the primary activities their foundation: firm infrastructure, human resource management, technology, and procurement.

Suppliers are judged two ways: a potential supplier on credentials such as certification; an existing supplier on measured performance such as on-time and accurate delivery.

Practice 1: Harbor Bakehouse's value chain

Harbor Bakehouse is a fictional regional bakery. Choose the value chain activity each task belongs to.

Practice 2: Potential or existing supplier?

For each criterion, decide whether it is mainly used to judge a potential supplier (before orders) or to keep evaluating an existing supplier.

Takeaway: Separate the activities that make and deliver the product from those that support them, then look for value in both. Judge new suppliers on credentials, and keep judging current suppliers on measured performance.
