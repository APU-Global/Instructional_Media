Week 7 Interactive: Measuring Supplier Performance

Week 7 objective: Calculate common supplier and supply chain indicators, place them in the SCOR model, and recognize indicators to use with care.

Key terms

Supplier capabilities to evaluate: on-time delivery, accuracy of delivery, ability to handle demand fluctuations, and ability to hold inventory until needed.

The SCOR model benchmarks five processes: plan (design and planning of the chain), source (ordering and acquiring goods: purchase orders, scheduling, receipts, storage), make, deliver (customer orders, packaging, shipping), and return (products returned by customers).

Inventory turnover check

Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold ÷ average aggregate inventory value. The defaults are the operations text's food-supplier example (last year). Try the earlier year: $16,255,000 and $1,763,350.

Takeaway: A fair scorecard uses several indicators tied to the whole chain, sets the same objectives and metrics for both supplier and buyer, and never judges a partner on price or speed alone.
