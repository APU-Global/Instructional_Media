About the course

Investigation and assessment of the effect of cultural concepts on health promotion and disease prevention. This course explores how culture influences health behaviors in multicultural populations.

The course introduction adds that you will explore health disparities, bias, communication strategies, and inclusive public health interventions, along with personal ideologies, individual skills development, and leadership competencies. Each week pairs a chapter of Diversity and Cultural Competence in Health Care (Weeks 1–6) with articles and short videos. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Projected total

Eight discussions, 40 points each

Each week’s discussion has a Review step (the assigned reading and resources), a Respond prompt, and a Discuss step with your colleagues. Respond by Thursday and discuss through Saturday (Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Posts and responses made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
Answer every Respond prompt by the first deadline, supported by 2 APA-formatted sources.
PEER RESPONSES
150–250 words each
Respond to more than the minimum of two peers. Expand on points in your own words with scholarly support, offer alternative perspectives, or ask questions of clarification.
SUBSTANTIVE
Avoid simple posts of agreement; if you agree, explain why and further the conversation. Discussions are checked for plagiarism and AI-generated content.

Discussions are graded with the 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements (16), Integrative Communication (16), and Written Communication (8), for 40 points.
