Week 6 Interactive: From Information to Intelligence

Week 6 objective: Identify intelligence agency partnerships and the process that informs counterterrorism strategies.

Put the intelligence process in order

The National Criminal Intelligence Sharing Plan describes six steps. Use the arrow buttons to move each step, then select Check the order.

    Practice 1: Information or intelligence?

    Each example is fictional. Decide whether it is raw information, tactical intelligence, or strategic intelligence.

    Practice 2: Good intelligence practice?

    Decide whether each practice fits the guidelines for intelligence work described this week.

    Takeaway: Information plus analysis equals intelligence. Tactical intelligence drives today's cases; strategic intelligence guides long-term priorities. A disciplined six-step process, with legal safeguards and information sharing as policy, connects federal, state, and local partners.

    Process and definitions from McKee, A. J. (n.d.), Police Methods, Section 1.5 Intelligence-Led Policing (docmckee.com); local practice from Nee, K. (2019), "Notes From the Field: Connection to Combat Terrorism," National Institute of Justice. Examples are fictional.
