About the course

Examination of ethical principles from a Christian perspective. This course raises the student’s moral recognition of ethical issues within an organizational setting, including the role leadership plays in creating and sustaining an ethical culture in the workplace. Students utilize case studies, personal experiences, and current events to examine the implications of decisions made by organizational leaders in response to an ethical dilemma.

Welcome aboard: Every leader casts light or shadow.
