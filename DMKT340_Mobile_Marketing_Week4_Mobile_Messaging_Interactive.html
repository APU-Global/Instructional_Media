Week 4 Interactive: Mobile Messaging Strategy

Week 4 objective: Check email and SMS practices for permission, value, and fit, and match each campaign question to the metric that answers it.

What keeps a message welcome

Permission: people must opt in to marketing messages and be able to opt out at will; never send without permission, and honor every unsubscribe, including an SMS "STOP to opt out" option. Value: each message should be useful to the reader. Fit: an SMS has 160 characters, should be branded so people know who sent it, and should arrive at a sensible time. Measure: track delivery and bounces, unique opens, unsubscribes, pass-on rate, clickthrough, and conversion, and use A/B split tests.

Takeaway: A message on someone's phone is a privilege. Ask first, bring value, time it well, and let the numbers tell you whether people still want to hear from you.
