Week 6 Interactive: Interest For You and Against You

Week 6 objective: Choose the right finance model (compound interest, annuity, payout annuity, or loan) and use it to compare saving and borrowing decisions.

Four models

Compound interest (one deposit)
P_N = P₀(1 + r/k)^(Nk)
Savings annuity (many deposits)
P_N = d((1 + r/k)^(Nk) − 1) ÷ (r/k)
Payout annuity (many withdrawals)
P₀ = d(1 − (1 + r/k)^(−Nk)) ÷ (r/k)
Loan (many payments)
P₀ = d(1 − (1 + r/k)^(−Nk)) ÷ (r/k)

P₀ = starting amount, d = regular deposit or payment, r = annual rate as a decimal, k = compounding periods per year, N = years. Simple interest is I = P₀rt.
