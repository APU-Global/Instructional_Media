Should Jackson's Quality Copies buy the copier?

The copier costs $50,000 and lasts 7 years. It saves $11,000 a year in labor, costs $1,000 a year to maintain, and sells for $5,000 at the end. Change the required rate of return to see how timing changes the decision.

Takeaway: NPV adds the present value of all cash inflows and subtracts the present value of all outflows. If NPV is zero or more, the investment earns at least the required rate of return: accept. If it is negative, reject.
