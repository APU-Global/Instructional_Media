Week 3 Interactive: Motivation Theory Lab

Week 3 objective: Distinguish intrinsic from extrinsic motivation and apply four theories of motivation to real learners.

Four theories at a glance

  • Maslow's hierarchy of needs: basic needs (physiological, safety, love/belonging, esteem) come before higher-order needs such as self-actualization.
  • Expectancy-value theory: motivation depends on expecting to succeed and on valuing the task.
  • Self-determination theory: autonomy, competence, and relatedness foster intrinsic motivation.
  • Goal orientation theory: mastery goals (learning and improving) versus performance goals (looking competent, avoiding failure).

Takeaway: Rewards are not free: they can crowd out a learner's own reasons to learn. Ask whether a learner's needs are met, whether they expect to succeed and value the task, whether they have autonomy, competence, and relatedness, and whether they are aiming for mastery.
