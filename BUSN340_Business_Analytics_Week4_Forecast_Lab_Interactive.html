Week 4 Interactive: Forecast Lab

Week 4 objective: Evaluate analytical tools for sales and market share projections (CLOs 1 and 3).

Two families of forecasting tools

Qualitative
Based on judgment; useful when past data are not available, such as for a new product: executive judgment, sales force opinions, the Delphi method, and market surveys.
Quantitative
Based on past numerical data, when patterns are expected to continue: time series methods (naive, moving average) and causal methods such as regression.
