Week 2 Interactive: Sleep Detective

Week 2 objective: Look beyond hours in bed: recognize common sleep disorders, judge everyday sleep habits, and compare your own sleep with recommendations (CLO 1, CLO 4).

Practice 1: Name that sleep problem

Sleep problems are not only about the number of hours. Read each fictional case and choose the best match.

Practice 2: Bedtime habit audit

Decide whether each habit helps or hurts sleep.

Practice 3: Your sleep week

Discussion 2 asks you to compare your own sleep with recommendations. Set the hours you slept each night this past week (or a typical week). Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

Average per night: Weekly total: hours

Scale 0 to 12 hours; the green band marks the 7 to 8 hours most adults say they need.

Takeaway: Your body clock and sleep stages need enough time to run their course, and most adults need seven to eight hours. When good habits are in place and sleep is still poor, a disorder such as insomnia or sleep apnea may be the cause, and that is a reason to talk with a doctor.
