Week 8 Interactive: Name the Act, Weigh the Argument

Week 8 objective: Use precise end-of-life terms and represent both sides of the physician-assisted death debate fairly, as Discussion 8 asks.

Practice 1: name the act

Each case is fictional. Choose the term that fits, then select Check my answers. The key difference is often intent and who acts.

Practice 2: which side does this argument support?

Sort each statement. Sorting an argument is not agreeing with it; a strong post states the best version of each side.

Takeaway: Define terms by intent and who acts. Supporters of physician-assisted death stress autonomy and relief of suffering; opponents stress the dignity of persons, professional duties, and the difference between relieving suffering and intending death. Laws differ by state and have changed over time, so check the current law.
