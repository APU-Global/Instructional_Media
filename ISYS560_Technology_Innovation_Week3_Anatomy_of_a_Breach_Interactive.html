Week 3 Interactive: Information Assets and Cyber Security

Week 3 objective: Analyze a data breach by its people, technology, and process causes, and tell compliance apart from real security.

The TJX case in brief

A hacker in a store parking lot entered TJX's network through an insecure Wi-Fi base station. At least 45.7 million card numbers were stolen, and estimated losses ran from $1.35 billion to $4.5 billion. Three kinds of failure combined: a people failure (an alleged FBI informant who double-crossed investigators), a technology failure (weak WEP wireless security), and a process failure (a delayed rollout of the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard).

Practice 2: Compliance or security?

Decide whether the reading supports each statement.

Takeaway: When you analyze a breach, look for the people, technology, and process failures that combined. Then ask which control would have broken the chain. Frameworks and compliance rules set a floor, but security comes from a culture of vigilance across the whole organization.
