Week 2 Interactive: Prompting for Cultural Competence

Week 2 objective: Recognize cultural dimensions in a business situation and choose wording for a culturally competent message, the skill behind Discussion 2's AI mentor prompt.

Cultural dimensions to listen for

  • Individualism vs. collectivism: looser ties to groups, or identity shaped by group membership.
  • Power distance: how acceptable an unequal distribution of power is.
  • Uncertainty avoidance: how threatened people feel by ambiguous or risky situations.
  • Masculinity vs. femininity: valuing achievement and competition, or relationships and quality of life.
  • Direct vs. indirect / low vs. high context: getting straight to the point, or letting the setting and relationship carry meaning.

Cultures are tendencies, not rules for every person. Use the cues, and still treat each reader as an individual.

Practice 1: name the dimension

Each situation comes from Northbeam Components, a fictional parts maker with partners in several countries. Choose the cultural dimension the situation best illustrates.

Practice 2: choose the better wording

Northbeam must tell a long-time partner that a critical component will be six months late. For each audience cue, choose the opening or detail that fits best.

Takeaway: A culturally competent message reads the audience's cultural tendencies (power distance, uncertainty avoidance, collectivism, directness) and avoids stereotypes. Whatever the culture, the message should be honest, fair, and clear about the plan. Put these cues into your AI prompt as persona, context, and task.
