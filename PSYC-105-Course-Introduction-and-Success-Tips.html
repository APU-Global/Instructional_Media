Welcome to PSYC 105: Introduction to Psychology

A foundational look at the scientific study of human behavior and mental processes, through the lens of a Christian worldview.

What you will do
Examine how biological, cognitive, developmental, social, and health factors shape who we are, and learn to think critically about human nature, mental health, and the evolution of therapeutic practice.
