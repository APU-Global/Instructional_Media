Practice: You review the bail decision

Each bail hearing is fictional. Decide whether the decision is consistent with the Eighth Amendment cases or likely excessive or improper.

Takeaway: Bail exists to secure the defendant's appearance and protect the presumption of innocence, not to punish. It must be individualized and no higher than needed (Stack v. Boyle). Detention before trial is allowed for flight risk or danger only with careful procedures (Salerno), and fines and forfeitures must not be grossly disproportionate.
