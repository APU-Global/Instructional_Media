Week 8 Interactive: Dimensions of Culture

Week 8 objective: Identify the cultural dimensions behind management practices so you can lead across borders without misreading people.

Why dimensions help

The GLOBE project studied more than 17,000 managers in 62 societal cultures to link culture with leadership. Its dimensions include performance orientation, uncertainty avoidance, assertiveness, power distance, gender egalitarianism, institutional collectivism, humane orientation, and future orientation. Knowing them helps a leader ask “what does this behavior mean here?” before judging it.

Practice: which dimension is showing?

Each company is fictional. Choose the cultural dimension each practice reflects most clearly.

Takeaway: Before you judge a behavior as slow, rude, or aggressive, ask which cultural dimension might explain it. Global leaders read the culture first, then adapt how they communicate, decide, and reward.
