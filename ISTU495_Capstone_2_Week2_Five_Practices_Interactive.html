Week 2 Interactive: The Five Practices

Week 2 focus: Recognize the Five Practices of Exemplary Leadership in everyday situations and connect one commitment to your own experience for Discussion 2.

The five practices

The Week 2 Overview quotes James Kouzes: “When leaders are doing their best, they Model the Way, Inspire a Shared Vision, Challenge the Process, Enable Others to Act, and Encourage the Heart.” The Exemplary Leadership module breaks each practice into behaviors you can observe, such as clarifying your values, seizing the initiative, creating a climate of trust, and personalizing recognition.

Practice 1: Which practice is this?

Each leader is fictional. Choose the practice the leader is showing most clearly, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: Shape your commitment story

Discussion 2 asks you to describe an experience that connects to one commitment. Choose a commitment to see which practice it echoes, then sketch your story. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Choose a commitment.

Takeaway: The Five Practices are a vocabulary for what leaders actually do. A commitment pairs a visible action with its purpose, so when you tell your story, name the commitment, the details, the people involved, and the outcomes.
