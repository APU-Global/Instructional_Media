Agent, Host, Environment

Epidemiology studies the distribution and determinants of health-related states in populations and applies that study to control health problems. Break the chain of disease at different points, then place prevention on a timeline.

Part 1 · Break the chain: the epidemiologic triad

The triad explains disease as the interaction of agent, host, and environment. Changing any one element can change the likelihood of disease. Scenario: several people became ill after eating at a restaurant (a foodborne Salmonella outbreak). Select an action, then select the element it targets.

Part 2 · Levels of prevention

Prevention can occur before, during, or after disease develops. Primary prevention prevents disease from occurring; secondary detects it early; tertiary reduces its consequences.

Using evidence in practice

Epidemiologists describe patterns of disease, look for causes, and recommend ways to prevent or control illness, in health departments, research, hospitals, and policy settings. Their findings guide public health practice.
