Week 2 Interactive: Culturally Competent Care Strategies

Week 2 objective: Identify how healthcare organizations provide culturally competent care at the individual, organizational, access, and implementation levels.

Why this matters

Culturally and linguistically diverse patients use healthcare services less and face barriers such as language, legal restrictions, and different health beliefs. A scoping review of 67 studies by Handtke, Schilgen, and Mösko (2019) sorted what organizations actually do into four groups. Knowing the groups helps an administrator see where a program is strong and where it has gaps.

Practice 1: name the level

Each item describes a strategy from the review. Choose the group it belongs to.

Practice 2: true or false?

Decide whether each statement is true or false according to the Handtke et al. review.

Takeaway: Cultural competence is a property of the whole organization: the people who give care, the services and environment around them, the ways care reaches the community, and the leadership and plans that keep change going. Check all four levels when you assess a program.

Facts from Handtke, O., Schilgen, B., & Mösko, M. (2019), "Culturally competent healthcare: A scoping review of strategies implemented in healthcare organizations and a model of culturally competent healthcare provision," PLoS ONE, 14(7), e0219971. Scenarios are fictional.
