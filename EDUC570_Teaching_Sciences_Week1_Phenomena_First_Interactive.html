Week 1 Interactive: Planning for Engagement with Phenomena

Week 1 objective: Distinguish phenomena from topics, explanations, and questions, and identify what makes phenomena effective for instruction.

What is a phenomenon?

Natural phenomena are “observable events that occur in the universe and that we can use our science knowledge to explain or predict.” They are not topics, explanations, scientific terms, or questions. Explaining phenomena is “the central reason students engage in the three dimensions of the NGSS,” and “it is the phenomenon plus the student-generated questions about the phenomenon that guides the learning and teaching.”

Takeaway: Open with an event children can observe and relate to, not a topic, a term, or a question. Let students’ own questions about it drive the practices they use, and expect them to figure it out step by step, not immediately.
