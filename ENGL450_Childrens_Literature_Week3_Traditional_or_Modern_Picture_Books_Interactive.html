Week 3 Interactive: Traditional or Modern?

Week 3 objective: Use the five dimensions from "The Changing Image of the Picturebook" to decide whether a picture book is traditional or modern, as Discussion 3 asks you to do with Black and White.

The five dimensions

Practice 1: Traditional or modern?

Practice 2: Name the dimension

Which of the five dimensions does each clue describe?

Takeaway: Modern picture books ask more of readers: complex compositions, surprising viewpoints, pages that break their own edges, and stories readers help build. Successful reading of them calls for flexible thinking, so look closely and name the dimension you see.
