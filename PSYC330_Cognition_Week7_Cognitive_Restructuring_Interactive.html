Week 7 Interactive: Cognitive Restructuring

Week 7 objective: Practice the steps of cognitive restructuring: name the thought behind a feeling, weigh the evidence, and replace an inaccurate thought or make an action plan when the thought is accurate.

The steps

  1. Name the distressing feeling and the specific thought underneath it.
  2. Weigh the evidence: what supports the thought, and what does not?
  3. If the evidence does not support it, replace it with a more accurate thought (not just a more positive one).
  4. If the thought is accurate, make an action plan.

0 of 4 cases completed correctly

Takeaway: Cognitive restructuring aims at accurate thinking, not forced positivity. Test each thought against concrete evidence. When the evidence does not support it, write a balanced replacement; when it does, the next step is an action plan. In practice, a trained clinician guides this work, and clients practice it between sessions.

Steps and principles from Mueser et al. (2015), "Evaluation of cognitive restructuring for post-traumatic stress disorder in people with severe mental illness," British Journal of Psychiatry, 206(6), 501–508 (PMC4450219), and Introduction to Psychology v1.0 (Saylor Academy), Section 13.1 "Reducing Disorder by Confronting It: Psychotherapy."
