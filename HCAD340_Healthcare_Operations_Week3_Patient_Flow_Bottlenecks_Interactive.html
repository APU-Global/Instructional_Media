Week 3 Interactive: Finding the Bottleneck in Patient Flow

Week 3 objective: Discuss methods for improving patient flow optimization (CLO 3).

Three ideas to apply

The bottleneck
The slowest step in a process. Its capacity sets the capacity of the whole process.
Waiting and idleness
Work piles up in front of the bottleneck, and the steps after it sit idle waiting for it.
Invest at the constraint
Speeding up a step that is not the bottleneck adds no output. Fixing the bottleneck may reveal a new one.

Try it: Maple Grove Clinic’s patient flow

Maple Grove Clinic (fictional) sees every patient in four steps, one after another. Change the minutes per patient or the number of staff at a step and watch the bottleneck move. Capacity per step = (minutes open × staff) ÷ minutes per patient.

Practice: read the flow

Use the clinic’s starting numbers (4, 8, 15, and 5 minutes; one staff member each; 480 minutes). Try changes in the tool above, then answer.

Takeaway: Find the slowest step before spending money: the bottleneck’s capacity is the process’s capacity, and improvements elsewhere add nothing. When you fix the bottleneck, look for the next one. In a hospital, ED crowding can start downstream, with inpatient beds and discharges. Use this thinking in Discussion 3 and in Part 1 of Designing Comprehensive Solutions.
