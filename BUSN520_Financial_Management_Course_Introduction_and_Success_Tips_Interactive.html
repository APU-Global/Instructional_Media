About the course

Analysis of business conditions and the development of rational solutions based on sound financial practices. Students acquire managerial skills for budget operations, accessing capital, and investing in resources. Students evaluate shareholder value-maximizing goals within an ethical and biblical framework.

There is no textbook to buy: your readings are Harvard Business Publishing course materials, library articles, and videos linked in each week. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points (Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
300 words minimum
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the week’s resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150 words minimum each
Post at least two substantive responses to your colleagues. Offer further resources, questions of clarification, or context and insight; avoid simple posts of agreement.
SOURCES
APA formatting, with a minimum of 1–2 scholarly, peer-reviewed sources properly cited and referenced. Wikipedia, Investopedia, blogs, and online magazines are not scholarly sources.

Discussions are graded with the course discussion rubric: Discussion Responses (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10), for 50 points.
