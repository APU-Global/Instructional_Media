About the course

The public health official is often required to lead or manages people, projects, and organizations, and this course presents the knowledge, skills, aptitudes and attitudes required to successfully fulfill those responsibilities. In fulfilling this administrative role, project management, personnel oversight, budgeting, communication, and external stakeholder issues (e.g., regulators, boards, constituents, and media) are considered. Case studies and examples from practice will be examined and evaluated.

You will also describe how a Christian worldview informs your understanding and practice. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points (Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here is what the course asks of every discussion.

INITIAL POST
By the first deadline
Respond to the prompt with a substantive post, answering every part of the week’s questions.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
By the second deadline
Interact with at least two peers. Expand on their points, offer alternative perspectives, or reflect back their meaning to seek clarification; avoid simple posts of agreement.
SOURCES
Scholarly support
Support initial posts and responses with scholarly references and authoritative sources, in APA format.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Assignment Requirements (15), Integrative Communication (15), and Written Communication (10), for 40 points. Posts made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.
