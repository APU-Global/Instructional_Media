Week 2 Interactive: Content Theories of Motivation

Week 2 focus: Compare the four content theories in Discussion 2 (Maslow, ERG, Herzberg, McClelland) and choose the one that best fits a workplace situation.

The four content theories at a glance

MaslowHierarchy of needs. Five ranked needs: physiological, safety, social, esteem, self-actualization. A satisfied need no longer motivates.
ERGAlderfer. Existence, relatedness, growth. No fixed order, several needs at once, and frustration-regression.
HerzbergTwo-factor theory. Hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators (achievement, recognition, interesting work, growth) motivate.
McClellandAcquired needs. Achievement, affiliation, and power, in different strengths. The dominant need drives behavior.

Part 1: Which need does it meet?

For each workplace provision, choose the Maslow need it mainly satisfies, then select Check my answers. The feedback also gives the ERG category.

Part 2: Which theory explains it best?

Each workplace is fictional. Choose the content theory that best explains what is happening, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Content theories ask what people need. Maslow and ERG describe a range of needs (ERG allows several at once and regression when growth is blocked), Herzberg separates hygiene factors from true motivators, and McClelland explains how a dominant need for achievement, affiliation, or power shapes behavior. In Discussion 2, show why the theory you chose fits your scenario better than the others.
