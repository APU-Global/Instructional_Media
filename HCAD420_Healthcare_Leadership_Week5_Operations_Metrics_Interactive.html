Week 5 Interactive: Data-Driven Operations

Week 5 objective: Use operational metrics and visual tools to support management decisions that sustain healthcare operations (CLO 4).

Practice 1: utilization and efficiency

A fictional imaging suite. Design capacity is the maximum output under ideal conditions; effective capacity subtracts planned downtime, maintenance, and other constraints. Change the numbers to see the indicators.

Practice 4: read the control chart

Control limits separate normal, common-cause variation from points that need investigation. Decide what each fictional result calls for.

Takeaway: Track a few measures that drive results, compare actual output with capacity, fix the bottleneck first, rank problems with a Pareto chart, and investigate only the points that fall outside the control limits.
