Week 5 Interactive: Lean Thinking in Healthcare

Week 5 objective: Apply process improvement methodologies to enhance patient care delivery (CLO 5).

Waste is anything the patient would not value

In Lean, waste is any activity that has no value for the customer: the customer would not pay for it, yet resources were spent on it. The seven wastes:

Defects
Work that fails requirements and must be redone.
Overproduction
Producing or acquiring before it is needed.
Transportation
Moving things without adding value.
Waiting
Idle time for people or work.
Inventory
Stock that ties up money without yet creating value.
Motion
Unnecessary movement of people or equipment.
Over-processing
Using more costly resources than needed.

Takeaway: Lean starts with the patient: define value from their perspective, map every step, and remove what adds no value, whether defects, overproduction, transportation, waiting, inventory, motion, or over-processing.
