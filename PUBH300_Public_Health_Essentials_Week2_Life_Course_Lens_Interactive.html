Week 2 Interactive: Life Course Lens

Week 2 focus: How social determinants shape health over a lifetime, the question behind Discussion 2 | Health Equity in Action.

Key ideas

Developmental perspective
Exposures during critical and sensitive periods of development, when the body is most adaptable, can shift health for life.
Structural perspective
Social identity and position (such as race, ethnicity, class, gender, disability, or immigrant status) shape who is exposed to risks and who has resources to cope.
Weathering
Cumulative, stress-related wear and tear from high-effort coping that speeds biological aging and the early onset of chronic disease.
Transgenerational transmission
Health disadvantage passed from one generation to the next through biological, family, and social pathways.

Takeaway: Disparities grow from when exposures happen (developmental), who is exposed and why (structural), and what is passed down (generations). Effective equity work reaches beyond the individual to families, communities, and policy.
