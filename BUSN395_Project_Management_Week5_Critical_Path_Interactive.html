Step 4: Duration or effort?

Three volunteers work side by side for 4 hours to set up the hall. Duration is how long the activity takes; effort is the total person-hours spent.



Takeaway: The critical path is the longest path through the network and sets the shortest time the project can take. Watch its tasks most closely: a slip there moves the finish date, and if you need to finish sooner, adding resources to the critical path works better than adding them elsewhere. Keep duration (calendar time) and effort (person-hours) separate when you estimate.
