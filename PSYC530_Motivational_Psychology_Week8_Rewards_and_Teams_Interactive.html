Week 8 Interactive: Motivation and Productivity

Week 8 focus: Identify factors linked to high and low producers, match reward systems to the work, and keep teams from losing effort, as Discussion 8 asks you to do.

Part 1: Higher or lower producers?

Research on job performance links each factor below to performance. Choose the direction.

Part 2: Match the reward system to the work

Each organization is fictional. Choose the incentive that fits best. A reward system gets the behavior it rewards, so match it to the work and the goal.

Part 3: Stop social loafing

A 14-person project group at Westbrook Health (fictional) is behind schedule, and a few members do most of the work. Select every action that research suggests will reduce social loafing, then select Check my plan.

Takeaway: Motivation is one input to productivity alongside ability, fair treatment, and stress. Reward systems produce the behavior they reward, so match them to the work and the real goal. In teams, keep groups small, make each contribution visible, and give people engaging work so effort is not lost.
