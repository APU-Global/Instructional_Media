Week 6 Interactive: Vision and Entrepreneurship

Week 6 objective: Recognize the five dimensions of entrepreneurial orientation and test whether a goal is SMART.

Five dimensions of entrepreneurial orientation

Autonomy
Freedom to develop an idea and see it through.
Competitive aggressiveness
Intensely and directly challenging rivals.
Innovativeness
Pursuing creativity and experimentation.
Proactiveness
Anticipating and acting on future needs.
Risk taking
Bold rather than cautious action.

Practice 1: Name the dimension

Each organization is fictional. Choose the dimension of entrepreneurial orientation each move shows most clearly.

Practice 2: Make the vision SMART

Each fictional goal is missing one SMART quality. Choose the one that is missing.

Takeaway: Visionaries act entrepreneurially, but strengths can be overdone: too much aggressiveness can cost future partnerships, and most entrepreneurs act only after planning and forecasting to reduce uncertainty. Turn a vision into specific, measurable, aggressive, realistic, and time-bound goals so others can help you reach it.

Concepts from Mastering Strategic Management (Saylor Academy, 2012), 2.1 Vision, Mission, and Goals and 2.4 Entrepreneurial Orientation. All organizations and goals are fictional.
