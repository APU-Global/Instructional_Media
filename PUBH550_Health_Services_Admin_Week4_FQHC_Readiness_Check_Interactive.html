FQHC basics (Medicare Benefit Policy Manual, Chapter 13)

WHO CAN ENROLL
Health Center Program grantees (section 330 grants), look-alikes (meet the section 330 definition without the grant), and tribal or urban Indian outpatient programs.
SERVICE AREA
Serve a designated medically underserved area (MUA) or medically underserved population (MUP).
SLIDING FEE SCALE
Offer a sliding fee scale to people with incomes below 200 percent of the federal poverty level.
GOVERNANCE AND QUALITY
A board whose majority receive their care at the center; comprehensive services; an ongoing quality assurance program.

Takeaway: FQHC status can bring Medicare payment under the FQHC prospective payment system and no Part B deductible for FQHC services, but it also brings real duties: an underserved service area, a sliding fee scale, a patient-majority board, comprehensive services, and quality assurance.
