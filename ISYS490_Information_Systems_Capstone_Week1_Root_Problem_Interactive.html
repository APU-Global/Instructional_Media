Week 1 Interactive: Problem Identification

Week 1 objective: Distinguish a root problem from a symptom and judge whether an information systems solution fits.

Is this the right problem, and the right solution?

A project title often names a solution (“replace the laptops”), not the need behind it. The Real World Application instructions say the right solution aligns with the organization’s strategic goals and delivers at least one of these:

Financial
Increased revenue or substantially reduced expenses.
Operational
Doing more with less: lower labor costs, higher efficiency or throughput.
Regulatory
Meeting requirements such as PCI or California’s mandatory lunch and break rules.

An information system “might be a good solution, but it is not the right solution” when the root cause is a process or people issue, or when the timing and scale do not fit. Your capstone project should be completable in 3 to 6 months.

Practice: six project requests

Each organization is fictional. For each request, choose the main impact and your verdict, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Before you propose a system, name the need it serves. The right capstone project has a financial, operational, or regulatory impact, aligns with strategy, and is the right size for the time you have.
