Week 7 objective: Identify how much authority each third-party step carries and recognize the five conflict-handling styles.

The third-party ladder

MediationA neutral helps the parties reach their own agreement; recommendations are not binding.
Impasses PanelRecommends procedures or assists with factfinding and recommendations.
Binding decisionPanel final action or an arbitrator’s award binds the parties.
