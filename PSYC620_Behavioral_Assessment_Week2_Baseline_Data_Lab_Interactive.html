Week 2 Interactive: Baseline Data Lab

Week 2 objective: Plan self-monitoring for a habit, read baseline data, and use an ABC chart to find the cues and consequences that keep the habit going.

Key terms

Self-monitoring
Measuring and recording your own behavior. Recording can itself change the behavior, which is called reactivity.
Baseline phase
Data are collected but no strategies are in place. Continue until a clear pattern emerges; treatment data are compared against it.
ABC chart
A structured diary of the Antecedent, the Behavior and its form, and the Consequence for each episode.
Habit reversal
Awareness training plus a competing response that makes the habit hard to do, such as a clenched fist instead of nail biting.
