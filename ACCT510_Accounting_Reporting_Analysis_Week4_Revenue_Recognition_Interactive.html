Week 4 Interactive: Revenue Recognition and Earnings Quality

Week 4 objective: Apply the transfer-of-control test to decide when revenue is recognized, and spot items that lower the quality of reported earnings.

The five-step model (IFRS 15)

  1. Identify the contract with a customer (approved, goods and price identified, commercial substance, collection probable).
  2. Identify the performance obligations.
  3. Determine the transaction price.
  4. Allocate the price to the performance obligations.
  5. Recognize revenue when (or as) each obligation is satisfied: when control transfers, at a point in time or over time.

Quality of earnings is the share of profit that comes from sustainable, ongoing core activities. Classify each item.

Takeaway: Revenue follows the transfer of control, not the invoice and not the cash. When you read an income statement, separate continuing operations from one-time items and check the notes for estimates and policy changes before trusting the bottom line.
