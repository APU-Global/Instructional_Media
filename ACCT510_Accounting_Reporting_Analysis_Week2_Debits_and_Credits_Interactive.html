Week 2 Interactive: Debits, Credits, and the Accounting Equation

Week 2 objective: Analyze a transaction, choose the debit and credit that record it, and tell temporary accounts from permanent ones.

The rules (the same for five hundred years)

Debits increase
  • Expenses and losses
  • Assets
  • Dividends paid
Credits increase
  • Liabilities
  • Capital stock
  • Revenues and gains
  • Retained earnings

Every effect has a cause: each debit is matched by an equal credit, so debits always equal credits and assets = liabilities + stockholders' equity stays in balance.

Practice 2: temporary or permanent?

At year-end, closing entries reset some accounts to zero and leave others alone. Classify each account.

Takeaway: Analyze first, then record. Ask what changed and what caused it: the change and its cause are the debit and the credit. Income statement accounts and dividends are temporary and close into retained earnings each year; balance sheet accounts are permanent.
