Week 6 Interactive: Chatbots in Consumer Messaging

Week 6 objective: Design chatbot responses that are honest about being automated and know when to hand a conversation to a person.

What a chatbot is for

Website chat answers quick questions right away, at the moment a visitor is deciding, at a much lower cost per contact than a call center. Chatbots can deliver information after hours, when no one is available, using a simple flowchart or machine learning. But customers dislike robotic responses and want to feel heard by a real person, and marketing messages should always be clearly labelled for what they are. A good bot says it is a bot, handles simple questions, and hands off to a person when needed.

Practice 1: write the bot's next line

Maple & Main Home Goods (fictional) has five stores and a website chatbot. For each moment, choose the best reply.

Practice 2: bot or person?

Decide whether each request is a good job for the chatbot or should go to a person.

Takeaway: Chatbots are tools, not people. Let them say what they are, answer the simple questions fast, and hand off to a person when the conversation needs one.
