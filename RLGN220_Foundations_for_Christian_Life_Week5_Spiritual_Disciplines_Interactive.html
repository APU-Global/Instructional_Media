Week 5 Interactive: Spiritual Disciplines

Week 5 objective: Identify Foster’s inward, outward, and corporate disciplines and recognize the six streams of Christian tradition.

Practices of discipline and grace

Richard Foster’s Celebration of Discipline describes twelve disciplines in three groups: Inward (meditation, prayer, fasting, study), Outward (simplicity, solitude, submission, service), and Corporate (confession, worship, guidance, celebration). The course module calls them “an invitation to a life full of wonder and joy.” The Six Streams module presents six traditions that “embody the core characteristics of Jesus’ life.”

Takeaway: The disciplines work in three directions: inward practices form the heart, outward practices shape how we live, and corporate practices connect us to the community. Each of the six streams highlights a different dimension of Jesus’ life, and growing in faith can mean appreciating the streams that feel least familiar.
