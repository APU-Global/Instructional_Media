Week 7 Interactive: Presenting to an Executive Audience

Week 7 objective: Apply audience analysis, persuasion, slide design, and delivery practices to an executive proposal.

Presenting to the C-Suite

Real World Application Part 4 is a recorded, 10 to 12 slide Strategic Proposal with speaker notes, an executive summary, Christian worldview and ethics, recommendations, and a call to action.

Audience
Know who decides and what they need to decide.
Persuasion
Convince with evidence, then make a clear call to action.
Delivery
You are the most important visual aid. Practice, and control your setting.

Practice: which move is stronger?

Choose the better option in each situation, then select Check my answers.

Slide headline checker

Type a slide headline or key message. Aim for one idea in ten words or fewer. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Target: 10 words or fewer.

Takeaway: Lead with the decision your audience must make, support it with evidence, keep slides to one idea, end with a call to action, and practice until the delivery feels natural.
