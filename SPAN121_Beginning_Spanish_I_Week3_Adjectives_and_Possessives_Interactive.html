Week 3 Interactive: Adjective Agreement and Possessives

Week 3 objective: Make adjectives and possessive adjectives agree in gender and number with the nouns they describe.

Agreement at a glance

-o adjectives: four forms
un amigo alto · una amiga alta · unos amigos altos · unas amigas altas
-e and most consonants: two forms
un amigo inteligente · unas amigas inteligentes · fiel / fieles
Nationality: four forms
español · española · españoles · españolas

Possessives agree with the thing owned, not the owner: mi/mis, tu/tus, su/sus, nuestro/nuestra/nuestros/nuestras.

Part 1: Choose the adjective

Pick the form that agrees with the noun.

Part 2: Choose the possessive

Pick the possessive adjective that fits.

Takeaway: Look at the noun first. Its gender and number decide the ending of every adjective and possessive that goes with it.
