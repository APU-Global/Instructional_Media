About the course

Examination of the causes and consequences of disease and the promotion of individual, family, and community health. Students study public health, social science, and behavioral science findings. Examples of topics covered are major chronic and infectious diseases, mental health, reproductive health, and drug and alcohol use and misuse. Coursework emphasizes identification of healthy personal behaviors that promote wellness and minimize unhealthy lifestyle. Along the way you will integrate faith and ethics into healthcare practices.

Projected total

Post by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight weekly discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like. (Week 8 runs earlier: post by Wednesday, discuss through Friday.) In Week 5 you also join the Part 3B peer review discussion, where you share your paper draft.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
