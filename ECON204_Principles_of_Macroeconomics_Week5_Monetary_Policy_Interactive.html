Week 5 Interactive: Monetary Policy Desk

Week 5 objective: Trace how monetary policy reaches the economy, choose the right policy for a situation, and explain the Fed's response to the Great Recession.

The toolkit

Expansionary (loose)

Lowers interest rates and raises the supply of credit to fight recession and unemployment.

Contractionary (tight)

Raises interest rates and reduces credit to hold down inflation.

Quantitative easing

When short-term rates are already near zero, the central bank buys long-term bonds and mortgage-backed securities to push credit into the economy.

    Takeaway: Monetary policy should be countercyclical: loosen in a recession, tighten when inflation threatens.
