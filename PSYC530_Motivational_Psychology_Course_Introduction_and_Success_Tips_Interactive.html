About the course

Psychological theories of motivation and their relevance to people, the workplace, and the discipline of psychology with specific application in the field of organizational psychology. Students explore a variety of factors that motivate individuals in organizational settings and strategies to address motivational challenges from a psychological perspective. Throughout the course you integrate a Christian worldview of motivation within an organizational setting.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points. Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
250–300 words
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the assigned readings and resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150–250 words each
Respond to a minimum of three peers in an exchange of ideas that continues through the second deadline.
SCHOLARLY SOURCES
Use scholarly resources beyond the class-provided readings. Posts made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Engagement (10), Critical Thinking (20), Contributions to Discussion (15), and Communication and Collaboration (5), for 50 points.
