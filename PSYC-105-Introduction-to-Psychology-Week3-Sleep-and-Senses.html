Why it vanishes

Where the optic nerve leaves the eye there are no photoreceptors. Your brain does not leave a hole. It fills in the gap using the surroundings. Switch the background to see the line complete itself.

Sensory adaptation in action

Photoreceptors tire when stimulated by one color for a long time. Stare at the dot without moving your eyes. When the colors disappear, look at the blank field and blink slowly.

What you should see afterward
red→cyan-ishgreen→magenta-ishblue→yellow-ish

Connect it to your assignment: adaptation saves attention for change, and your brain still builds a perception from tired signals.
