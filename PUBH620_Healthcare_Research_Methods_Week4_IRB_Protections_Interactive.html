Why IRBs exist

After the Nuremberg trials and studies such as Tuskegee, the U.S. National Research Act of 1974 required institutions that receive federal research support to establish Institutional Review Boards. An IRB reviews research in advance, and periodically afterward, to protect the rights and welfare of human subjects.
