About the course

Investigation of the historical development of the theoretical foundations of modern public administration practice. The course serves as an overview of the public administration profession within a democratic society, a description of the history and development of the field of public administration, and an introduction to ethical issues faced by public administrators.

Throughout the course you examine public policy and leadership from a Christian worldview perspective. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Projected total

Initial post due by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points (Week 8: initial post due by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
300 words minimum
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the assigned readings and resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
200 words minimum each
Post at least three substantive responses to colleagues. Offer further resources, questions of clarification, or context and insight; avoid simple posts of agreement.
SOURCES
2 APA-formatted sources are required per discussion. Several prompts ask you to research scholarly or authoritative literature or cite a case study.

Discussions are graded with the 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10), for 50 points.
