Week 4 Interactive: Stakeholder Analysis

Week 4 objective: Place stakeholder groups on an interest and influence map and recognize perception errors that can distort a stakeholder analysis.

Two separate questions

  • Interest: how much the group is affected by, and cares about, the change.
  • Influence: the group's relative power over the decision, from direct control or veto power to nuisance influence or none.

Being affected and having power do not always go together. Avoid the trap of rating every group high on both.

Practice 1: place each group

Tri-County Supply, a fictional distributor, is replacing paper timesheets, order tickets, and invoices with an integrated software system. Choose the best position for each group.

Practice 2: name the perception error

Your own perceptions can distort an analysis. Choose the error each analyst is making.

Takeaway: Rate interest and influence separately, explain each placement, and record interests, support level, engagement strategy, and frequency in the Stakeholder Analysis Template. Then test your assumptions by asking each group what it values; perception errors can make a guess feel like a fact.
