PPP check: what does a dollar really buy?

Exchange rates tell you what a currency trades for. Purchasing power parity (PPP) asks what it buys. Price the same basket of everyday goods in both countries and see how your pricing strategy should change.

Simplified on purpose: real PPP studies price hundreds of goods. The Economist's Big Mac Index is the famous one-item version. The numbers here are placeholders; use data for your own countries.
