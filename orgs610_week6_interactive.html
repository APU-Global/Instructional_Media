Technical Fix or Adaptive Challenge? Leading Teams

Some problems need an expert answer. Others need people to change beliefs and habits. Sort challenges, trace a team's development, and match leadership approaches to the situation.

Part 1 · Diagnose the challenge

A technical problem has a known solution that an expert can apply. An adaptive challenge requires changes in beliefs, habits, and relationships, so the people facing it must do the learning. Sort each challenge. (Illustrative scenarios.)

Part 2 · How teams develop

Teams move through stages of development. Select a stage on the chart to see what it looks like and what a leader can do. Clear roles, shared goals, and trust support performance, while dysfunction often comes from unclear expectations or unresolved conflict.
