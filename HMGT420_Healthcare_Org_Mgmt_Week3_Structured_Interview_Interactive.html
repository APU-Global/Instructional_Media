Week 3 Interactive: Structured Interviews and Fit

Week 3 objective: Write and sort structured interview questions, tell person-job fit from person-organization fit, and avoid questions and judgments that introduce bias.

The scenario

You are on the hiring team at fictional Cedar Valley Health, filling a Management Analyst II position. Your team has already written the job description, so the selection criteria are set. Now you need structured interview questions that every candidate will answer.

SituationalAsks what the candidate would do in a sample situation.
Behavior descriptionAsks what the candidate did in a past situation.
Allowed screening questionA direct, job-related check, such as work authorization or ability to perform the job's functions.
Do not askQuestions about national origin, age, marital status, religion, or disability are off limits.

Practice 1: sort the interview questions

Choose the type for each question, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: what kind of fit?

Each note comes from an interviewer's scorecard. Decide what it is evidence of.

Takeaway: Set the criteria first, then ask every candidate the same structured questions: situational ("what would you do?") and behavior description ("tell me about a time"). Judge person-job fit (skills meet demands) and person-organization fit (values match), never similarity to yourself.
