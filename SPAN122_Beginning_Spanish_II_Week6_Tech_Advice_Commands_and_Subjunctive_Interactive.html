Week 6 Interactive: Tú Commands and the Present Subjunctive

Week 6 objective: Give a friend advice with affirmative and negative tú commands, and use the subjunctive after quiero que and prefiero que.

Quick reference

Command-ar verbs-er / -ir verbs
Affirmative túHabla. Canta. Juega.Come. Escribe. Pide.
Negative túNo hables. No cantes. No juegues.No comas. No escribas. No pidas.
Irregular affirmativedecir: di · hacer: haz · ir: ve · poner: pon · salir: sal · ser: sé · tener: ten · venir: ven

Present subjunctive: hable, hables, hable, hablemos, habléis, hablen; coma, comas, and so on; vuelva, vuelvas, vuelva, volvamos, volváis, vuelvan; diga, digas, and so on. After a wish or request about someone else: Yo quiero que tú pagues la cuenta.

Practice 1: Dale un consejo (give advice)

A friend describes a technology problem. Choose the command that completes your advice.

Practice 2: Quiero que...

Choose the verb form that completes the sentence.

Takeaway: Affirmative tú commands look like the present (apaga, escribe), except eight short irregulars (di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven). Negative commands and the present subjunctive switch the vowel (no apagues; quiero que apagues).
