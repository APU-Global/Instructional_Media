Week 3 Interactive: Social Media in Healthcare

Week 3 objective: Distinguish personal from professional social media posts and recognize privacy, accuracy, and tone problems in healthcare marketing posts.

Personal vs. professional

A personal post can be casual and about your own life. A professional post speaks for the organization: it has a defined audience, accurate and useful content, a consistent voice across platforms, and nothing that identifies a patient. Both kinds leave a lasting digital footprint, so the rules of netiquette apply to both.
