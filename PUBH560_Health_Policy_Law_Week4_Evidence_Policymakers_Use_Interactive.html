Week 4 Interactive: Research and Data in Health Policy

Week 4 objective: Explain how research and data are used to develop and evaluate health policy.

What policymakers look for

Brownson et al. (2006) found that policymakers often look for data that show public support for an issue, show its priority over other issues, are locally relevant (for example, at the voting-district level), and tell a personal story of how people's lives are affected. Jacobs et al. (2012) add that the best type of evidence depends on the question being asked.
