Week 5 Interactive: Poll Question Fixer

Week 5 focus: Writing a closed-ended poll question that produces usable data, before you poll three people for Discussion 5.

Key ideas

Select each card to open it.

Activity 1: What is wrong with this poll?

Each fictional poll question has at most one problem. Name it, or mark it ready to use.

Activity 2: Pick the best response options

Choose the set of options that is both mutually exclusive and exhaustive.

0 / 4

Activity 3: Tally your poll

Enter how many of your respondents chose each option (fictional example: vegetable servings per day). The tally shows counts and shares as a text chart.

Takeaway: Ask one idea per question in clear, plain words. Make response options mutually exclusive and exhaustive, and pretest them. A poll of three friends is a pilot: it can suggest a question, not describe a whole population.
