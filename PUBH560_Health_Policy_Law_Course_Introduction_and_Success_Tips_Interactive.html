About the course

This course introduces the ways that public health law and policy impact society, examining the interrelated response from government, health professionals, the media, and other stakeholders. Response methods of public health officials in pandemic or epidemic emergencies will be discussed, including communicating health policies to the public. Legal and ethical matters will also be considered, along with how community safety and preparedness can be encouraged. These and other dilemmas will be examined through case studies and current events.

Welcome aboard: Health policy and law shape the conditions in which whole communities live. In this course you analyze real California and Los Angeles County policies through case studies, follow one public health policy from selection to critical analysis and faith-informed reflection, and weigh the legal, ethical, and economic sides of health policy.
