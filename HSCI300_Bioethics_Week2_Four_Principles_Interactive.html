Week 2 Interactive: Four Principles

Week 2 objective: Apply the principles of bioethics to real-world situations, the skill your Dax Cowart essay and Discussion 2 ask for.

The four principles

Respect for autonomy
Respecting a person's right to choose their own course of action and supporting independent decisions.
Beneficence
Doing good: not inflicting harm, preventing harm, removing harm, and promoting good.
Nonmaleficence
Refraining from unnecessary harm; any harm must be justified by the patient's good.
Justice
Fairness in who receives care and resources, regardless of social or economic status.
