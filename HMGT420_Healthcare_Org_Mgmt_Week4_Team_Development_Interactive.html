Week 4 Interactive: Team Development and the Team Contract

Week 4 objective: Recognize Tuckman's stages of group development in a healthcare team and use a team contract to make communication expectations explicit.

Tuckman's five stages

FormingPolite, guarded, looking to the leader for direction.
StormingTesting power and influence; disagreements surface.
NormingGround rules, norms, and procedures take shape.
PerformingShared vision; members manage how they work.
AdjourningThe group ends; feelings are mixed.

Practice 1: spot the stage

A fictional clinic team is struggling to share information. Identify the stage in each snapshot.

Practice 2: build the team contract

Teams that agree on ground rules, goals, and roles handle challenges better. Match each question to its section of the team contract.

Takeaway: Communication problems look different at each stage, so name the stage first. Then make expectations explicit in a team contract covering values and goals, roles, decision making, communication, and performance, and shift your role from directing to coaching to delegating as the team matures.
