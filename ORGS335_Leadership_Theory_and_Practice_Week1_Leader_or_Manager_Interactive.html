Week 1 Interactive: Leadership vs. Management

Week 1 objective: Distinguish leadership from management and formal from informal leadership, then apply the distinction to your own path.

Two related but distinct ideas

LeadershipEstablishing a direction and influencing others to follow. It pursues change and challenges the status quo: vision, innovating, inspiring, long-term, people, big ideas.
ManagementAdministering the many complex details of operations. It seeks control and stability: planning, organizing, budgeting, scheduling, instructing, details.

Most managers need both. The greatest success comes when strong leadership is paired with effective management. And leadership does not require a title: formal leaders hold positions of authority, while informal leaders influence others without one.

Practice 1: Leading or managing?

Each action comes from a fictional organization. Decide whether it is mainly leadership work or management work, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: Formal or informal leadership?

Decide whether each person is showing formal leadership (influence that comes with an assigned position) or informal leadership (influence without an official position).

Takeaway: Look at your own week. Which of your tasks set direction and inspire people, and which keep things organized and stable? Effective leaders do both, and you can start leading informally long before you hold a title.
