Week 7 Interactive: Decision Traps in a Crisis

Week 7 objective: Describe typical decision-making errors leaders make in a crisis and the safeguards that counter them.

Six traps

OverconfidenceOverestimating your ability to predict future events.
HindsightJudging a past decision as obviously wrong once you know how it turned out.
AnchoringRelying too heavily on a single piece of information.
FramingBeing swayed by how a problem is presented.
Escalation of commitmentContinuing a failing course because of what has already been invested.
GroupthinkGroup pressure that silences doubts and creates an illusion of unanimity.

Takeaway: In a crisis you cannot remove uncertainty, but you can name the traps: check your confidence, update your anchors, look past the frame, stop throwing good money after bad, and make room for dissent.
