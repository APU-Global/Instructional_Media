Week 2 Interactive: The Consumer Buying Process

Week 2 objective: Identify the five stages of the consumer decision-making process and how involvement changes the way people buy.

Key terms for Discussion 2

  • Need recognition: the buyer realizes a need or want, sometimes prompted by marketers
  • Search for information: the buyer gathers information from friends, family, reviews, independent sources, ads, and salespeople
  • Product evaluation: the buyer uses evaluative criteria to narrow a set of choices
  • Product choice and purchase: the buyer chooses what to buy and also where, how, and on what terms
  • Postpurchase use and evaluation: the buyer judges whether the product met expectations; if not, postpurchase dissonance (buyer's remorse) follows
  • Involvement: how personally important a purchase is and how much information the buyer needs; low, limited, or high

Takeaway: Buyers move through need recognition, search, evaluation, purchase, and postpurchase evaluation, and the effort they spend depends on involvement. A strong customer experience helps at every stage, including after the sale.
