Week 2 Interactive: Screening New Product Ideas

Week 2 objective: Apply the idea-screening questions to decide which product ideas deserve more investment.

What screening asks

The purpose of idea screening is to drop weak ideas early, before much money is lost. Screening asks whether the product adds value and meets a market need; whether it is financially feasible (it can sell enough, at a price, to make money); whether it is process feasible (the company can make and service it to quality specifications, again and again); and whether it is a strategic fit with the company's image and strategy. Feasibility checks address investment risk; fit addresses opportunity risk.

Practice 2: investment risk or opportunity risk?

When a company assesses feasibility, it is assessing investment risk. When it assesses fit, it is assessing opportunity risk. Classify each concern.

Takeaway: Screen early and with the same questions every time: value, financial feasibility, process feasibility, and strategic fit. The sooner a weak idea is dropped, the less is lost, and the more resources remain for the idea most likely to succeed.
