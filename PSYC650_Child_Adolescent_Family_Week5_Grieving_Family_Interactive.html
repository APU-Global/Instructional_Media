Week 5 Interactive: Supporting a Grieving Family

Week 5 focus: Choose family intervention strategies and explain why they work, as Discussion 5 asks for a recently widowed father and his three children.

Part 1: The Ortiz family

Daniel Ortiz (fictional) lost his wife four months ago. He is raising Lily (7), Ana (9), and Mateo (14). For each finding, choose the response the research supports best.

Part 2: Structural or strategic?

Structural–strategic family therapy combines two systemic approaches. Sort each technique.

Part 3: Weigh the evidence

Before you recommend an approach, check how strong its support is.

Takeaway: Treat the family as a system. Supporting the surviving parent strengthens the whole family; separate child and adolescent groups plus joint family sessions fit a family like the Ortizes. Watch quiet children closely, reassess at the end, and be honest about how strong each approach’s evidence is.

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 16(7), 1255 (PMC6479931). The Ortiz family is fictional.
