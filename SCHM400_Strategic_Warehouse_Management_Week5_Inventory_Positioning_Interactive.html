Week 5 Interactive: Inventory Positioning

Objective: Decide where inventory should be positioned in a multi-echelon e-commerce network and explain the cost and service trade-offs.

Three echelons

Local warehouseServes one province or metro area. Usually under 24 hours; lowest delivery cost.
Regional warehouseServes several provinces. Two to three days.
Supplier warehouseCan serve the whole country. Four to seven days; highest delivery cost.

Part 1: Where should the stock sit?

Northgate Goods is a fictional online seller that can rent space in an open warehouse platform. Use the study's findings to choose where each product's inventory should be positioned.

Part 2: Check the trade-offs

Choose the best answer.

Takeaway: Inventory positioning trades rent and holding cost against delivery time. High-margin, high-demand, and time-sensitive products belong close to customers; low-margin, slow, patient-demand items can stay upstream. Improper positioning shows up as slow deliveries, lost sales, and unhappy customers.
