About the course

Exploration of the basic legal and ethical concepts and challenges facing public administrators. Students identify principles that might guide legal and ethical choices and show the practical impacts of these principles. Students consider the tensions among ethics, societal influences, and faith including examination of contemporary issues in public administration decision making.

Each week pairs a chapter of the course textbook with articles, Harvard Business Publishing cases, and a talk or video on a real public-sector dilemma. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Projected total

Eight discussions, 50 points each

Each week’s discussion has a Review step (the assigned reading or case), a Respond prompt, and a Discuss step with your colleagues. Post your initial response by the first deadline and keep the conversation going through the second deadline in the course calendar. Posts made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

INITIAL POST
Answer every prompt
Address all of the week’s Respond questions, drawing on the assigned reading. Several weeks ask you to find and link an article or video.
DISCUSS
At least two substantive responses
Suggest further resources, ask questions of clarification, or add context and insight. Avoid simple posts of agreement; if you agree, explain why and further the conversation.
WRITING
Check spelling and grammar before you post, and apply APA formatting. Discussions are checked for plagiarism and AI-generated content.

Discussions are graded with the 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10), for 50 points.

Post readiness check

Before you submit a discussion post, check each statement that is true of your draft. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
