Week 3 Interactive: Email and SMS Messaging

Week 3 objective: Judge whether email and text messages respect the customer's permission, deliver value, and fit the channel.

What earns a place in the inbox

Permission: spam is commercial email the recipient never agreed to receive. Permission must be explicit; a double opt-in confirms it with a link, every commercial email needs an unsubscribe link, and texts must honor STOP (unsubscribe) and HELP (support). Value: every message should give the customer something useful; people who receive value are more willing to give something back. Fit: phones are personal and always on, so timing matters, and an email's content, tone, and design should match the brand's other channels.

Practice 1: what is going right or wrong?

Harbor Lane Coffee (fictional) is building its email and text program. Choose the best description of each practice, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: name the message type

Direct marketing uses promotional emails (an immediate goal such as a purchase or download), newsletters (a long-term relationship), and transactional messages (usually automated: payments, confirmations, reminders). Classify each Harbor Lane message.

Takeaway: An inbox is earned, not taken. Ask first, bring value every time, fit the channel and the moment, and always make it easy to leave.
