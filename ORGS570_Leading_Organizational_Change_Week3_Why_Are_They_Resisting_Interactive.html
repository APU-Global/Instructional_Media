Week 3 Interactive: Why Are They Resisting?

Week 3 objective: Diagnose why people resist change and choose a response, as Discussion 3 asks you to identify difficulties in the change process and strategies to offset them.

Part 1: Why is this person resisting?

A fictional distribution company is replacing its paper order system with a new software platform. Name the main reason behind each comment.

Part 2: Where on the range of reactions?

Reactions to change run from resistance to enthusiasm. Place each fictional employee's behavior.

Part 3: Which leader response fits?

Choose the response that best addresses each situation.

Takeaway: Resistance has reasons. Diagnose before you respond, treat objections as feedback, and inform, support, and involve people. “Know this, my beloved brothers: let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger…” (James 1:19, ESV).
