Week 2 Interactive: Why We Need Employment Laws

Week 2 objective: Analyze the basic laws and regulations within healthcare human resource administration (CLO 3).

Three ideas to apply

Direct discrimination
A decision openly based on a protected characteristic, such as age, sex, religion, race, or disability.
Indirect (systemic) discrimination
A rule that looks neutral but falls harder on a protected group and is not tied to the job.
Job-related requirement
Connected to doing the job safely, adopted honestly and in good faith, and accommodation is not possible without undue hardship.

The three safety rights

Safety laws give employers broad power to direct work, and give workers three rights in return: the right to know about hazards, the right to participate in health and safety activities, and the right to refuse unsafe work.

Practice 2: which safety right is at work?

Choose the right each worker is using.

Takeaway: Employment laws set a floor for fair, safe treatment. Watch for rules that look neutral but are not tied to the job, and make sure every worker knows about hazards, can take part in safety, and can refuse unsafe work.
