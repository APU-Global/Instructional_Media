Week 7 Interactive: From Plan to Action

Week 7 focus: write the Recommendations section of your final Capstone with defined goals, action steps, timelines, roles, and measurable indicators (CLO 2, CLO 4).

SMART goals and three kinds of measures

Goals turn a vision into daily guidance. Measures tell you whether a change is an improvement.

SMART
Specific (explicit, not vague), Measurable (can be quantified), Achievable (challenging but attainable), Realistic (feasible), Time-bound (has a deadline).
Outcome, process, balancing
Outcome measures show the result for patients, staff, and others. Process measures show whether actions happen as planned and on schedule. Balancing measures watch for problems the change causes elsewhere.

Practice 3: build the action plan

An action plan spells out the steps, who has lead responsibility, and the milestones that show progress. Choose what each line provides.

Takeaway: Prioritize a few achievable strategies, write each goal so it is specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, and time-bound, choose outcome, process, and balancing measures, and give every step an owner and a dated milestone. A plan like that is a living document, not a report that sits on a shelf.
