Week 6 Interactive: Grant Stewardship Check

Week 6 objective: Follow a grant from application to reporting, write measurable outcomes, and spot stewardship risks in managing grant funds.

Practice 1: Is the outcome measurable?

Your Grant Proposal needs at least two measurable outcomes. Decide whether each fictional outcome is measurable as written.

Practice 2: Good stewardship or risk?

Each situation is fictional. Decide whether it shows good stewardship or creates a risk.

Takeaway: Funders want to see every dollar tied to a goal and every goal tied to evidence. Write outcomes with a number, a target, and a date, connect the budget narrative to your objectives, plan performance reporting from day one, and remember that approved commitments, such as a match, become binding.

Content from National Institute of Justice, Guidance for Applicants and Awardees (nij.ojp.gov); Bureau of Justice Assistance, Performance Measures: Overview (bja.ojp.gov); and the CRJU 430 Grant Proposal assignment. Outcomes and situations are fictional.
