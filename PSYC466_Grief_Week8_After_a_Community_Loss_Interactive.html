Week 8 Interactive: Responding to Community Loss

Week 8 objective: Apply five first-response principles and recognize a social crisis after a community loss.

Five principles for the first days

Open RN summarizes SAMHSA's tips for supporting survivors of a disaster:

  • Promote safety: make sure basic needs are met and explain simply how to get them.
  • Promote calm: listen with empathy, even to anger, and offer objective information.
  • Promote connectedness: help people reach family and loved ones; keep families together.
  • Promote self-efficacy: suggest ways people can help themselves and guide them to resources.
  • Promote help and hope: know the available services, direct people to them, and update people on what is being done.

Practice 1: Which principle?

A fictional wildfire has destroyed much of a small town. Choose the principle each helper's action shows best.

Practice 2: What kind of crisis?

Open RN sorts crises into three categories. Choose the category for each event.

Takeaway: Community loss strikes many people at once and in many ways. Begin with safety, calm, connection, self-efficacy, and hope, and remember that recovery takes months, not days.
