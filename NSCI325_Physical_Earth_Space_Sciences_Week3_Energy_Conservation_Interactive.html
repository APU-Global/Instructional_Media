Week 3 Interactive: Energy Conservation

Week 3 objective (Discussion 3): Explain why energy is always conserved yet not always available, and compare the benefits and costs of renewable and nonrenewable resources.

1. Follow the energy

Energy can never be consumed; it only changes form. Choose a device to see how 100 units of stored energy split into useful work and heat.


2. Renewable or nonrenewable?

Renewable sources can be replenished within human lifespans. Nonrenewable sources are finite and cannot be replenished on a human timescale. Sort each source, then select Check my sorting.

3. Weigh the trade-offs

Each town and person is fictional. Choose the answer the readings support, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: The total amount of energy never changes, but every conversion spreads some of it out as heat that can no longer do useful work. That is why resources run out, why efficiency matters, and why every energy source, renewable or not, has costs to weigh.

Facts from Averill & Eldredge, General Chemistry: Principles, Patterns, and Applications v1.0 (Saylor Academy, 2012), Sections 5.5, 18.2, and 18.3; Ball, Introductory Chemistry v1.0 (Saylor Academy, 2012), Section 7.1; and Fisher (Ed.), Environmental Biology (Lumen Learning), Sections 11.1–11.3. Scenarios are fictional.
