Week 3 Interactive: Description Comes First

Week 3 objective: Distinguish the stages of D2PA, classify descriptive evidence by level of culture, and use the four-fifths rule as a quick numeric check.

The D2PA approach

This course builds your DEIB Strategic Plan with four stages, modeled on a physician caring for a patient:

  • Description: examine the organization and describe its DEI and belonging conditions from evidence (Week 3).
  • Diagnosis: name the primary DEI and belonging issue and its root cause (Week 4).
  • Prescription: list as many possible treatments as you can (Week 5).
  • Application: choose the best treatment and express it as SMART goals (Week 6).

Good description looks at all three levels of culture: visible artifacts (physical environment, employee interactions, policies, reward systems), shared values (principles, standards, and goals), and taken-for-granted assumptions.
