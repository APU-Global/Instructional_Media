Week 2 Interactive: Culturally Competent Leadership

Week 2 objective: Recognize cultural value dimensions and the parts of cultural intelligence, and practice the active listening that inclusive leadership requires.

Two tools for the culturally competent leader

Cultural intelligence (CI) is the ability to successfully adapt to unfamiliar cultural settings. Its four parts are the ABCs:

  • Acquire knowledge of how cultures are created, interpreted, and shared.
  • Build strategic thinking: plan how to use that knowledge in a new setting.
  • Contemplate your motivation: step back, suspend judgments, reflect on assumptions, and listen.
  • Do: adapt your behavior and perform in the new setting.

Five value dimensions (drawn from Hofstede) describe where cultures tend to fall. Each is a continuum:

  • Identity: group needs and harmony ↔ individual needs and achievement.
  • Power: egalitarian ↔ hierarchical.
  • Gender: nurture, care, and quality of life ↔ ambition, competition, and achievement (cultural values, not whether someone is a man or a woman).
  • Uncertainty: comfortable with ambiguity ↔ need for rules and structure.
  • Time: relationship-oriented ↔ task-oriented.
