Week 8 Interactive: Burnout, Secondary Traumatic Stress, and Self-Care

Week 8 objective: Distinguish the two components of compassion fatigue and sort risk factors from protective practices.

Key terms

Compassion fatigue (CF) is "the extreme stress and burnout from helping others." Current models describe two components:

  • Burnout: builds gradually from chronic work stress; exhaustion, less enjoyment of work, arguing with coworkers, loss of morale.
  • Secondary traumatic stress (STS): comes on suddenly from indirect exposure to others' trauma; intrusive thoughts or nightmares, avoiding reminders, numbness, hypervigilance.

Self-care is a primary protective factor, and prevention is "a first line of defense."
