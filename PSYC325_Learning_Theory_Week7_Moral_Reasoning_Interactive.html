Week 7 Interactive: Moral Reasoning Levels

Week 7 objective: Classify moral reasoning using Kohlberg's levels and choose teaching strategies for spiritual, moral, and ethical learning.

Kohlberg's three levels

  • Preconventional: self-interest; avoid punishment and seek reward.
  • Conventional: care about others' approval and uphold social norms and laws.
  • Postconventional: self-chosen, universal ethical principles such as justice, dignity, and equality.

Kohlberg classified the reason a person gives, not whether they say yes or no. People may answer the same way for very different reasons.

Practice 1: classify the reasoning

A fictional dilemma modeled on Kohlberg's: Sam's neighbor will die without a medicine the pharmacist sells for twice what Sam can pay, and the pharmacist refuses a payment plan. Sam considers taking it. Each person below gives a reason. Choose the level.

Practice 2: match the teaching strategy

Chapter 7 names several ways to foster spiritual, moral, and ethical learning. Which does each fictional classroom activity mainly use?

Takeaway: When you design dilemmas, put two values in tension, ask for reasons, and set ground rules for respectful dialogue. Remember the model's limits: people do not always reason at one level, and culture and an ethic of care also shape moral thinking.
