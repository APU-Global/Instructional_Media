Week 8 Interactive: Build a Data Story

Week 8 objective: Communicate a data-driven, customer-centered strategy persuasively to stakeholders (CLOs 1 and 3).

Three elements of a data story

Data
The few numbers your audience needs to make its decision, reported in a form it will actually use.
Visuals
Charts that make the pattern easy to see. Each one has a clear purpose; keep them simple and labeled.
Narrative
You, the speaker, connect the numbers to the goal: why it matters and what you recommend.

Practice 1: data, visual, or narrative?

A student is preparing a narrated board presentation for a customer-centered strategy. Label each choice.

Practice 2: pick the chart

Choose the chart that shows each message most clearly.

Practice 3: Ten words or fewer

A common rule of thumb for slides is no more than ten words, in key words rather than full sentences. Draft a slide headline for your recommendation and check its length. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Example of a tighter version: “Shift budget to retain high-value customers” (6 words).

Takeaway: A data story is not a data dump. Choose the numbers your audience needs, show the pattern with a simple labeled visual, and let your narrative explain why it matters and what you recommend. Use the three elements in Discussion 8 as you explain how you built a persuasive strategy.
