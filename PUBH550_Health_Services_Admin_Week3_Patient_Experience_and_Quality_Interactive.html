Week 3 Interactive: Patient Experience and Quality

Week 3 objective: Tell patient experience from patient satisfaction, name the dimensions of quality care, and plan a small test of change, as Discussion 3 and Case Study 2's follow-up questions ask.

Key ideas

PATIENT SATISFACTION
Whether a patient's expectations were met.
PATIENT EXPERIENCE
Whether, or how often, specific aspects of care happened, such as clear communication.
QUALITY OF CARE (WHO)
Effective, safe, people-centred, timely, equitable, integrated, and efficient.
MODEL FOR IMPROVEMENT
What are we trying to accomplish? How will we know that change is an improvement? What change can we make that will result in an improvement? Then test with Plan-Do-Study-Act.

Part 1: Experience or satisfaction?

Each item is a survey question from a fictional clinic. Does it measure patient experience or patient satisfaction?

Part 2: Which dimension of quality?

Choose the WHO quality dimension each fictional problem most clearly fails.

Part 3: Plan a test of change

A fictional clinic wants more patients to return for follow-up visits. Match each statement to the step of the Model for Improvement or PDSA cycle it belongs to.

Takeaway: Marketing promises value; quality delivers it. Measure what actually happens to patients, check every dimension of quality, and improve in small, tested cycles. "Let each of you look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others" (Philippians 2:4, ESV).
