Week 4 Interactive: Biopsychosocial Sorter

Week 4 objective: Analyze how biological, psychological, and social factors interact to shape a person's addiction.

Why this matters

The biopsychosocial model sees each person as a whole person, with a history that includes friends, family, jobs, living environments, beliefs, values, and life experiences. Recovery requires long-term solutions that address medical, psychological, and social concerns.
