Week 7 Interactive: Universal Design for Learning

Week 7 objective: Match teaching moves to the three principles of the CAST UDL Guidelines you will use in Part 1 | Literacy Foundational Concepts.

Three principles: design multiple means of...

The goal of UDL is learner agency that is purposeful & reflective, resourceful & authentic, strategic & action-oriented. No single means works best for every learner, so teachers design options under each principle.

Engagement
Design options for welcoming interests & identities, sustaining effort & persistence, and emotional capacity.
Representation
Design options for perception, language & symbols, and building knowledge.
Action & expression
Design options for interaction, expression & communication, and strategy development.

Practice

Each move comes from a fictional lesson. Choose the principle it serves most directly, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: There is not one means of engagement, representation, or action and expression that is optimal for every learner. Build options for all three into the lesson from the start, so every student can work toward the goal of UDL: learner agency.

Principles, guidelines, and considerations from CAST (2024), Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0, udlguidelines.cast.org. Lessons are fictional.
