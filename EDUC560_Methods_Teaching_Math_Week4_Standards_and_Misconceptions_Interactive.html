Week 4 Interactive: Building Your Teacher's Toolkit

Week 4 objective: Read a California Common Core State Standard by its code and recognize common misconceptions for a grade level.

How to read a standard

In kindergarten through grade 8, the standards are organized by grade, then domain, cluster, and standard. In 3.NBT.2, 3 is the grade, NBT is the domain (Number and Operations in Base Ten), and 2 is the standard number. The standards do not dictate curriculum or pedagogy, and standards that begin with "understand" are especially good places to connect content with the mathematical practices.
