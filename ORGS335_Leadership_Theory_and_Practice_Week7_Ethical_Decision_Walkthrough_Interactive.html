Week 7 Interactive: The Ethical Leader

Week 7 objective: Apply a five-step ethical decision-making process to a workplace dilemma and identify how leaders set an ethical tone.

The scenario (fictional)

Elena manages a product team at Northwind Medical Devices. A home blood-pressure monitor is already two weeks behind schedule, and the client will cancel the deal if it does not ship this Friday. Late Thursday, Elena sees test results showing that a small share of units give inaccurate readings. Fixing the problem would add three weeks. Her boss says, "It's a small error. Ship it."

Walk the five steps

Choose the best answer at each step. Feedback appears below the choices; when you find the best answer, Next step unlocks.

Step 1 of 6

Takeaway: Pressure to meet deadlines is where most ethical lapses begin. Assess, see every stakeholder, weigh consequences, duties, and integrity, decide and speak up, then monitor. As a leader, model the behavior you want and make it safe for people to raise problems.
