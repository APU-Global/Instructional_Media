Week 4 Interactive: Attachment Styles and Emotional Intelligence

Week 4 objective: Identify the four attachment styles from children's behavior in the strange situation and connect attachment to emotional intelligence.

The strange situation

In Mary Ainsworth's strange situation, a parent and a stranger move in and out of a room while a young child's behavior is recorded. Children are grouped into four attachment styles:

  • Secure: explores freely while the parent is present, engages with the stranger, may be upset at separation, happy at reunion.
  • Ambivalent (insecure-resistant): wary, clings rather than explores, extremely distressed at separation, ambivalent at reunion.
  • Avoidant (insecure-avoidant): avoids or ignores the parent, little emotion at departure or return, treats the stranger much like the parent.
  • Disorganized: no consistent way of coping, such as crying at separation but avoiding the parent, or approaching and then freezing.
