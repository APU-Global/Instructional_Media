Week 6 Interactive: Genetic Tests and Health Data

Week 6 objective: Explain what genetic tests can and cannot tell us, and read population health data with care, for Discussion 6 and Bioethics of Data Part 2.

Practice 1: which kind of test is this?

Each person is fictional. Match the situation to the type of genetic test, then select Check my answers.

Remember the limits

  • A test often cannot say whether, or how severely, a person will develop a condition.
  • Large tests can turn up secondary findings unrelated to why the test was ordered.
  • Results can bring relief, or anxiety, guilt, and family tension, because relatives share DNA.
  • Genetic data raise questions of privacy, consent for future use, and discrimination.

Practice 2: sort the Social Vulnerability Index variables

The CDC Social Vulnerability Index (SVI) ranks communities on 15 social factors grouped into four themes. You will compare SVI scores with COVID-19 data in Bioethics of Data Part 2. Sort each variable into its theme.

Takeaway: Genetic and population data can guide care and direct help to vulnerable communities, but they give probabilities, not certainties. Ask who decides how the data are used, who might be stigmatized, and how consent and privacy are protected.
