Week 7 Interactive: Holistic Discipleship

Week 7 objective: Explain holistic discipleship, its four movements, and how the university’s Distinctives apply it to life and learning.

Discipleship in all of life

Brant Himes defines discipleship as “the response to the call to follow-after Jesus Christ in all aspects of human life and endeavor, from the inner personal disciplines to the deliberate shaping of culture—in the very midst of the world.” The Week 7 overview warns against compartmentalization: making faith solely internal and personal, limited to one morning a week.

Takeaway: Discipleship is not a check-list: it is “our entire life bound up in Jesus Christ.” Spiritual disciplines cultivate belief-obedience, and whole-life discipleship carries that faith into work, family, learning, and the common good, loving God with all our heart, soul, strength, and mind, and our neighbor as ourselves (Luke 10:27).
