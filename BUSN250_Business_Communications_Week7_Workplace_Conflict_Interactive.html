Week 7 Interactive: Conflict Conversation Coach

Week 7 objective: Apply Chapter 16's conflict management strategies to choose words that address a workplace conflict without attacking people, as you will in Discussion 7.

Conflict strategies from Chapter 16

  • Avoidance buys time, but the conflict will remain and resurface.
  • Supportive communication focuses on the points, not personalities; defensive communication is marked by control, evaluation, and judgment.
  • Face-saving language separates the message from the messenger.
  • Empathetic listening attends to literal and implied meanings.
  • Gunnysacking is carrying old, unresolved grievances into new situations.
  • Manage your emotions: wait until strong feelings pass before you respond.

Takeaway: Conflict is normal and can lead to growth. Raise issues early, keep the focus on the points rather than personalities, save face for others, listen for what is implied, and wait until strong emotions pass before you respond.
