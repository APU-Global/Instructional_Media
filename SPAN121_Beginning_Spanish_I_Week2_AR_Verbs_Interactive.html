Week 2 Interactive: Subject Pronouns and -ar Verbs

Week 2 objective: Choose the right subject pronoun and the right present-tense ending for regular -ar verbs, and form negatives and questions.

The pattern

Drop -ar from the infinitive and add the ending that matches the subject. Example: hablar (to speak).

Present tense of hablar
SingularPlural
yo hablonosotros/nosotras hablamos
tú hablasvosotros/vosotras habláis (Spain)
él / ella / usted hablaellos / ellas / ustedes hablan

Put no before the verb to make it negative. Spanish needs no helper verb like "do."

Choose the correct Spanish sentence.

Takeaway: Name the doer, drop -ar, add -o, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an. Put no before the verb for a negative, and open every question with ¿.
