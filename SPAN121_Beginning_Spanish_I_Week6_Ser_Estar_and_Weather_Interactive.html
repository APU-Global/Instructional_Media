Week 6 Interactive: Weather and the Ways to Say “To Be”

Week 6 objective: Choose ser, estar, hace, hay, or tener to describe weather, places, people, and conditions.

Five ways to say "to be"

ser: identity, origin, time, basic traits
Soy de Nueva York. Son las dos.
estar: location, health, conditions, actions in progress
Quito está en Ecuador. Está lloviendo.
hace: weather
Hace calor. Hace viento.
hay: something exists
Hay niebla. Hay humedad.
tener: how a person feels
Tengo frío. Tiene calor.

Part 2: What is the weather?

Choose the Spanish that matches each fictional forecast.

Takeaway: Weather usually takes hace; people have cold or heat (tener); ser says what something is, estar where or how it is, and hay that it exists.
