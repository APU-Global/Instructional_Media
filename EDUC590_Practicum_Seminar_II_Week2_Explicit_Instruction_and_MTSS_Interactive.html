Week 2 Interactive: Explicit Instruction and MTSS

Week 2 objective: Recognize direct, systematic, explicit teaching of foundational reading skills and the level of support each MTSS tier provides.

Explicit instruction in brief

The CalTPA Literacy Cycle Guide defines explicit literacy instruction: "All concepts are directly and explicitly taught to students with continuous student–teacher interaction. Learning is never assumed," with practice under teacher guidance and feedback. Systematic instruction follows the logical order of language, from the easiest concepts to the more difficult. Rubric 2.2 asks for a direct, systematic, and explicit approach to the selected foundational reading skill(s). TPE 7 names the MTSS tiers: Tier 1 best first instruction, Tier 2 targeted, supplemental instruction, and Tier 3 referrals for intensive intervention.
