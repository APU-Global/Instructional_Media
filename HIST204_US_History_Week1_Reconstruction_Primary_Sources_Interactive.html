Week 1 Interactive: Primary Sources from Reconstruction

Week 1 objective: Tell primary sources from secondary sources, then ask a document who made it, for whom, why, and what it can (and cannot) prove. You will use these skills in Discussion 1.

Primary or secondary?

A primary source was created during the period you are studying by people who lived through it: letters, speeches, laws, posters, photographs, newspaper reports. A secondary source was created later to explain or interpret the past, like a textbook chapter or a historian's podcast.
