Week 6 Interactive: The Balanced Scorecard

Week 6 objective: Place performance measures in the four areas of the Balanced Scorecard and distinguish feedforward, concurrent, and feedback controls, as Discussion 6 and Case Study Response #5 ask.

The four areas

FinancialThe most traditional indicators, such as operating costs and return on investment.
CustomerCustomer satisfaction and retention.
InternalProduction and innovation, including indicators of future productivity.
Learning and growthEmployee satisfaction and retention, and information system performance.

Takeaway: A balanced scorecard looks beyond last quarter's profit. Customer, internal, and learning and growth measures, linked to strategy, signal where financial results are heading, and a mix of feedforward, concurrent, and feedback controls catches problems at different points.
