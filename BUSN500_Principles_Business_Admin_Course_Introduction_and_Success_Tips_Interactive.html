Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points (in Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
300 words minimum
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, including the Personal Reflection when there is one.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150 words minimum each
Post at least two substantive responses to colleagues. Avoid simple agreement; further the conversation.
SCHOLARLY SOURCES
Cite 1–2 scholarly, peer-reviewed sources in APA format. Textbooks, Wikipedia, Investopedia, and blogs do not count as scholarly.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Discussion Responses (16), Integrative Communication (16), and Written Communication (8), for 40 points. Posts and responses made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

Welcome aboard: Managers get things done effectively and efficiently with and through other people. In BUSN 500 you practice the four management functions on real cases, from SAS Institute to the organization you choose for your Business Evaluation, and you test each decision against an ethical, Christian worldview. Choose your organization early, find scholarly sources each week, and ask questions in Ask the Prof whenever you need help.
