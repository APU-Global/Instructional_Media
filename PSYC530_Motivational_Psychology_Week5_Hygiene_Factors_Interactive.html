Week 5 Interactive: Motivators, Demotivators, and Dissatisfiers

Week 5 focus: Separate Herzberg’s hygiene factors from motivators, then recommend fixes for a demotivating workplace, as Discussion 5 and Part 2 of the Motivational Analysis and Proposal ask you to do.

Part 1: Hygiene factor or motivator?

Hygiene factors are part of the context of the job: poor ones cause dissatisfaction, good ones are taken for granted. Motivators are intrinsic to the job and encourage people to try harder. Sort each factor.

Part 2: Consultant’s desk at Riverbend Distribution

Riverbend Distribution (fictional, about 200 warehouse and office employees) has rising absences and complaints. For each finding from your interviews, choose the fix that research supports best.

Part 3: After the fixes

Riverbend fixes every problem above. Complaints fall, but effort stays flat. What should leaders add next?

Takeaway: Hygiene factors are “things that we miss when they are absent but take for granted if they are present.” Fixing unclear roles, broken promises, unfair procedures, and poor conditions lowers dissatisfaction and the risk of burnout, but lasting motivation also needs motivators: achievement, recognition, interesting work, responsibility, and growth.
