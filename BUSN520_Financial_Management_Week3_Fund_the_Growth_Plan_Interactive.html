Week 3 Interactive: Fund the Growth Plan

Week 3 focus: Corporate financial planning connects a growth strategy to the money needed to carry it out. Use the External Funds Needed (EFN) formula to see how much outside financing a sales plan requires, then practice the planning ideas behind it.

1. EFN explorer: Coastline Outfitters (fictional)

EFN = (A0/S0)·ΔS − (AP0/S0)·ΔS − M·S1·RR. Assets (A0) and payables (AP0) are assumed to stay the same percentage of sales. Change the plan and watch the funding gap.

2. Practice: forecast and fund

A fictional firm had sales of $500,000, assets of $400,000, and payables of $50,000. It earns a 6% net margin and retains half of its profit. Sales are planned to rise to $600,000. Enter dollar answers (within $1).

3. The people side of planning

Takeaway: A financial plan turns strategy into numbers: sales, expense, and capital budgets roll up into budgeted statements, and growth needs assets that must be financed. EFN shows the gap; payables and retained earnings fill part of it, and the rest must come from lenders or investors. Bring these questions to your finance-executive interview.
