Week 6 Interactive: Decision-Making Traps

Week 6 objective: Recognize decision-making traps that can distort data-driven and AI-assisted decisions.

Five traps

Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky found that decision makers are influenced by five traps. Overconfidence: overestimating your ability to predict future events. Hindsight: believing, after the fact, that an outcome was obvious, which matters most when judging someone else's decision. Anchoring: relying too heavily on a single piece of information. Framing: being swayed by how a problem is presented. Escalation of commitment: continuing a failing course of action because of what has already been invested.

Practice

Each startup and manager is fictional. Choose the trap each scenario shows, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Data and AI tools can widen the search for alternatives, but they do not remove human bias. Set your decision criteria and turning-back points in advance, look for disconfirming evidence, and ask how the problem was framed before you decide.
