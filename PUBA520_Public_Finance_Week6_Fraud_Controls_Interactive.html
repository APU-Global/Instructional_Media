Fiduciary risk and its defenses

Fiduciary risk is "the risk of public money being stolen, used for purposes other than those intended, or used corruptly." Public financial management answers it in layers:

INTERNAL CONTROLS
Payment authorization, managing against budgets, reconciliations: designed to minimize the risk of loss.
INTERNAL AUDIT
The oversight system management establishes to review how controls work.
EXTERNAL AUDIT
An independent auditor tests the procedures that prevent fraud and abuse and gives an opinion on whether the statements are fair.
LEGISLATIVE OVERSIGHT
A public accounts or audit committee reviews financial and audit reports and can use them to identify issues.

Part 3: Read the audit opinion

Takeaway: Fraud can misstate the statements, take assets, or corrupt decisions. No single control stops all three: internal controls, internal audit, external audit, and legislative oversight work together, and the mere existence of that system encourages good practice.
