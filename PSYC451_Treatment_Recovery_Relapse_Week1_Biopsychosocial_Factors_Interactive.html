Week 1 Interactive: The Three Cs and the Biopsychosocial Threads

Objective: Recognize addictive use and identify the biological, psychological, and social factors that shape a person’s path through addiction.

Two ways to look at addiction

Florin and Trytek identify addictive use by three Cs:

  • Compulsion: an overwhelming urge to use, acted on.
  • Loss of control: the person can no longer predict how much they will use or what will happen.
  • Consequences: use continues despite financial, legal, social, interpersonal, emotional, or spiritual harm.

The causes are layered. Biological factors include heredity and the brain’s reward system; psychological factors include expectancies, reinforcement, and co-occurring mental health conditions; social factors include peers, family, poverty, and trauma (Bridley & Daffin; Florin & Trytek).
