Week 2 Interactive: Sex, Gender, and Sexual Orientation

Week 2 objective: Distinguish sex, gender identity, gender roles, gender stereotypes, and sexual orientation, and recognize three explanations of how gender roles are learned.

Key terms

  • Sex: the biological category of male or female, defined by genetic composition and reproductive anatomy and function.
  • Gender identity: a person's psychological sense of being male or female.
  • Gender roles: the behaviors, attitudes, and traits a culture designates as masculine or feminine.
  • Gender stereotypes: beliefs and expectations about the typical characteristics of men and women.
  • Sexual orientation: the direction of a person's emotional and erotic attraction.

These do not always correspond with one another. Being transgender describes gender identity, not sexual orientation.

Takeaway: Precise terms prevent assumptions. Knowing someone's sex or gender does not tell you their gender identity or sexual orientation, and many gender stereotypes are larger than any real difference. When in doubt, ask respectfully and listen.
