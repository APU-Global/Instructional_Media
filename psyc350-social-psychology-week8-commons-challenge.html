The Commons Challenge

Me versus we

You and three other fishers share one pond. Each season you choose how many fish to take (0–4). The pond holds up to 40 fish, and whatever is left regrows by about 30%.

The more you take, the more you earn, but if everyone takes too much, the pond collapses. The others are simulated, each with their own style.

What helps groups cooperate

Open communication and shared identity, fair rules and accountability (notice what the ledger did to the free rider), and appeals to values like stewardship of shared resources.
