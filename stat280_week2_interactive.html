Part 2 · Choose the right summary

The shape of a distribution influences which summary is most informative. Pick the best measure of center and spread for each situation.

Remember

Always look at the distribution. Graphs reveal what numbers hide, and an outlier deserves investigation before you decide how to handle it.
Interpret in context. Numbers need a story about the real situation. “The average commute is 31 minutes” says little unless you know whether a few very long trips are pulling it up.
