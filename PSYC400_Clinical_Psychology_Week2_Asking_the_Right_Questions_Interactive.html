Week 2 Interactive: Planning a Clinical Assessment

Week 2 objective: Write assessment questions that evaluate needs, strengths, potential diagnosis, and treatment options, and recognize what makes an assessment reliable and valid.

What an assessment is for

Clinical assessment collects information through observation, tests, and interviews to understand a person's problem and symptoms. It is ongoing: it shows whether treatment is needed, which treatment fits this client, and later whether it worked, measured against a baseline. A semi-structured interview starts from set questions but lets you follow up. Good questions also explore the client's skills, supports, and cultural context, not only problems.
