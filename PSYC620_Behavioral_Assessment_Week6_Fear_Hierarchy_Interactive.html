Week 6 Interactive: Climb the Fear Hierarchy

Week 6 objective: Explain how avoidance keeps a learned fear alive, walk through systematic desensitization step by step, and tell exposure techniques apart.

How a fear is learned and kept

Learned by pairing
Little Albert came to fear a white rat after it was paired with a loud sound, and the fear generalized to similar objects (Watson & Rayner, 1920).
Kept by avoidance
Moving away from the feared stimulus lowers fear. That relief negatively reinforces avoiding, so the person never learns the stimulus is safe.
Fear and relaxation cannot coexist
Systematic desensitization pairs relaxation with feared situations, from least to most feared.
In vivo or imaginal
Exposure can be in person or imagined. Imagined exposure is less intense but also less effective.
