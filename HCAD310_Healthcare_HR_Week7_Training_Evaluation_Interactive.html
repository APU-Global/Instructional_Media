Week 7 Interactive: Why Training Matters

Week 7 objective: Explain how employers plan, target, and measure training so that it pays off for employees and the organization (CLO 1).

Before training: assess the need at three levels

Organizational
What skills will the organization's strategy need, given changing technology and demographics?
Occupational (task)
What skills do specific jobs require, and where are people in those jobs falling short?
Individual
Which employees need training, based on their own performance?

Practice 1: what kind of need is this?

Each hospital situation is fictional. Choose the level of needs assessment, or decide that training is not the answer.

After training: Kirkpatrick's levels

The Kirkpatrick model evaluates training at four levels: reaction, learning, behaviour, and results. The textbook adds a fifth level, return on investment (ROI), which asks whether the gain is greater than the cost.

Practice 2: which level does this evidence measure?

A fictional emergency department ran a workshop on de-escalating agitated patients and visitors. Match each piece of evidence to its level.

Takeaway: Training pays off when it targets a real skill gap and is measured beyond "did they like it?" Evidence of learning, changed behaviour, results, and ROI is what lets HR show leaders that training is an investment worth weighing against other commitments such as benefits, bonuses, and tuition assistance.
