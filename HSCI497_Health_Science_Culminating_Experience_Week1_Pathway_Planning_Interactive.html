Week 1 Interactive: Career Fit and SMART Goals

Week 1 objective: Connect what draws you to a health sciences role with a concentration area, and write goals for your culminating experience that are Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Realistic, and Time-oriented.

Two tools for planning

The BSHS Career Paths resource suggests asking which part of a role appeals most: the what (educating people and promoting wellness), the how (leading teams and communication), the where (running a site's daily operations), the why (the psychological factors behind how people take part in healthcare), or the who (community data and trends). Human Relations then recommends SMART goals with shorter-term objectives, written down and revisited often.

Takeaway: Start with what draws you to the work (what, how, where, why, or who), then write it as a SMART goal with clear objectives. Write it down, own it, and revisit it as you and your circumstances change.
