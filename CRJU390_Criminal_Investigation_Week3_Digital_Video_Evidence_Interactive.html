Week 3 Interactive: Digital Video Evidence

Week 3 objective: Put the steps for preserving and enhancing digital evidence in order and judge which practices keep it reliable.

Valuable and fragile

McKee defines digital evidence as any information or data stored or transmitted in digital form that can be used in court. Examiners make exact copies called forensic images without altering the original, calculate hash values before and after imaging to prove nothing changed, and keep chain of custody logs for everyone who handles the evidence. The Fourth Amendment requires a search warrant supported by probable cause before officers access private digital data.

The course guide Forensic Image Upscaling applies the same rules to enhancement: never work directly on the original file, keep an audit trail, and treat basic enhancement as preliminary analysis only. Evidence offered in court must be processed by certified experts using specialized forensic software.
