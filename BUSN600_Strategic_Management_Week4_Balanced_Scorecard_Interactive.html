Week 4 Interactive: Building a Balanced Scorecard

Week 4 objective: Place performance measures in the four balanced scorecard perspectives, trace strategy to goal to objective to quantifiable metric, and judge results against performance referents, as the Basic Balanced Scorecard assignment asks.

Four perspectives, four questions

FINANCIAL
“How do we look to shareholders?”
CUSTOMER
“How do customers see us?”
INTERNAL BUSINESS PROCESS
“What must we excel at?”
LEARNING AND GROWTH
“Can we continue to improve and create value?”

Part 1: Sort the measures

Choose the balanced scorecard perspective for each measure.

Part 2: Strategy to goal to objective to metric

A fictional coffee shop is building its scorecard. Identify the level of each statement.

Part 3: Measures need referents

Compare a fictional result with its past performance and the industry average.

Takeaway: Balance financial measures with customer, process, and learning and growth measures, link each metric back to strategy, and always read a number against a referent. “Know well the condition of your flocks, and give attention to your herds, …” (Proverbs 27:23, ESV).
