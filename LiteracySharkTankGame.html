Build Your Pitch

⏱️

30 Minutes to Turn Student Data into an Inclusive Intervention

Your team will work through four design challenges. Use the student case file, assessment data, and course resources to build an intervention that is specific, research-backed, differentiated, and inclusive.

🔍

Step 1: Audit Assessment Data

8 MIN
Ask: What is really going on with this student?

Review the student’s screening and assessment data. Look beyond the overall score to identify the specific literacy needs. Look for:

  • Decoding vs. comprehension gaps
  • RAN/fluency bottlenecks
  • Vocabulary or language needs
  • Patterns across different assessments
Your task: Decide what additional diagnostic information or tools you would need to establish a clear baseline and target instruction.

Connection: TPE U7.10 • Fisher Modules 8–9

🌎

Step 2: Language & Morphology

8 MIN
Ask: How will we help access the language of the content?

Plan vocabulary instruction using morphology and etymology (Greek/Latin roots, prefixes, suffixes). For English Learners, consider how the home language becomes an asset.

Your task:
  • Select key academic/content vocabulary.
  • Identify useful roots, prefixes, or suffixes.
  • Add ELD scaffolds and language supports.
  • Incorporate translanguaging/home-language connections.

Connection: TPE U7.11 • TPE U7.5d

🧩

Step 3: Dyslexia & SPED Access

8 MIN
Ask: What gives access without lowering the bar?

Design targeted supports for reading and writing that address the student’s specific needs while keeping them engaged with grade-level content and expectations.

Your task:
  • Choose specific multisensory strategies.
  • Identify appropriate reading/writing accommodations.
  • Consider how you will collaborate with SPED providers.
  • Avoid overwhelming or unnecessary supports.

Remember: Access ≠ reduced rigor.

Connection: U2.3 • U3.5 • TPE MM 7.1, 7.3

❤️

Step 4: Inclusion & Faith

6 MIN
Ask: Why does this matter beyond academics?

Create the closing value proposition for your pitch. Explain how your intervention communicates that every student is created with dignity, purpose, and value.

Connect your instructional decisions to a theology of radical inclusion and Imago Dei.

Your task: Show how your intervention helps the student not only access learning, but belong, participate, and thrive.

Connection: Core Mission

Deliver The Pitch

🎥

15 Minutes • 90 Seconds to Make Your Case

Your team has designed an intervention. Now it’s time to sell your solution! Work together to record a high-energy 90-second Shark Tank-style pitch that convinces your audience that your literacy intervention is the right fit for your student.

🎤 Your 90-Second Pitch Should Include:

1

Meet the Student

Briefly introduce the student’s key literacy needs and the most important data that informed your decisions.

2

Reveal Your Product

Name and describe your targeted literacy intervention. What will you do, and what specific skills will you address?

3

Show the Difference

Highlight your most important instructional strategies, language supports, and SPED/dyslexia accommodations. Explain how your plan maintains grade-level rigor.

4

Make Your Case

End with your value proposition: Why is this intervention inclusive, research-backed, and designed to help this student belong, access, and thrive?

⚡ Pitch Like a Shark Tank Team

  • ✓ Keep it focused and persuasive.
  • ✓ Make sure every team member has a role.
  • ✓ Use your pitch deck to support your presentation.
  • ✓ Be confident—you're the experts!
  • ✓ Stay within the 90-second limit.
