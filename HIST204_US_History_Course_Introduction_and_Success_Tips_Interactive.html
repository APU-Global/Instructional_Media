About the course

Major developments of U.S. history from the Reconstruction Era through recent times. Coursework emphasizes the ideas, groups, and events that helped form American culture. You will develop critical reading and writing skills by analyzing primary documents of the era and by considering how past movements have shaped our country in the present day, including what these lessons mean for your faith, life, and learning.

Projected total

Post by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like. (Week 8 runs earlier: post by Wednesday, discuss through Friday.)

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
