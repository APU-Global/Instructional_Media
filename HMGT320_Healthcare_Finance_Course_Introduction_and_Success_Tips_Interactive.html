About the course

Examination of healthcare financial management, financial statement analysis, budgeting, and healthcare IT compliance. Students develop practical skills related to financial statement analysis and financial management for solving problems and making decisions that promote ethical stewardship of resources and financial sustainability of healthcare organizations. Throughout the course you will connect these decisions to a Christian worldview.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. (In Week 8: respond by Wednesday and discuss through Friday.) Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
