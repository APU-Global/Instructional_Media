The Utility Scale

The ethical action is the one that achieves the greatest good for the greatest number. Weigh a real policy decision, then find the problem hiding in the arithmetic.

The case

A county health board is deciding whether to close a rural clinic and use the savings to expand an urban clinic and lower taxes. For each group, set how much a typical member would be affected (from −10, seriously harmed, to +10, greatly helped). Every affected person counts.
