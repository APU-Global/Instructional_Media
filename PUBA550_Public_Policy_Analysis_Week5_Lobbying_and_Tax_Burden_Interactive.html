Week 5 Interactive: Who Gains, Who Pays?

Week 5 objective: Recognize how interest groups influence policy, tell the public interest and public choice explanations apart, and work out who bears the burden of a tax.

Part 1: What is the lobbyist doing?

Lobbyists provide legal, political, and technical information, and some groups mobilize constituents. Which is each example?

Part 2: Public interest or public choice?

Which theory of government action does each explanation reflect?

Part 3: Regressive, proportional, or progressive?

Three fictional households in the Town of Cedar Falls earn $20,000, $50,000, and $200,000 a year. Compute each household’s tax as a percentage of income, then classify the tax.

Part 4: Who bears the tax?

Before a new excise tax, a fictional product sells for $10 per unit. The state then imposes a tax of $3 per unit. In the new equilibrium, buyers pay $12 per unit.




Takeaway: When an organized group asks for a policy, ask two questions: what information is it giving you, and who will actually bear the cost? Tax incidence and public choice help you see past who signs the check.
