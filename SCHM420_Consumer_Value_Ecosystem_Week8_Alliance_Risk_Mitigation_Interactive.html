Week 8 Interactive: Alliance Risks and Mitigation

Week 8 objective: Identify the risks of having a partner make a major assembly and match each risk with a contract or management remedy.

Key terms

Cooperation lets firms share rather than duplicate resources, but it brings risks: loss of control, the transfer of valuable secrets, and being taken advantage of by partners. A partner may also put other customers first, work to different quality standards, or learn enough to become a competitor.

Remedies come from the contract (incentives, quality standards, clear accountabilities) and from managing the relationship (collaboration, rewarding good performance, shared forecasts, and redundancy).

Practice 1: Name the risk

Aerolux is a fictional aircraft maker whose wing assembly is built by a partner. Identify the risk in each situation.

Practice 2: Choose the mitigation

Choose the remedy that best addresses each problem.

Takeaway: Every alliance trades some control and knowledge for cost savings and shared expertise. Name the specific risk, write protections into the agreement, and manage the relationship with collaboration, fair rewards, and backup plans.
