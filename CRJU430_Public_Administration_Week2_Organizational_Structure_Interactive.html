Takeaway: Before redrawing a chart, name the work: line operations serve the public, administrative support keeps the agency running, and auxiliary services support the line. Then look at centralization, formalization, levels, and departmentalization: these choices decide who talks to whom, and where communication gaps are likely to appear.
