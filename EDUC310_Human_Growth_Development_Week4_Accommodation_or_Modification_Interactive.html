Week 4 Interactive: Supporting Exceptional Students

Week 4 objective: Distinguish accommodations from modifications and choose inclusive practices for exceptional students.

Know the difference

An accommodation removes the effects of a disability but does not alter the performance outcome (more time, smaller groups, substitutions, instructional prompts). A modification fundamentally alters the expected performance, so it approximates rather than shows mastery of the standard. Try accommodations first, and always work from the student’s IEP or Section 504 Plan.

Takeaway: Start by asking, “What is the goal of this lesson?” If you can keep the goal and change only time, support, input, or output, you are accommodating. Change the goal itself only when accommodations do not work, and only as the IEP or 504 Plan directs.
