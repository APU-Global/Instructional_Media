Week 3 Interactive: From Strategy Formation to Execution

Week 3 objective: Explain how an organization moves from strategy formulation to implementation and recognize intended, emergent, and realized strategy.

Lewin's three stages of planned change

1. UnfreezePrepare people: communicate the plan and the vision behind it, create urgency, win over opinion leaders, offer support, and let people help plan.
2. ChangeExecute: keep supporting people, create small wins by phasing the work, and remove obstacles as they appear.
3. RefreezeMake it stick: publicize success and build the new way into the culture, routines, and rewards.

Formulation decides what the organization will do; implementation is how it gets done through organizing, leading, and controlling.

Practice 1: Which stage is it?

Summit Home Health, a fictional agency, is moving every nurse from paper charts to tablets. Choose the stage each action belongs to.

Practice 2: Intended, emergent, or nonrealized?

Two years later, look back at Summit's strategy. Choose the best label for each part.

Takeaway: Strategy is realized only when people carry it out. Prepare them, keep supporting them through the change, and build the new way into the culture, while watching for opportunities the plan did not foresee.
