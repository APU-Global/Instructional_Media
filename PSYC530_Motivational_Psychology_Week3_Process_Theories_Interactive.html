Week 3 Interactive: Process Theories of Motivation

Week 3 focus: Use equity, expectancy, goal-setting, and job characteristics theories to diagnose how motivation breaks down, as Discussion 3 and the Motivation Assessment ask you to do.

Part 1: Expectancy check

Jordan sells snack combos at a movie theater and averages 100 a day. The manager asks for 300. Set how strongly Jordan holds each belief, then read the diagnosis.

Part 2: Equity comparison

Equity theory compares your outcome-to-input ratio with a referent’s. Rate inputs (effort, skills, experience, loyalty) from 1 to 10 and enter hourly pay as the outcome.

Part 3: Motivating potential of a job

Hackman and Oldham’s formula: MPS = ((skill variety + task identity + task significance) ÷ 3) × autonomy × feedback. Rate a job you know from 1 (very low) to 7 (very high); the scale is for practice only.

Part 4: Diagnose the case

Each workplace is fictional. Choose the process theory that best explains the problem, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Process theories explain how people decide to put in effort. Ask: Is it fair compared with others (equity)? Can I do it, will it pay off, and do I want the payoff (expectancy)? Is the target SMART, with feedback, ability, and commitment (goal setting)? Is the work meaningful, with autonomy and feedback (job characteristics)? Find the broken link before choosing a fix.
