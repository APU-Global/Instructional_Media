Week 3 Interactive: Transformational and Transactional Leadership

Week 3 objective: Recognize the four transformational tools and the three transactional methods in everyday leader behavior.

Transformational tools and transactional methods

Transformational (changes what people want)
  • Charisma (idealized influence): inspires confidence, commitment, and admiration.
  • Inspirational motivation: a vision that inspires others.
  • Intellectual stimulation: challenges norms and encourages creative thinking.
  • Individualized consideration: personal care for each follower.
Transactional (exchanges rewards for results)
  • Contingent reward: rewards accomplishments.
  • Active management by exception: leaves people alone but prevents problems.
  • Passive management by exception: steps in only after something goes wrong.

Research supports transformational leadership strongly, but transactional methods (except passive management by exception) are also effective. Leaders are encouraged to use both.

Practice: name the tool

Each leader is fictional. Choose the transformational tool or transactional method the leader is using, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Strong leaders combine an inspiring vision, fresh thinking, and personal care with fair rewards and early problem prevention. The one habit to drop is waiting for problems to explode before you engage.
