Week 6 objective: Identify evidence-based, low-cost strategies that improve maternal and child health in resource-constrained settings.

SDG 3.2 at a glance

SDG 3.2 aims to end preventable deaths of newborns and children under 5 by 2030. Rates are deaths per 1,000 live births.

Neonatal mortality rate (NMR)Probability of death between birth and 28 days. Target: fewer than 12 per 1,000 live births.
Under-5 mortality rate (U5MR)Probability of death between birth and age 5. Target: fewer than 25 per 1,000 live births.
Global progressU5MR fell from 71.2 in 2000 to 37.1 in 2019; NMR fell more slowly, from 28.0 to 17.9.

Takeaway: Most child deaths are preventable with affordable tools: skilled care around birth, vaccines, bednets, and oral rehydration, and good maternal nutrition. The GBD authors add that these work best inside strong health systems with efforts to reduce inequity that are explicit, sustained, and universal.
