Week 3 Interactive: Segmenting and Targeting

Week 3 objective: Identify the four bases for segmenting a market and match targeting strategies to the firms that use them.

Key terms for Discussion 3

  • Behavioral segmentation: benefits sought, usage rate, usage situation, and loyalty status
  • Demographic segmentation: age, income, gender, family life cycle, occupation, and similar traits
  • Geographic segmentation: region, size of city, population density, and climate
  • Psychographic segmentation: activities, interests, opinions, values, attitudes, and lifestyles
  • Target market: the group of customers an organization chooses to direct its marketing efforts toward
  • Positioning: how consumers perceive a product relative to the competition

Takeaway: Segment with several lenses, then choose targets that are large enough, growing, reachable, not swamped by competitors, within your resources, and a fit with your mission. Finally, position the offering where buyers see it as different and better.
