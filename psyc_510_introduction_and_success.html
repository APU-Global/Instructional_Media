Welcome to PSYC 510: Tests and Assessment

Psychological tests and assessments guide the choices professionals make about diagnosis, treatment, and decisions about people. Over eight weeks you will learn the language of testing, evaluate instruments, and choose them with ethics, cultural sensitivity, and a Christian worldview in mind.
