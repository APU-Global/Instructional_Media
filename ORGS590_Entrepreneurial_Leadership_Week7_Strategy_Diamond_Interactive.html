Week 7 Interactive: The Strategy Diamond

Week 7 objective: Use the strategy diamond to check that a strategic innovation plan is complete.

The five facets

Hambrick and Fredrickson's strategy diamond treats a strategy as an integrated set of choices. Arenas: where the firm will be active (geographic, product, and value-chain markets). Differentiators: what is unique and gives the firm an advantage, often based on tangible or intangible assets. Vehicles: how it will get there, through organic growth, acquisitions, or partnerships. Staging and pacing: the sequence and speed of strategic moves. Economic logic: how the firm will earn profits above its cost of capital.

Practice

Harvest Table is a fictional meal-kit company based in Oregon. Choose the facet of the strategy diamond each part of its plan addresses, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: A strategic innovation plan is complete when it answers all five questions: where (arenas), how we win (differentiators), how we get there (vehicles), in what order and how fast (staging and pacing), and how we make money (economic logic).
