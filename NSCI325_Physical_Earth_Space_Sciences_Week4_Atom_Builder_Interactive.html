Week 4 Interactive: Build an Atom

Week 4 objective (Discussion 4): Describe the parts of an atom, how they determine an element, its mass number, and its charge, and the experiments that showed atoms are real.

1. Atom builder

Add or remove particles. The number of protons decides the element (its atomic number). Protons plus neutrons give the mass number. If electrons do not equal protons, the atom is an ion.

Takeaway: No one sees an atom with the naked eye, yet many independent lines of evidence (whole-number combining ratios, Thomson's charged cathode rays, and Rutherford's bouncing alpha particles) all point to the same model: a tiny, dense, positive nucleus of protons and neutrons with electrons outside it.
