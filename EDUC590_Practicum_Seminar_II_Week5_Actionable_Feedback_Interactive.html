Week 5 Interactive: Specific, Actionable Feedback

Week 5 objective: Recognize and write feedback that names the criterion, cites evidence, and gives a next step.

Three parts of actionable feedback

The Guide says actionable feedback "communicates specifically what students have done well, what students can do next to improve their learning, and the degree to which students have made progress toward meeting the learning goals." Comments like "Good Job!" encourage but do not guide. Strong feedback (1) names the criterion tied to the ELA/Literacy or ELD goal, (2) points to evidence in the student's response, and (3) gives a next step the student can take now.
