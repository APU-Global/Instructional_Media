Week 7 Interactive: SMART Goals for an Ethical Strategic Plan

Week 7 objective: Distinguish goals, objectives, and measures, write SMART objectives for an ethical strategic plan, and recognize pressures that lead to ethical lapses.

Goals, objectives, and measures

Goals are outcome statements of what the organization is trying to accomplish. Objectives are precise, time-based, measurable actions that support a goal. Measures are the metrics used to gauge performance on objectives.

SMART: specific, measurable, attainable, realistic, and timely.
