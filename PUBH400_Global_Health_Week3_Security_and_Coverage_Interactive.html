Week 3 Interactive: Designing a Health System That Holds

Week 3 objective: Compare health system approaches and identify the elements of an effective, integrated system.

Two agendas in every health system

Lal et al. (2021) compared how health systems built mainly around global health security, mainly around universal health coverage, or around both fared in the COVID-19 pandemic.

Global health security (GHS)Preventing, detecting, and responding to public health threats through core capacities such as surveillance, risk communication, and coordination.
Universal health coverage (UHC)Access to comprehensive, appropriate, timely, quality health services without financial burden, usually through primary health care.
IntegratedHealth security capacities built into UHC-based primary care, the combination the authors found most effective.

Practice 2: Which design principle?

Lal et al. (2021) end with four recommendations: integration, financing, resilience, and equity. Match each action to its principle. Choose an answer for each item, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: No single agenda is enough. The systems that held up best joined health security capacities to universal coverage through primary care, and the authors' four principles, integration, financing, resilience, and equity, give you a checklist for designing your own hybrid.
