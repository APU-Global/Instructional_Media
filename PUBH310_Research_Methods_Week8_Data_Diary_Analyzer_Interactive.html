Week 8 Interactive: Data Diary Analyzer

Week 8 focus: Naming your data type, cleaning your log, and choosing the right summary before you build your Discussion 8 Data Diary visual.

Key ideas

Select each card to open it.

Activity 1: What kind of data is it?

Each item is something a student might track in a data diary. Classify it the way Kulkarni (2016) does.

Activity 2: Clean the log

A fictional student logged glasses of water for five days: Mon 6, Tue 5, Tue 5, Wed 60, Thu 7. Work through the three cleaning phases.

Activity 3: Summarize your own diary

Enter one number per day, separated by commas (at least 4 days). The analyzer reports the count, mean, median, mode, and range, with a text chart.

Takeaway: Know your data type first. Screen, diagnose, and edit before you count, and report what you exclude. Use the mode for categories, the median for ordered data, and the mean for interval and ratio numbers, then pick a chart that fits: bar or pie for categories, histogram or box plot for numbers.
