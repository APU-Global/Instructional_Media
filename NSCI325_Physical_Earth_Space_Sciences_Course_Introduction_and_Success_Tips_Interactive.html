About the course

Examination of the fundamental concepts of earth, space, and physical sciences. The course provides an exploration of the Earth’s structure and composition, atmosphere, and geographical attributes. It also focuses on the study of the solar system, the universe and the structure and properties of matter, and the principles of motion and energy. Students will also conduct appropriate scientific investigations using the scientific inquiry process.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and resources.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
