Week 6 Interactive: Hall of Shame Makeover

Week 6 objective: Recognize which warning sign puts an activity in the PE “Hall of Shame,” then choose a redesign that solves the problem.

The seven warning signs (from Discussion 6)

1. Absence of an educational objective
2. Potential to embarrass children
3. Elimination of participants
4. Overemphasis on fun rather than goal-directed learning
5. Lack of emphasis on skill acquisition or fitness development
6. Low participation time
7. Likelihood of danger or high risk of injury

An activity can show more than one sign; choose the one that is the main problem.

Practice: diagnose and fix

Each activity is a fictional example. (Dodgeball is left out on purpose, as in the discussion.)

Takeaway: Diagnose first, then redesign. Name a skill goal, keep every child moving, keep results private, and design risk out before play. California’s framework calls practices that strip students’ dignity harmful and inappropriate, and asks teachers to drop any activity built on waiting or elimination.

Warning signs as listed in EDUC 410 Discussion 6. Practices from the California Department of Education (2009), Physical Education Framework for California Public Schools, Chapter 6 (“Establishing a Safe Environment” and “Providing Effective Practice”). Activities are fictional.
