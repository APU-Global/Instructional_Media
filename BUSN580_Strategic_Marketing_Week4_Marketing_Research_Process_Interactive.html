Week 4 Interactive: Marketing Information

Week 4 objective: Apply the seven-step marketing research process, choose an appropriate research design, and tell primary data from secondary data.

Three research designs

ExploratoryLess structured; asks why. Depth interviews, focus groups, ethnography.
DescriptiveHard numbers that answer who, what, where, when, and how, often by survey.
CausalAn experiment that changes one variable and measures the effect on another.

Takeaway: Start by defining the problem, look for secondary data before paying for primary data, and match the design to the question: exploratory to learn why, descriptive to measure, causal to test what if. Gather information honestly; industrial espionage and careless use of customer data destroy trust.
