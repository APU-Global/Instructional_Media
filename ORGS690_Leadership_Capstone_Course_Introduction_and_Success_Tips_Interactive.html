About the course

Application of strategic leadership skills. Students conduct research, demonstrate ethical decision-making, communicate professionally, and learn to foster leadership qualities in others through coaching and development exercises. Students design an organization-based program aimed at contributing to the common good of the organization and of the local or global community.

Your capstone is a program you design to reimagine an organization: a focused vision for change that draws on everything you learned in the program. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Skills you will build

Select a week to see its topic, reading, and work. Every week also has a Deep Dive page and a Week Resources page. Discussions alternate with small-group Coaching Discussions in which you coach one another on your capstone projects.

The gradebook holds 800 points in three weighted categories: Discussions (15%), Coaching Discussions (24%), and the Capstone Project (41%). Select a category to learn more.

The syllabus also lists the Capstone Conference Meeting with Instructor (200 points, 20%): your one-on-one meetings in Weeks 3 and 6. The course has no gradebook item for these meetings, so ask your instructor how they will be recorded.

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

There are two kinds of discussions. Week 8 follows a shorter rhythm: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday.

DISCUSSIONS 1, 3, 8 · 50 POINTS
Initial post: 250–300 words minimum (Discussion 3: 300). Discussions 3 and 8 use APA formatting and cite 1–2 peer-reviewed articles.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150–200 words minimum each (Discussion 3: 150). Respond to more than the minimum of two peers with constructive feedback, questions, and coaching.
COACHING DISCUSSIONS 2, 5, 7 · 80 POINTS
Your instructor assigns small groups. Share your capstone work and coach your peers; in Week 5 you share a 2–3 minute video on your project.

Both kinds are graded with a 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements, Integrative Communication, and Written Communication (20/20/10 = 50 for Discussions; 40/30/10 = 80 for Coaching Discussions).
