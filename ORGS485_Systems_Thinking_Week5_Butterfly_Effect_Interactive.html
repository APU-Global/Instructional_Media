Week 5 Interactive: Chaos Theory

Week 5 objective: Explain, in simple terms, how a system can follow fixed rules and still be unpredictable, and what that means for managers.

Key ideas

Deterministic, yet unpredictable

Edward Lorenz showed in 1963 that weather, though an entirely deterministic system, cannot be reliably predicted from an earlier known state.

Sensitivity to initial conditions

The “butterfly effect”: tiny differences at the start grow into large ones. Crowds, dripping taps, and sand on a dune show it too.

Patterns, not predictions

We can recognize patterns and broad tendencies, but not predict any particular event. Order can emerge at the “edge of chaos.”
