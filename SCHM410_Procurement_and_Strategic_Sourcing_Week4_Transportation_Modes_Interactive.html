Week 4 Interactive: Transportation Modes and Logistics Choices

Week 4 objective: Choose a transportation mode from a shipment's cost, speed, size, and distance needs, and match logistics responses to location risks.

Key terms

  • Truck: most flexible; the only door-to-door mode; truckload or less-than-truckload.
  • Rail: cost effective for bulky, heavy goods over long distances (typically over 500 miles); energy efficient.
  • Air: speed over cost; small, light, valuable, or perishable goods; reliable but expensive.
  • Water: inexpensive for high-volume, heavy cargo over long distances; longer transit.
  • Pipeline: crude oil, natural gas, and petroleum products; very cheap per kilometre once built.
  • Intermodal: several modes under one contract, with containers that are not re-handled at each change.

Practice 1: Pick the mode

Each shipment is fictional. Choose the mode that best fits the need described.

Practice 2: Respond to location and supply risk

Choose the logistics response each fictional buyer is using.

Takeaway: There is no single best mode. Start from the shipment's needs (speed, cost, size, distance, door-to-door service), count the environmental impact, and plan for the risk that distance adds.
