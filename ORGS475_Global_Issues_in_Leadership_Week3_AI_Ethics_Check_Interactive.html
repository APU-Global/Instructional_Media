Week 3 Interactive: Power Tool or Pandora's Box?

Week 3 objective: Identify ethical pitfalls in AI-generated organizational messages and choose oversight practices that keep leaders accountable, as in Discussion 3.

Pitfalls to look for

  • Privacy: monitoring people or using their data without permission, the public's top AI concern in this week's reading.
  • Deception by omission: partial truths or leaving out relevant information.
  • Intentional bias: selecting only information that supports a position, or wording that favors or excludes a group.
  • No clear accountability: nobody owns the decision, a reason the public trusts companies and governments using AI less.

Practice 1: name the pitfall

Harborline Logistics, a fictional shipping company, asked an AI tool to draft internal messages. Choose the main ethical pitfall in each draft.

Practice 2: choose the safeguard

For each situation, choose the response that best keeps leaders responsible for AI-assisted decisions.

Takeaway: AI can draft a mission or message in seconds, but it can also invade privacy, omit what matters, or carry bias. Public trust drops when no one is accountable. Global leaders keep a named person responsible, invite concerns, and adapt to how each audience sees AI.
