Week 3 Interactive: Reading Your Plate

Week 3 objective: Analyze a day of eating, interpret nutrition recommendations, and explain how nutrient needs change across life (CLO 1, CLO 3).

Practice 1: Energy calculator

Discussion 3 asks you to analyze a nutrition log. Enter the grams from one day of your log (or use the fictional sample day). Carbohydrate and protein supply 4 calories per gram, alcohol 7, and fat 9. Then compare the shares with the recommendations in your reading.

Practice 3: Needs change across life

Match each fictional person with the nutrient focus the readings describe.

Takeaway: Calories come from carbohydrate, protein, fat, and alcohol, but a healthy plate is about more than calories. Recommendations set targets and limits, and the nutrients that need attention change with pregnancy, growth, aging, and diet choices.
