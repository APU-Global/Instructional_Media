Week 4 Interactive: Primary Care Workforce Supply and Demand

Week 4 objective: Examine healthcare careers from various roles by interpreting workforce supply and demand data.

Why this matters

Workforce projections tell administrators and job seekers where demand is heading. A national study by Bazemore, Petterson, and McCulloch counted primary care physicians, nurse practitioners (NPs), and physician assistants (PAs) and projected needs through 2040. Reading those numbers carefully shows why some careers are growing and why a headline count can hide important shifts.

Practice 1: work the numbers

Use the figures in each item to choose the correct answer. A calculator may help.

Practice 2: true or false?

Decide whether each statement is true or false according to the study.

Takeaway: Demand for primary care is growing faster than the supply of physicians, and nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and team-based care are filling the gap. When you judge whether a career will grow, look at what drives demand and read the numbers behind the headline.

Facts from Bazemore, A. W., Petterson, S. M., & McCulloch, K. K. (2025), "US primary care workforce growth: A decade of limited progress, and projected needs through 2040," Journal of General Internal Medicine, 40(2), 339-346.
