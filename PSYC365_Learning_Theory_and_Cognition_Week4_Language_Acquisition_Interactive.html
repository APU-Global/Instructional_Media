Week 4 objective: Identify the components of language and connect evidence to the major theories of language acquisition.

What the readings say

Lally and Valentine-French describe language in layers: phonemes (the smallest units of sound that change meaning), morphemes (the smallest units of meaning), syntax (the rules for building sentences), and pragmatics (the social rules of communication). Learning theory explains some language growth through reinforcement and imitation, but children also produce forms no adult says, which supports Chomsky's idea of built-in grammar rules. Children deprived of language early may never fully learn it, which points to a critical period, and even toddlers use language to make themselves understood by others.
