Welcome to Math for the Elementary Teacher II

One of two math content courses for future elementary teachers. You will build deep understanding of geometry, measurement, data analysis, statistics, and probability and learn how to teach it. Tap a skill to see what it means for you.

Prerequisite: MATH 099 with a C- or better, or SAT 540 / ACT 23 math. MATH 301 and 302 can be taken in either order.

Eight weeks, two exams (Exam 1 covers Weeks 1–3 content; Exam 2 covers Weeks 4–8). Tap a week to see its topic, textbook reading, and what is due.
