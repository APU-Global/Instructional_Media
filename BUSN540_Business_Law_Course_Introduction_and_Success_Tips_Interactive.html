About the course

Jurisprudence augmented with a historical review of the U.S. legal system. You will review managerial skills and models to resolve ethical challenges in corporate governance along with the rudiments of legalities in organizations. Topics include courts and legal systems, contract law, corporations, partnerships, limited liability companies, intellectual property, personal and real property, and stocks and bonds and their regulation under securities laws, all considered from a Christian perspective.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 30 points. In Week 8, respond by Wednesday and discuss through Friday. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
300 words minimum
Respond to every prompt, with APA formatting and 1–2 scholarly, peer-reviewed sources properly cited and referenced.
COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
At least two, 150 words minimum each
Suggest further resources, ask clarifying questions, or add context and insight. Avoid simple agreement.
SCHOLARLY SOURCES
Include at least one industry-authoritative or scholarly source per discussion. Textbooks, Wikipedia, blogs, and online magazines are not scholarly sources.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Discussion Responses (12), Integrative Communication (12), and Written Communication (6), for 30 points.
