Week 4 Interactive: Performance Issue Triage

Week 4 objective: Respond to performance problems consistently and fairly: classify the issue, check for just cause, and choose an appraisal method.

The performance issue model

MANDATED ISSUE

Serious, often a legal requirement (privacy, safety, harassment). Handle at once under a written policy.

SINGLE INCIDENT

A one-time misstep. A coaching conversation is usually enough.

BEHAVIOR PATTERN

An uncorrected incident that has become a habit. Talk clearly about expectations.

PERSISTENT PATTERN

The pattern continues after correction. Investigate, then consider an improvement plan or progressive discipline.

Takeaway: Discipline exists to correct behavior, not to punish. Coach single incidents early, document every step, investigate before acting, and apply rules evenly. Tie every evaluation to the job description, and choose methods that measure what the job actually requires.
