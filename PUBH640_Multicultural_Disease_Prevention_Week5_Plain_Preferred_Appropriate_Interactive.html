Week 5 Interactive: Reviewing a Campaign for Language and Culture

Week 5 objective: Apply CDC’s three trust-building communication practices to a health campaign, and check the facts about language diversity and health literacy behind them.

Three practices that build trust

CDC summarizes these key recommendations from the National Academies’ workshops on trust and health literacy.

Plain language
Words, sentences, and terms the audience can understand and use.
Preferred language and channels
The language the audience prefers, delivered where they already get information.
Culturally and linguistically appropriate
Wording, examples, and images that fit the audience’s culture and language use.
