Week 4 Interactive: The Poet's Toolbox

Week 4 objective: Recognize the tools teachers highlight in elementary poetry lessons, and sequence a short poetry unit you could bring to Discussion 4.

Tools young poets use

Alliteration: repeated beginning sounds. Friday (2023) links it to students' growing phonemic awareness.
Simile: a comparison using like or as, as in a class poem on "What love is like."
Personification: giving human actions to things, as when Friday's students imagined light sources as alive.
Repetition: repeating a word or line for rhythm and emphasis; Friday's free verse models use it occasionally.
