Week 8 Interactive: Identifying Treatment Outcomes

Week 8 objective: Recognize the main types of psychotherapy outcomes and choose outcomes to decrease and to increase for a client.

Types of outcomes (Cuijpers, 2019)

  • Symptom reduction: the most studied outcome, measured against a baseline.
  • Client-defined outcomes: the problems the client names, such as returning to work or fighting less at home, often rated with idiographic measures.
  • Quality of life: physical, psychological, and social health, including relationships and daily activities.
  • Negative outcomes: deterioration, serious adverse events, non-response, and dropout.
  • Economic outcomes: costs compared with benefits, often in cost-effectiveness analyses.
