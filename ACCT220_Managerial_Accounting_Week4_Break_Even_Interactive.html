Week 4 objective: Use contribution margin and cost-volume-profit analysis to find break-even and target profit.

Snowboard Company

Each snowboard sells for $250 and has $150 of variable cost. Fixed costs are $50,000 a month. Move the sliders to see how each change shifts the break-even point.

Takeaway: Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution margin per unit. Add the target profit to fixed costs to find the units needed for a profit goal. A higher price or lower variable cost raises the contribution margin and lowers break-even.
