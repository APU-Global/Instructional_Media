Week 5 Interactive: Domestic Violence Risk Assessment

Week 5 objective: Apply a brief lethality screen to fictional cases, compare the main risk instruments, and explain their limits.

Practice 1: Screen the case

Read each fictional case. Check every DA-5 item that is present, then select Score this case. The DA-5 treats three or more "yes" answers as high risk (Garcia-Vergara et al., 2022).

Practice 2: Know your tools

Match each description with the instrument it describes, then select Check my answers.

Practice 3: True or false?

Decide whether each statement about domestic violence risk assessment is true.

Takeaway: Escalating violence, separation, threats to kill, weapons, strangulation, and violent jealousy signal lethal risk. Brief screens such as the DA-5 and the Lethality Screen help first responders act quickly, but they can miss factors that the full Danger Assessment counts, so evaluators corroborate information and state the limits of any tool.

Source: Garcia-Vergara, E., Almeda, N., Fernández-Navarro, F., & Becerra-Alonso, D. (2022). Risk assessment instruments for intimate partner femicide: A systematic review. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 896901. This practice is for learning only and is not a clinical screening tool. Cases are fictional.
