Welcome aboard: Marketing strategy starts with the customer and now runs through digital channels in a global market. In this course you build a five-part Strategic Marketing Plan for an organization you choose, analyze Chipotle’s digital pivot, work through Harvard Business Publishing cases and a simulation, and weigh marketing decisions through a Christian worldview. Create your HBP account in Week 1, choose your plan organization early, and ask questions in Ask the Prof whenever you need help.
