Week 3 Interactive: Capacity and Risk Lab

Week 3 objective: Measure capacity, tell everyday variability from disruption, and match mitigation strategies to when they are used.

Part 1: Capacity calculator

Design capacity is the maximum designed output rate. Effective capacity is design capacity minus allowances. Utilization = actual output ÷ design capacity. Efficiency = actual output ÷ effective capacity.

Part 2: Operational or disruption risk?

Operational risks are day-to-day disturbances such as lead-time and demand fluctuations. Disruption risks are low-frequency, high-impact events that make facilities or links temporarily unavailable. All firms are fictional.

Part 3: Proactive or reactive?

Proactive strategies are put in place before a disruption, such as how the network is configured. Reactive strategies respond once it hits.

Takeaway: Capacity commits resources for the long term, so check what your suppliers and distributors can absorb. Name the type of risk first, then combine proactive structure (multiple, geographically spread partners; mitigation inventory; backup suppliers) with reactive options, and plan how partners will recover together.
