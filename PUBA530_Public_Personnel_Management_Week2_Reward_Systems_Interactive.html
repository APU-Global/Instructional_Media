Week 2 Interactive: Rewards in Lean Times

Week 2 objective: Compare public and private reward systems: apply a pay philosophy, recognize how government jobs are valued, and choose motivators that work when raises are scarce.

Three pay philosophies

MARKET

Pay the going rate for the job in the labor market, based on salary research.

MARKET PLUS

Find the going rate and add a percentage, to attract and keep the best people.

MARKET MINUS

Pay a percentage below the market, often offset by other benefits such as flextime.

Takeaway: Compensation is the whole package, not just the paycheck. Public employers usually rely on classification and pay grades, which protect fairness but can feel rigid. When money is tight, explain clearly how pay is set, invest in training and career paths, and recognize achievement, because employees judge fairness by comparing themselves with others.
