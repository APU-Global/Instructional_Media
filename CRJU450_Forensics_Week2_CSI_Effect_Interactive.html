Takeaway: Many jurors expect scientific evidence, but research found little sign that CSI changed their verdicts. Real scenes are controlled: one logged entry point, no “tourism,” and elimination samples, because every contact can transfer material under Locard’s exchange principle.

Sources: Shelton, D. E. (2008), The “CSI Effect”: Does It Really Exist?, National Institute of Justice (nij.ojp.gov); McKee, A. J. (2024), Fundamentals of Criminal Investigation, Sections 1.5 and 3.2 (docmckee.com). Scenarios are fictional.
