What makes a goal SMART

Your strategic plan uses Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Saylor’s Organizational Behavior uses a close variant (specific, measurable, aggressive, realistic, time-bound). Either way:

  • Specific: a clear target. “Do your best” gives no target.
  • Measurable: you can tell whether you reached it, even for “soft” targets such as satisfaction, which can be tracked with surveys.
  • Achievable: difficult but based in reality; impossible goals have no motivational value.
  • Relevant: linked to the factors that drive success; for your plan, the root cause you diagnosed.
  • Time-bound: states when the target will be reached.
