Week 7 Interactive: Workflow Automation

Week 7 objective: Sequence an automated message workflow and identify its triggers, automated messages, decision points, and human touches.

The parts of a workflow

Automation is a process triggered by a customer action: each action can trigger an automatic email or text. A new contact should start a pre-planned drip sequence that moves from welcoming, to informing, to inviting, and real workflows add decision points (did they open, click, or read?) so the next message depends on what the person did last. Your Week 7 presentation diagrams decision points, automated events, and outcomes, with each call to action surrounded by in-person or automated messages designed to attract or inform instead of sell.

Riverbend Bike Co-op (fictional) gains a new subscriber who signed up after reading its blog post on commuting by bike. Choose the message that belongs at each step, then select Check my order.

Takeaway: A good workflow is a planned conversation: a trigger starts it, automated messages welcome, inform, and invite, decision points let behavior choose the next step, and people step in where a relationship needs them.
