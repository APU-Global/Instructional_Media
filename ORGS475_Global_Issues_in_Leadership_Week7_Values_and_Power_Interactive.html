Week 7 Interactive: Values, Power, and AI

Week 7 objective: Identify the six bases of power and apply consequences, duties, and character to a values dilemma, preparing for Discussion 7 and Leadership Manifesto Part 5.

Six bases of power (French and Raven)

  • Legitimate: comes from one's role or position.
  • Reward: the ability to grant something people want.
  • Coercive: the ability to take something away or punish.
  • Expert: comes from knowledge and skill.
  • Information: comes from access to specific information.
  • Referent: people like, respect, and want to be like the person.

Practice 1: name the base of power

Each person works at Solvara Health, a fictional global medical-device company. Choose the main source of power in each situation.

Practice 2: three lenses on a dilemma

A student asked an AI tool about the course's Sample Moral Dilemma: an autonomous car must choose between its one passenger and five pedestrians. Match each AI statement to the ethical lens it uses.

Takeaway: Power is the ability to influence others, and how a leader uses it reveals that leader's values. Referent and expert power build commitment; coercion works through fear. When you test whether AI "has values," notice whether it ranks consequences, duties, and character, or only lists them. Your own manifesto should do the ranking.
