Week 3 Interactive: Irregular Preterites and Double Object Pronouns

Week 3 objective: Choose irregular and stem-changing preterite forms, and replace two objects with pronouns in the right order.

Quick reference

Irregular preterite stems take one set of endings, with no written accents: -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron
VerbStemExample
estar, poder, ponerestuv-, pud-, pus-estuve, pudo, pusimos
querer, saber, tener, venirquis-, sup-, tuv-, vin-quiso, supe, tuviste, vinieron
Stem-changing -ir verbs (3rd person only)pedir (i, i), dormir (ue, u)pidió, pidieron; durmió, durmieron

Double object pronouns: indirect before direct (ID): Te lo preparo. Le and les become se before lo, la, los, las: Le compra unos zapatos → Se los compra.

Takeaway: Irregular preterite stems (estuv-, pud-, pus-, quis-, sup-, tuv-, vin-) share one set of endings with no written accents. With two object pronouns, the indirect comes first, and le/les become se before lo, la, los, las.
