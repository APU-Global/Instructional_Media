Week 3 Interactive: Planning a Stakeholder Interview

Week 3 objective: Plan the stakeholder interview that Discussion 3 and Capstone Project Part 2 require: decide whom to involve, prepare the interview well, and write questions that let the stakeholder speak.

Three overlapping stakeholder groups

The Community Tool Box evaluation framework (Chapter 36, Section 1) names three groups to involve.

PROGRAM OPERATIONS
Community leaders, volunteers, collaborators, partners, funders, administrators, managers, and staff.
SERVED OR AFFECTED
Participants, families, neighborhood organizations, residents, including people who face barriers and those who question the program.
PRIMARY INTENDED USERS
The specific people expected to act on the findings.

Part 1: Which group?

The fictional Harbor County job-training program is the focus. Place each person or organization in its best-fitting group.

Part 2: Does it follow the EPA guide?

The EPA Public Participation Guide lists principles for planning stakeholder interviews. Decide whether each choice follows them.

Part 3: Choose the better question

Part 2 asks how the stakeholder’s needs and interests are being met, what needs to be improved, and what would help. Choose the question that best opens up each topic.

Takeaway: Interviews work when you choose people deliberately, prepare them, protect the time, and ask questions that let them tell you what you did not know. "Let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak" (James 1:19, ESV).
