Week 6 Interactive: Design Detective

Week 6 objective: Identify experimental, quasi-experimental, and correlational designs and the threats each one leaves open.

The key questions

Was the independent variable manipulated? If not, the study is nonexperimental (correlational). If it was, were participants randomly assigned? If yes, it is a true experiment. If no, it is a quasi-experiment: nonequivalent groups, pretest-posttest, interrupted time series, or a combination of these.

Part 1: Name the design

Each study is fictional. Choose the design, then select Check my answers.

Part 2: Weigh the evidence

Takeaway: Random assignment is what earns a causal claim. Quasi-experiments settle directionality but leave confounds open; watch for history, maturation, and regression to the mean in pretest-posttest designs. When you compare an experimental article with a meta-analysis this week, ask how much each design can support.
