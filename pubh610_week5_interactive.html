PUBH 610 · Week 5 Interactive

Mission Anchors Decisions

A mission states why an organization exists, a vision describes where it is going, and values guide how it acts. Learn to tell them apart, test real decisions against values, and practice servant leadership.

Part 1 · Mission, vision, or value?

Riverside Community Health Center has statements on its wall. Classify each one: a mission says why the organization exists, a vision describes where it is going, and a value guides how it acts.

Part 2 · Test decisions against the values

Strong leaders connect daily work to the mission and test decisions against organizational values. Riverside’s values are:

CompassionIntegrityEquityStewardship

For each proposal, mark every value you think it conflicts with, then check. Select none if it fits all four.

Part 3 · Servant leadership in practice

Servant leadership puts the needs of followers and the community first. It emphasizes listening, empathy, and stewardship. Choose the response that best reflects it.

Alignment matters. Notice where your personal values align with your organization’s and where they conflict. When they conflict, raise concerns constructively. Reflect, too, on how your own faith informs your sense of calling and service, and respect the beliefs of those you lead.
