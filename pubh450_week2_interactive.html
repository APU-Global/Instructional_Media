Think in Systems: AI in a Health System

AI does not operate alone: it changes the relationships among patients, providers, organizations, and communities. Explore a hospital’s new AI tool from every stakeholder’s point of view, then test an AI tool against sound guidance.

Part 1 · Map the stakeholders

Scenario: a hospital adds an AI triage tool to its emergency department. Select each stakeholder to see what it may gain and risk. Then turn on the ripple effects to see how one group’s experience can change another’s. (Illustrative scenario.)

    Part 3 · Test the tool

    AI symptom checkers and similar tools can be tested against known cases: compare the tool’s output with sound clinical guidance and note where it helps and where it falls short. Judge each result. (Fictional cases for practice, not medical advice.)

    Keep humans in the loop. AI can inform decisions about care and resources, but human oversight remains essential: AI informs, people decide.
