Week 5 Interactive: Needs-Based Motivation

Week 5 objective: Identify the needs behind employee behavior and the drawbacks each acquired need brings to a new manager.

Why needs matter for emerging leaders

Need-based theories say a manager's job is to identify what people need and make the work environment a means of meeting those needs. Maslow ranks physiological, safety, social, esteem, and self-actualization needs. McClelland says people acquire needs for achievement, affiliation, and power, and each has strengths and drawbacks in a management role.

Practice 1: which need is speaking?

For each fictional team member, choose the Maslow need they are most trying to satisfy.

Practice 2: coaching emerging leaders

Each fictional emerging leader shows one dominant acquired need. Choose it, then check the coaching insight.

Takeaway: The same treatment can motivate one person and frustrate another, so start by learning what each person needs. When you develop an emerging leader, help them see the drawbacks of their own need orientation: achievers must learn to coach and delegate, affiliators to give honest feedback, and power-oriented leaders to use influence for others.
