Week 6 objective: Recognize the elements of humility and the conditions under which contact between groups reduces prejudice.

Humility and contact

Emmons describes humility as a character strength that includes an accurate (not underestimated) sense of one's abilities, the ability to acknowledge mistakes and gaps in knowledge, openness to new ideas and contradictory information, a relatively low focus on self, and appreciation of the many ways people contribute. Worthy, Lavigne, and Romero describe the contact hypothesis: closer connection with members of other groups can reduce prejudice, especially when contact provides information that shows stereotypes are wrong and when people take one another's perspective.
