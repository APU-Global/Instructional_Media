Week 3 Interactive: Flexible Budgets and Variances

Week 3 objective: Examine managerial concepts germane to healthcare organizations, including those related to operational budgeting.

Why flex the budget?

A master budget is set for one planned level of activity. When actual volume differs, comparing actual costs with the master budget is unfair: more tests should cost more. A flexible budget revises the master budget to the actual level of activity. Variable costs are recalculated at actual volume; fixed costs stay at their budgeted total. A variance is unfavorable when the lab spent more than expected and favorable when it spent less.

Part 4: Manage by exception

The lab's policy: investigate any variance, favorable or unfavorable, equal to 10% or more of the flexible budget amount.

Takeaway: Compare actual costs with what the actual volume should have cost. A flexible budget separates the effect of volume from the effect of spending, and a management-by-exception rule focuses attention on the variances that matter most.
