Week 1 Interactive: The Eleven Elements of TPE 7

Week 1 objective: Recognize each of the eleven elements of TPE 7 in classroom practice and connect the foundational skills to your three observed lessons.

TPE 7 in brief

TPE 7, Effective Literacy Instruction for All Students, has eleven elements. 1-4 describe how literacy teaching is grounded and delivered (standards and the five framework themes; UDL, MTSS, and the Dyslexia Guidelines; asset-based practice; active, engaging, responsive instruction). 5 lists the foundational skills. 6-9 are the other framework themes: Meaning Making, Language Development, Effective Expression, and Content Knowledge. 10 is monitoring progress and screening, and 11 is English language development.

Practice 1: which element is this?

Each classroom is fictional. Choose the TPE 7 element the moment shows best, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: which observed lesson?

Your three Student Teaching Observations divide the foundational skills among them. Match each emphasis to its lesson plan.

Takeaway: For your TPE paper, write one paragraph per element, each anchored in a real moment from your placement. Keep the Element 5 list handy: it is the checklist for your three observed lessons.

Element wording from the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing, California Teaching Performance Expectations (TPEs), TPE 7 (n.d.); lesson plan emphases from the EDUC 591 Student Teaching Observation Parts 1-3. Classrooms are fictional.
