Week 6 Interactive: Ratio or Fraction?

Week 6 objective: Tell part-to-part ratios from part-to-whole fractions, and solve ratio problems by scaling before writing a proportion.

Tape diagram builder

Set a girls-to-boys ratio and the number of groups. Each box is one student per group.

   

Practice

Type a whole number, a fraction such as 2/5, or a ratio such as 3:5. Equivalent answers count (4/10 is the same as 2/5). Then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: A ratio of 2 : 3 girls to boys compares part to part. The fraction of the class that is girls compares part to whole: 2 of every 5 students, or 2/5. Before you cross-multiply, scale the ratio up or down with a table or tape diagram. That reasoning is what the equation records.

Ratio language, the recipe and unit-rate examples, and tape diagrams from the California Common Core State Standards: Mathematics (California Department of Education, 2013), standards 6.RP.1 to 6.RP.3. Part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios and the "Mixing Paint" task (3 parts yellow to 4 parts red) from the Mathematics Framework for California Public Schools (2024), Chapter 7.
