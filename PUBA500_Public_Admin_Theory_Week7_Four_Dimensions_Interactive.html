Week 7 Interactive: Public Financial Management

Week 7 objective: Apply Parry's four dimensions of public financial management and recognize the controls that reduce fiduciary risk.

Parry's four dimensions

  • Aggregate fiscal management: fiscal sustainability (funding activities from revenues and borrowing while remaining solvent), resource mobilization, and resource allocation under policy priorities.
  • Operational management: performance management, value for money, and managing within the budget.
  • Fiduciary risk management: controls, compliance, and oversight against the risk of public money being stolen, misused, or used corruptly.
  • Governance: accountability and transparency to the people and other stakeholders.

Safeguards against fiduciary risk

  • Internal controls: payment authorization, managing against budgets, reconciliations.
  • Internal audit: the oversight system that an organization’s management establishes.
  • External audit: an additional, outside element of risk management.
  • Legislative oversight: a committee such as a Public Accounts Committee reviews financial and audit reports; local governments should have similar committees.

Takeaway: Sound public finance is more than balancing the books. Sustainability, operations, risk, and governance work together, and open oversight is one of the strongest tools against misuse of public money.
