PUBH 610 · Week 4 Interactive

From Results to a Development Plan

Assessment results become useful when they lead to a plan. Rate your competencies, compare them with what a role requires and how others might see you, then turn the biggest gap into a SMART goal.

Part 1 · Compare yourself with the target

Competencies are the knowledge, skills, and behaviors effective leaders show. Choose a role, rate yourself honestly (and how you think others see you), and watch the picture form. Compare results with the competencies your role requires, and consider how others might see the same behaviors.

Part 2 · Name and rank the gaps

A gap is the difference between current and desired capability. Prioritize the gaps with the greatest impact, and be honest and specific. Select a competency to build a goal for it.
