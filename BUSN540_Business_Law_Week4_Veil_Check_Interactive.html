Takeaway: Courts pierce the veil when the corporation is used for fraud or injustice or when it does not act like a corporation: inadequate capital, skipped meetings, missing minutes and annual reports, and commingled assets. Even a well-kept veil never covers an owner’s own torts or personal contracts.
