About the course

Planning, instructing, and assessing within the elementary classroom. Candidates are responsible for designing learning experiences and assessment plans, and for teaching students using best practices. Candidates will be supervised and assessed by a designee of the school site administrator.

This is the companion course to EDUC 590 (corequisite). Its requirements are three observations of your own recorded teaching by your Clinical Supervisor in GoReact, plus final evaluations by your Clinical Supervisor and your Site Supervisor. CalTPA is not required to complete this course, but you are expected to make progress on it during your intern teaching placement.

The observation cycle (Weeks 3, 4, and 5)

Each Intern Teaching Observation follows the same four steps. Each lesson has its own required foundational-skill emphases from TPE 7.5.

1 · PLAN
Write a full 30–45 minute lesson plan on the Part B: Learning Segment Template, teaching every required emphasis two or three times. Submit it to your Clinical Supervisor before you film.
2 · RECORD
With video releases on file, record the entire lesson and upload it to GoReact.
3 · ANNOTATE
Build a Required Emphases chart with at least 4–6 entries: timestamp, what the teacher did, the foundational skill, what it adds, and the TPE met.
4 · CONFER
Schedule pre- and post-observation conferences with your Clinical Supervisor, and apply the feedback in your next observation.

Intern support hours

Interns log 96 hours of support each semester (192 for the school year). Each 8-week session needs 48 hours: 36 general and 12 English learner (EL). Your supervisor and instructor do not log these hours for you. Submit this session’s log in Week 8.



Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

What counts as support?

  • School site, district, or university support: district training, site faculty meetings, PLC and grade-level meetings, coursework, and Clinical Supervisor meetings.
  • Classroom observations and coaching; coaching from an administrator.
  • Co-planning with an EL or special education expert; weekly planning with an EL-authorized credential holder.
  • Observing SDAIE or ELD lessons; reviewing test results with colleagues; new teacher orientation.
