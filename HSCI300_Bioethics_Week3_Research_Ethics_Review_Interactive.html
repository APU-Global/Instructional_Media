Week 3 Interactive: Research Ethics Review Board

Week 3 objective: Evaluate human experimentation and scientific integrity, the core of Discussion 3.

Three core principles, plus integrity

Respect for persons
Free, informed, and ongoing consent; protection for those who cannot exercise autonomy.
Concern for welfare
Risks outweighed by benefits; privacy and confidentiality protected.
Justice
Fair treatment, with attention to groups that have historically carried more than their share of research risks.
Scholarly integrity
No fabricated data, no plagiarism, honest credit, and correction of errors.

    Takeaway: Trust in research rests on informed, voluntary consent, risks that benefits justify, fair selection, and honest data. When you propose a plan to reduce misconduct, build in independent review before a study, monitoring during it, and transparency after it.
