Week 2 Interactive: Training an AI Assistant

Week 2 objective: Judge which parts of an AI Assistant's knowledge and instructions are ready to use, and match each platform to how it is trained.

What "training" means

You are not coding a new AI. You customize how an existing one behaves and responds through prompts, settings, and sample dialogues. The AI Training Template sets the order:

  • Knowledge first: a narrow, population-specific topic; definitions, statistics, causes and risk factors, support options, coping strategies, and common myths; 5-7 credible sources, each with a 3-4 sentence summary.
  • Then the prompt: the assistant's role and tone, what it should do, what it should not do (such as diagnose or recommend treatment), and 2-3 conversation starters.
  • Then test: ask real questions and refine until the answers are accurate.
