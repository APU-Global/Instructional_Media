From Construct to Measurement

A construct is a broad concept; an operational definition states exactly how it is measured. Climb from idea to measurable variable, sort the levels of measurement, and see why differing definitions change conclusions.

Part 1 · Climb the ladder

Pick a construct, then choose how to turn it into a variable and finally into an operational definition. Wrong choices explain why they fall short.
