Practice 2: Que, quien, or lo que?

Que refers to things and people, quien(es) to people, and lo que to a situation or idea.

Takeaway: The imperfect paints the backdrop (time, feelings, habits, actions in progress); the preterite moves the story (beginnings, endings, completed events, interruptions). Use both in one story, as Discussion 5 asks.
