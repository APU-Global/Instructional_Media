About the course

Evaluation of public programs typically managed by public administrators, based on established quantitative and qualitative methods. Students analyze applied research design, data collection, data management, data analysis, analytical reporting, and governmental and organizational research and data. Students recognize the need to effectively collaborate with non-profit organizations and other contractors through which many of these programs are offered. Students evaluate programs, analyze policy research, and synthesize the research to plan programs, make critical decisions, and communicate their results to others.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points (Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Most prompts let you use the video recording tool, an audio response, or a written post. Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL RESPONSE
300 words
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the week’s resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
200 words minimum each
Respond to a minimum of three posts (Discussions 1–2: more than the minimum of two peers). Add resources, questions of clarification, or context and insight; avoid simple posts of agreement.
SOURCES
2 APA-formatted sources are required per discussion.

Discussions are graded with the 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10), for 50 points.
