Week 5 Interactive: The Homicide Ladder

Week 5 objective: Distinguish murder from manslaughter and grade a criminal homicide by the defendant's intent and circumstances.

Climb the ladder

What separates murder from manslaughter is criminal intent. Murder requires malice aforethought: intent to kill, intent to cause serious bodily injury, or a depraved heart. Manslaughter is an unlawful killing without malice. Select each rung, from not criminal to most serious.

Practice: Grade the homicide

Each scenario is fictional. Choose the most likely classification in a typical jurisdiction, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Start with intent. Malice (intent to kill, intent to cause serious bodily injury, or a depraved heart) makes a killing murder; premeditation, a specified means, or a serious felony raises it to first degree. Adequate provocation in a heat of passion lowers an intentional killing to voluntary manslaughter, and a killing with no murder intent is involuntary manslaughter.
