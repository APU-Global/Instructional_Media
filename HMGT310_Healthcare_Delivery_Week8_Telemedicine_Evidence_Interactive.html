Week 8 Interactive: Telemedicine Types, Evidence, and Equity

Week 8 objective: Assess the challenges and opportunities that shape the future of healthcare delivery, using telemedicine as the example.

Why this matters

Your discussion asks whether telemedicine is the future of healthcare. An umbrella review of 98 systematic reviews by Eze, Mateus, and Cravo Oliveira Hashiguchi (2020) sorted telemedicine into three types and summarized evidence on effectiveness, cost, patient experience, and implementation. Knowing the type of service and the strength of the evidence helps you make a balanced argument.
