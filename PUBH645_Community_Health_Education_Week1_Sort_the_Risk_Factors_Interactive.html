Week 1 Interactive: Risk Factors and Health Literacy

Week objective: Identify and classify risk factors for leading causes of death and connect them to health literacy in community health education.

Three ideas to carry into Discussion 1

  • Size the burden. In CDC's 2024 data, stroke is the 4th leading cause of death, Alzheimer's disease the 6th, and suicide the 10th.
  • Sort the risks. Medical conditions and behaviors can be changed; age and family history cannot.
  • Think in levels. Suicide risk and protective factors sit at the individual, relationship, community, and societal levels.

Practice 1: sort the stroke risk factors

CDC groups stroke risk into medical conditions, behaviors, and other factors you cannot change. Sort each one.

Practice 2: find the level

CDC places suicide risk factors at four levels. Choose the level for each.

Practice 3: personal or organizational health literacy?

All scenarios are fictional. Healthy People 2030 defines both kinds of health literacy.

Takeaway: Separate the risks a community can change (conditions and behaviors) from those it cannot (age, family history), look for factors at every level from the individual to society, and make your message usable: plain language, the audience's own language and channels, and culturally appropriate wording.
