Week 7 Interactive: Encourage the Heart

Week 7 focus: Choose recognition that fits the person and give praise specific enough to mean something, for Discussion 7.

Why recognition works

Herzberg counted recognition among the motivators that truly encourage people to try harder. In Maslow’s terms, recognition can meet esteem needs: being respected, feeling important, and being appreciated. But the same praise can land differently: someone driven by social needs may resent public praise that sets them apart. The Exemplary Leadership module calls this personalize recognition.

Practice 1: Which recognition fits?

Each person is fictional. Read the situation and choose the option that would mean the most to that person.

Practice 2: Which praise is more specific?

Choose the statement that gives more specific praise.

Practice 3: Plan a recognition

Think of someone you could recognize this week. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Takeaway: Meaningful recognition is sincere, specific, and personal: it names what the person did, why it mattered, and comes in a form they value. Scripture puts it simply: “Outdo one another in showing honor” (Romans 12:10, ESV).
