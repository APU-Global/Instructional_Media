Week 5 Interactive: Chronic Illness in Context

Week 5 objective: Identify the social and economic barriers that shape chronic disease management and match them with responsive questions.

Meet Mr. Joseph Redcloud (fictional)

Mr. Redcloud, 58, has Type 2 diabetes. His chart says his blood sugar is "uncontrolled" and that he has "missed follow-up." Chapter 11 reminds us that the subjective assessment is where the most important information is found. Each clue below comes from his conversation with a nurse. Decide which barrier it shows.

Practice 1: Name the barrier

Choose the barrier each clue reveals, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: Ask the right question

Choose the question that would best uncover each barrier. Questions come from Chapter 8's culturally responsive subjective assessment.

Takeaway: A chart that says "uncontrolled" rarely tells the whole story. Cost, food, distance, shame, and inconsistent care can all stand between a patient and good control. Ask open, non-blaming questions, then build a plan that fits the patient's life and culture.
