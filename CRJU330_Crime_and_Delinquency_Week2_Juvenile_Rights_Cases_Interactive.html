Practice 2: A right juveniles have, or not?

Decide whether each protection is guaranteed to juveniles by the U.S. Constitution, according to the Supreme Court decisions in your reading.

Takeaway: The Court gave juveniles most of the adult due process rights (Gault, Winship) but not a jury (McKeiver), required a waiver hearing before transfer (Kent), and, in the sentencing cases from Roper to Montgomery, treated youth as less culpable and capable of change.

Cases and holdings from Fundamentals of Juvenile Justice (Miller & McKee, 2024, docmckee.com), Section 2.2 Constitutional Rights of Juveniles and Section 2.5 Key Supreme Court Cases. Scenarios are fictional.
