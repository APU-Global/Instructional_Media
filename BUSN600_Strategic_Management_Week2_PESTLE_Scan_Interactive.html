Week 2 Interactive: PESTLE Scan

Week 2 objective: Use PESTLE to scan an organization’s general environment and turn each factor into an opportunity, a threat, and a strategic response, as the PESTLE Analysis Assignment asks.

The scenario

Cedar Ridge Bikes is a fictional regional maker of electric bicycles. It imports its battery cells, sells mainly to city commuters, and is planning to expand into two new states. You are scanning its general environment.

P · POLITICAL
Taxes, tariffs and trade, government stability.
E · ECONOMIC
Interest rates, inflation, GDP, unemployment, disposable income.
S · SOCIAL
Demographics and cultural trends.
T · TECHNOLOGICAL
Improvements from scientific advances.
L · LEGAL
Employment, safety, discrimination, antitrust, and intellectual property law.
E · ENVIRONMENTAL / ETHICAL
Natural disasters, pollution, weather, and (in this course) ethical expectations.

Part 1: Sort the signals

Choose the PESTLE segment each news item belongs to.

Part 2: Opportunity or threat?

For Cedar Ridge Bikes, is each factor mainly an opportunity or a threat?

Part 3: So what?

A PESTLE list is not the goal. Choose the best analysis.

Takeaway: Scan all six segments, trace how trends interact, and finish every factor with “so what?”: the opportunity or threat and the strategic adjustment. “Of Issachar, men who had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do, …” (1 Chronicles 12:32, ESV).
