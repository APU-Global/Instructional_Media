Week 2 Interactive: From Agenda to Rule

Week 2 objective: Trace how a law becomes a binding rule, recognize what keeps policy stable and what changes it, and identify how agencies make policy after a law passes.

Part 1: From statute to binding rule

Congress often passes broad goals and leaves the details to an agency. Use the Up and Down buttons to put the rulemaking steps in order, then check your order.

    Part 2: Stable, or about to change?

    Most policy stays stable; significant change usually has a cause. Each scenario is fictional. What best explains what happens?

    Part 3: How agencies make policy

    Agencies make policy by rulemaking, by implementation (applying general policies to specific cases), and by adjudication (deciding disputes). Which is each example?

    Takeaway: A law is only the start. Agencies turn broad goals into rules and decisions, institutions keep most policies stable, and crises, elections, and framing open the windows for change. Analyze a policy’s aftermath by asking what happened at each step.
