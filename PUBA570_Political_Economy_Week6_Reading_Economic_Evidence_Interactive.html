Week 6 objective: Convert nominal values to real values, recognize what GDP leaves out, and audit an AI-generated summary of an economic report.

Part 1: Nominal or real?

To compare dollars across years, divide each amount by the price index for its year (CPI ÷ 100). The CPI values are the ones used in the textbook (2001: 177.1; 2011: 224.9). A fictional county report says the median public-sector salary rose from $40,000 in 2001 to $50,000 in 2011.

2001 real salary = $40,000 ÷ 1.771 = $22,586. 2011 real salary = $50,000 ÷ 2.249 = $22,232. Real change = 22,232 ÷ 22,586 − 1 = −1.6%. The nominal salary rose 25.0%, but its purchasing power fell slightly.
