Attribution Detective

Why did they do that?

Every day we explain other people’s behavior in seconds. Dispositional attributions point to personality and traits. Situational attributions point to circumstances.

You will meet four people in four moments. First, make a snap judgment with the information you have. Then you will get the rest of the story and can change your mind.

Slide toward Personality if you think the person’s character explains it, or toward Situation if circumstances explain it.
