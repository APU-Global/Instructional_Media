Week 7 Interactive: Digital Marketing Analytics and Business Strategy

Week 7 objective: Classify a firm's business-level strategy with Porter's two dimensions, and connect the strategy to the marketing emphasis an analyst should track.

Two questions decide the position

(1) Source of advantage: does the firm win on low cost or on unique, desirable features? (2) Scope: does it serve customers in general or a narrow segment? The answers give cost leadership, differentiation, focused cost leadership, and focused differentiation. A few firms manage best-cost (relatively low prices plus substantial differentiation). A firm that is neither cheaper nor distinctive is stuck in the middle.

Each firm is fictional. Choose its strategy, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: what should the marketing analyst watch?

Match each firm from Practice 1 to the marketing emphasis that fits its strategy.

Takeaway: Strategy comes before metrics. Name the firm's source of advantage and scope, then set objectives with milestones and track, analyze, and optimize the tactics that support that position.
