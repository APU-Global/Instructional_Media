Week 3 Interactive: The Civil Service Reform Act of 1978

Week 3 objective: Apply the federal labor-management statute and the merit system principles created by the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978.

Three parts of the Act

Employee rightsForm, join, or assist a labor organization, or refrain, without fear of penalty or reprisal.
Management rightsMission, budget, staffing, hiring, layoffs, discipline, contracting out, emergencies.
Merit principlesFair and open competition, protection from favoritism, and performance that is corrected when inadequate.

Takeaway: The 1978 Act balances rights: management still runs the agency, employees may organize and bargain, and both sides can commit unfair labor practices.
