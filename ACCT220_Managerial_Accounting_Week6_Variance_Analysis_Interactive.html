Week 6 Interactive: Interpreting Cost Variances

Week 6 objective: Calculate price and quantity variances and decide which variances to investigate.

Jerry's Ice Cream: 210,000 units produced

Standards: 2 pounds of materials at $1.00 per pound and 0.10 direct labor hours at $13 per hour per unit. Actual: 440,000 pounds bought at $1.20 and 399,000 pounds used; 18,900 labor hours at $15 per hour. For each variance, decide whether it is favorable or unfavorable, then check.

Which variances should Jerry's investigate?

Management by exception: set a threshold as a percent of the flexible budget (materials $420,000, labor $273,000) and investigate every variance at or above it, favorable or unfavorable.

    Takeaway: A price variance isolates what was paid; a quantity or efficiency variance isolates how much was used. Investigate significant variances in both directions: a favorable variance can hide a problem, and unexplained variances can be a warning sign of fraud.
