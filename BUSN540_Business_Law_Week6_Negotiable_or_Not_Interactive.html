Takeaway: Negotiability is what lets paper move like cash: a good-faith purchaser can collect even when the original buyer has a defense. In finance, “commercial paper” usually means short-term corporate notes (three days to nine months). Match short-term needs to short-term paper and long-term investment to bonds, stock, or reinvested earnings.
