Welcome to PSYC 430: Cultural Psychology

Research and perspectives on the psychology of culture, studied through the lens of a Christian worldview.

What you will do
Explore diversity across age, race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, language, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, and gender, and examine bias, prejudice, privilege, and oppression.
Your big project
The Culturally Competent Intake is a four-part assignment in Weeks 4 through 7. In Week 3, an interview with a cultural expert gets you ready for it.

Class Learning Objectives

1Analyze research and cultural perspectives in psychology to support clients from diverse communities.
2Evaluate your interactions with various groups regarding culture.
3Examine diverse cultural viewpoints through a Christian worldview within the mental health profession.
4Research cultural factors in multicultural communities, including bias, prejudice, discrimination, privilege, and oppression.
5Apply cultural psychology research to plan relevant mental and behavioral health interactions and services.
