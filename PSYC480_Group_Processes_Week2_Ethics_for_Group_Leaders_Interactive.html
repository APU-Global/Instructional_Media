Week 2 Interactive: Ethics for Group Leaders

Week 2 objective: Apply ACA Code of Ethics standards on competence and group work to decisions group leaders face.

Six standards to know

  • C.2.a Boundaries of competence: practice only within the boundaries of your competence, based on education, training, supervised experience, credentials, and appropriate professional experience.
  • C.2.b New specialty areas: practice in a specialty new to you only after appropriate education, training, and supervised experience, and protect others from possible harm while you build those skills.
  • C.2.d Monitor effectiveness: continually monitor your effectiveness and seek peer supervision to evaluate your efficacy.
  • A.9.a Screening: select members whose needs and goals are compatible with the group, who will not impede the group process, and whose well-being will not be jeopardized.
  • A.9.b Protecting clients: take reasonable precautions to protect group members from physical, emotional, or psychological trauma.
  • B.4.a Group work confidentiality: clearly explain the importance and parameters of confidentiality for the specific group.

Practice 1: Which standard applies most directly?

Each leader is fictional. Choose the ACA standard the situation calls on most directly.

Takeaway: Good intentions are not enough. Ethical group leaders stay within their competence, prepare before entering a new specialty, screen and protect members, explain confidentiality, and seek supervision.
