Week 5 Interactive: Charting Your Career Path

Week 5 objective: Use the BAAP Career Paths guide's four questions to sort career goals, and match fictional graduates to featured career paths.

The four questions

The BAAP Career Paths guide suggests four questions for finding your focus:

  1. Who do you want to serve? (children or teens, families, international populations)
  2. What kind of work do you want to do? (counseling, case management, business, training)
  3. Where do you want to work? (business, ministry, school, healthcare, the justice system)
  4. Goal: Is your primary goal career progression or graduate education?

Practice 1: which question is this?

Each statement comes from a fictional student. Choose the question it answers. Then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: match the graduate to a path

Each fictional graduate describes the work they want. Choose the featured career path from the guide that fits best. Then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: There is no single "right" path. Answer who, what, where, and your goal, then read job postings for the requirements you still need. The hours, skills, and supervisor feedback from your capstone are evidence you can use.

Source: Bachelor of Arts in Applied Psychology: Career Paths (Rev. 9-2026), linked in PSYC 490 Discussion 5. All names and statements are fictional.
