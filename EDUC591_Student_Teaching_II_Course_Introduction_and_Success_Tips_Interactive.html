About the course

Candidate placement with a master teacher for one full term (full school days) in an elementary classroom. Candidates are responsible for designing learning experiences and assessment plans, and for teaching students using best practices.

EDUC 591 is the companion course to EDUC 590 (corequisite). Its requirements are three observations of your teaching, recorded and submitted in GoReact and observed by your Clinical Supervisor, and final evaluations by your Clinical Supervisor and your cooperating teacher. CalTPA is not required to complete this course, but you are expected to make progress on it during your placement.

Course learning outcome

CLO 1 · METHOD OF ASSESSMENT: DEMONSTRATION OF STUDENT TEACHING

Candidates will apply state standards and requirements for the teaching profession and reflect teacher dispositions in a classroom setting.

Credit/No Credit

EDUC 591 is graded on a credit/no credit basis, and every assignment is assessed as Complete or Incomplete. The rubrics give you guidance and feedback rather than a numerical grade. How each observation works

You teach and record three literacy lessons, each with its own required emphases. All three lesson plans go on the CalTPA Part B: Learning Segment Template.

1 · PLAN
Write a complete lesson plan (30–45 minutes) on the Part B template, using the California frameworks, CA Common Core Standards, ELD standards, and what you know about your students.
2 · SHARE
Submit your plans to your Clinical Supervisor so they can be reviewed before you teach. The weekly pages ask for each plan within 48 hours of recording. Schedule the pre- and post-observation conferences.
3 · RECORD
With a video release on file for every student on camera, record the entire lesson and upload it to GoReact.
4 · ANNOTATE
Build your Required Emphases Chart: 4–6 timestamps, what you did, the foundational skill, what it adds and why it matters, and the TPE met.
5 · CONFER
Meet with your Clinical Supervisor after each observation and apply the feedback in your next lesson.
6 · COVER IT ALL
Teach every part of the emphasis, with two or three instances of each, not just one skill.
