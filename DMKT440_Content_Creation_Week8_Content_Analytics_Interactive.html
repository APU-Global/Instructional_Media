Week 8 Interactive: Measuring Content Marketing

Week 8 objective: Use analytics to evaluate content: calculate funnel conversion rates, tell trends from vanity metrics, and choose metrics that fit a medium.

Numbers need a goal and a timeline

Stokes defines a KPI as a metric that shows whether an objective is being achieved. A single number rarely means much; saying 10% more users converted this month than last month shows a trend, while headline figures such as fan counts are vanity metrics. Funnel analysis breaks a conversion into steps, so you can see where visitors drop away: in his example 100 visitors land, 80 continue (80%), 20 take the next step (25%), and 10 convert (50%), an overall rate of 10%.
