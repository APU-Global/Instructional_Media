Week 5 Interactive: Building Cultural Competence

Week 5 objective: Apply the cultural intelligence model and tell a stereotype from a generalization used with care, the skills Discussion 3 asks you to use.

Curiosity, openness, humility

Cultural intelligence is the ability to adapt successfully to unfamiliar cultural settings. Its four parts can be remembered as the ABCs: Acquire knowledge, Build strategic thinking, Contemplate your motivation and assumptions, and Do: adapt your behavior. Stereotypes box people in; generalizations can be valid or faulty, and even valid ones never apply to everyone in a group.

Part 1: which part of the model?

Follow a fictional practicum student, Lena, as she begins work at a family resource center.

Part 2: stereotype or careful generalization?

Decide whether each fictional statement is a stereotype or a generalization used with care.

Takeaway: Before you label a behavior, ask which parts belong to everyone, which to a group, and which to this person. Then work the ABCs with humility.
