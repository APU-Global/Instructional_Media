Week 7 Interactive: Goal Lines and Evidence

Week 7 objective: Create an evidence-based intervention with a measurable goal and a plan for monitoring progress.

A plan you can measure

An intervention plan names the student's strengths and needs, the goals, the services, and how progress will be measured. Curriculum-based measures (CBM) are short probes that sample the year's curriculum. Take the median of the first three probes as the baseline, set a goal from the expected growth rate, and draw a goal line. Probe about every two weeks (never less often than every three), and let the data guide your decisions. "There is no failing in CBM": the probes are formative.
