Week 8 Interactive: Comparing Ethical Frameworks

Week 8 objective: Recognize how each ethical framework reasons about a dilemma and identify its typical weakness.

Five lenses

  • Virtue ethics: the character of the agent and the habits that lead to flourishing.
  • Natural law: objective goods grounded in human nature and discovered by reason.
  • Deontology: duties and actions that are right or wrong by nature, whatever the consequences.
  • Utilitarianism: the greatest good for the greatest number.
  • Care ethics: relationships, empathy, and responsibility for particular people.

Practice 1: Who is speaking?

Maya, a fictional team lead, learns that a friend on her team padded the hours on a timesheet. Choose the framework behind each thought.

Practice 2: Match the blind spot

Each overview names weaknesses. Choose the framework each criticism targets.

Takeaway: Every framework notices some features of a dilemma and misses others. Name the lens you are using, admit its blind spot, and decide which standard has the final word when frameworks disagree.

Sources: Washatka, J. (Ed.). (n.d.). Virtue Ethics, Natural Law Ethics, Deontological Ethics, Utilitarian Ethics, and Ethics of Care, An Overview (PDFs provided in the PHIL 210 course). Maya and her team are fictional.
