Week 8 Interactive: AI Oversight Checkpoints

Week 8 objective: Evaluate the ethical and legal concerns of AI in policing and identify where human verification and accountability belong.

Why checkpoints matter

AI platforms combine large datasets to find patterns quickly, but an association is not proof. The National Policing Institute calls keeping a human in the loop essential: AI outputs should inform, not dictate, decisions, and an officer should verify every critical detail, because current AI can be wrong or context-blind. Police data are also often incomplete or inaccurate. People, not software, are accountable for how a flag is used.

Part 2: Who owns each safeguard?

Takeaway: Treat every AI output as a claim to test. Put people at the decision points: before detention, before anyone is publicly named, and before a tool keeps running after an error. Justice, truth, and human dignity mean no one should lose liberty or reputation because of an unverified data profile.
