Week 2 Interactive: Leading by Coaching

Week 2 objective: Respond to a colleague's capstone proposal the way a coach does: with questions that help them find and own their next step.

What a coach does

The U.S. Office of Personnel Management describes coaching as structured dialogue that helps people deepen their insights and translate them into actions. The coachee drives the agenda and is responsible for the outcome. In peer coaching, each person is both coach and coachee. A useful habit from project management is the next question: after the first answer, ask "Can you tell me more about what you mean?" to uncover what is not yet said.

Practice: choose the coaching response

Each situation comes from a fictional small-group discussion. Choose the reply that coaches: it asks rather than tells and leaves the decision with your colleague. Then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Coaching is inquiry. Ask open questions about direction, research, options, and scope; follow up with the next question; and leave the decision with your colleague. As Proverbs 20:5 says, a person of understanding draws out the purpose in another's heart.
