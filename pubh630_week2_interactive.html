Prioritize Community Needs

Communities usually have more needs than resources. Set your criteria, score six needs, and see how the ranking shifts when community voice joins the data.

Step 1 · Make your criteria explicit

A needs assessment builds a defensible case. Decide how much each criterion matters. Severity measures how serious the need is; feasibility, how doable a response is; and community readiness, how ready people are to act.

Step 2 · Combine data and community voice

Numbers show scale; community input shows meaning.

Right and up is the strongest place to start. Bigger bubbles mean higher community readiness. Select a bubble or a list item for details.
