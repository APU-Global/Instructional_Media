A small BMI sample

A fictional clinic records the BMI of seven adults: 22, 24, 25, 27, 29, 31, 38. Use a calculator or spreadsheet. The mean is the sum divided by n; the median is the middle value of the ordered data; the sample variance divides the sum of squared deviations by n − 1, and the standard deviation is its square root.
