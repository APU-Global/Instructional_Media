Many branches, shared roots

Buddhism developed several major branches. They share core teachings but differ in emphasis and practice.

Theravada

Mahayana

Vajrayana

Awakening is the goal. Enlightenment frees from suffering. The focus is less on an external supreme being than on how to keep the mind peaceful and healthy, and on the practical application of wisdom.
