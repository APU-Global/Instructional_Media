Week 2 Interactive: Make or Buy, and Comparing Global Suppliers

Week 2 objective: Use break-even analysis for a make-or-buy decision, compare two global suppliers on more than price, and sort the factors that shape a sourcing choice. These are the steps Discussion 2 asks you to take.

Key terms

Sourcing is evaluating and hiring businesses to supply goods and services; procurement is actually purchasing them. The make-or-buy question has a strategic side (control of technology, skills, and quality) and an economic side, which is often a break-even analysis.

Break-even volume = difference in fixed costs ÷ difference in cost per unit. Below it, the option with lower fixed costs costs less in total; above it, the option with the lower cost per unit costs less.
