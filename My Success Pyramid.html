My Success Pyramid

ISTU 101 · Reflection activity · 0 points

Where are you on your journey?

This pyramid is the APU Global Student Success Framework, adapted from Maslow. Each level needs to feel steady before the next one can. Nothing here is graded or submitted.

  1. 1 Start at the bottom and tap a level.
  2. 2 Rate how steady it feels today and write a note.
  3. 3 Mark the level where you are now.

↑ Success: Graduation & Employment

The colored fill in each level rises as you rate it steadier.
