Week 6 Interactive: Financial Ratio Analysis

Week 6 objective: Calculate profit margin, efficiency, effectiveness, and financial condition ratios for two companies and use them to compare performance.

Four families of ratios

Profit margin
Gross profit margin = gross profit / sales; net profit margin = net profit / sales.
Management efficiency
Inventory turnover = sales / inventory.
Management effectiveness
Return on assets = net profit / total assets.
Financial condition
Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities; debt-to-equity = total liabilities / total equity.

Round percentages to one decimal place and ratios to two.

Takeaway: A ratio means little alone. Its value comes from trends over time and comparisons with competitors and the industry. Ratios are indicators, not verdicts: different accounting policies can distort comparisons, so read the notes and the MD&A before you conclude.
