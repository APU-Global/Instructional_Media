Week 5 Interactive: Codes of Conduct

Week 5 objective: Distinguish a code of ethics from a code of conduct, predict how different organizational cultures resolve a policy conflict, and recognize practices that close the compliance gap.

Ethics versus conduct

Discussion 3 put it this way: "A code of conduct focuses on one's actions, while a code of ethics focuses on the principles guiding those actions."

Compliance measures the distance between what an organization says it believes and what its members actually do.

Practice 1: principle or rule?

Each line is from a draft document for Sacred Spaces, the fictional furniture company in your project. Is it a principle (code of ethics) or a specific expected behavior (code of conduct)?

Practice 2: one rule, three cultures

A Sacred Spaces showroom adopts a strict appearance policy, and a long-time employee says it conflicts with his religious practice. Which culture produces each response?

Practice 3: closing the compliance gap

Which practices help members actually live the code, and which widen the gap between words and action?

Takeaway: Begin with values, then turn them into specific behaviors with reporting channels and consequences. Your organization's culture (authoritarian, utilitarian, or care) will shape how rules are enforced. A code only works when leaders live it: "be doers of the word, and not hearers only" (James 1:22, ESV).
