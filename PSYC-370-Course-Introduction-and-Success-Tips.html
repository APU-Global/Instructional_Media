Welcome to PSYC 370: Topics in Psychology: Psychopharmacology

An introduction to the behavioral, psychological, and physiological effects of chemicals used to treat psychological disorders, including alternatives to medication, through the lens of a Christian worldview.

What you will do
Identify the indications, actions, side effects, and dangers of psychopharmacological treatments, critically evaluate the research behind them, and compare them with alternative treatments.
Your Signature Assignment
In Week 1 you select a medication. You then research it across four parts that together make up a 14 to 20 page APA-formatted paper.
