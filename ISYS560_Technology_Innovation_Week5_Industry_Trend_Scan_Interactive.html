Week 5 Interactive: Industry Trend Research and Evaluation

Week 5 objective: Organize industry trend signals with PESTEL and sequence the steps for evaluating an innovation.

Why scan?

Managers must constantly scan the external environment for trends and events, and monitor changes in their own industry. PESTEL sorts what you find into six segments: political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal. Not every trend calls for an innovation; the evaluation process helps you decide which ones do.

Practice 1: Sort the signals

Lakeshore Home Health is a fictional home-care agency. Assign each trend signal to its PESTEL segment.

Practice 2: Order the evaluation steps

The Week 5 media "Evaluation Process for Innovation" lists seven steps. Use the arrow buttons to put them in order, then select Check my order.

    Takeaway: Use PESTEL to turn scattered news into opportunities and threats for your industry, and triangulate your evidence with industry, government, and market or scholarly sources. Then evaluate each candidate innovation: begin with the end in mind, engage stakeholders, and keep learning from the results.
