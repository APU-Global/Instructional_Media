Silent Infections

Many infections have no symptoms, so testing is key, and stigma discourages testing and treatment. See how screening, access, and respectful care change how many infections go undiagnosed, then match prevention strategies and check key facts.

Part 1 · How many infections are missed?

Imagine 1,000 people and an infection that often has no symptoms. Each dot is a person. Set the situation, then try two levers: making screening easy and reducing stigma. Prevention works best when it combines accurate information, access to services, and reduced stigma.

Part 2 · Match the prevention strategy

Prevention uses multiple approaches, including testing, vaccination, partner services, and condom use. Education and access to care support all of them.

Part 3 · True or false?

Reduce stigma. Stigma discourages testing and treatment, and infection rates differ across populations because of social factors. Respectful, nonjudgmental care improves outcomes.
