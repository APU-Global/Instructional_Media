About the course

Analysis of theories and strategies of resource allocation as a basis for managing revenues and debt in government, with a focus on budgeting as a tool for controlling both operations and policy at all levels of government. Students gain an understanding of the role of government in a market economy to include the impact of monies spent at the local, state, and federal levels. Students investigate why expenditures are channeled toward certain critical areas in the economy under conditions of market failure.

Throughout the course you connect financial decisions to ethical guidelines from a Christian worldview. Select a card to see each course outcome in detail.

Initial post due by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points (Week 8: initial post due by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
300 words minimum
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the week’s resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
200 words minimum each
Respond to a minimum of three posts. Offer further resources, questions of clarification, or context and insight; avoid simple posts of agreement.
SOURCES
APA formatting, with 2 APA-formatted authoritative or scholarly sources. No direct quotes: put ideas in your own words and cite them.

Discussions are graded with the 3-criterion syllabus rubric: Assignment Requirements (20), Integrative Communication (20), and Written Communication (10), for 50 points.
