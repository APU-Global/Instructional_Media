Week 5 Interactive: Text Reading Fluency

Week 5 objective: Diagnose which part of fluency a reader needs to work on and use word structure (morphology) to support fluent reading, the emphases of Intern Teaching Observation Part 3.

The three parts of fluency

ACCURACY
Reading the words correctly, and using context to confirm or self-correct.
RATE
Reading at an appropriate speed, an indicator of automaticity.
PROSODY
Expression: intonation and meaningful phrasing.

Activity 1: What should this reader work on?

Each reader is fictional and reading on-level text aloud. Choose the part of fluency that most needs attention.

Activity 2: Inflectional or derivational?

Morphology is the system of meaningful word parts. An inflectional ending makes a grammatical change (small → smaller); a derivational affix makes a word that is a different part of speech (happy → happiness). Classify each pair.

Takeaway: Fluency is more than speed. Plan for accuracy, rate, and prosody, use repeated readings so word reading becomes automatic, and teach syllable patterns and morphemes so students can chunk long words instead of reading letter by letter. Mark a timestamp for each part in your Required Emphases chart.
