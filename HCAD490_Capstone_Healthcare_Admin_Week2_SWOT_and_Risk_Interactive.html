Week 2 Interactive: SWOT and Risk Planning

Week 2 focus: define your healthcare problem, analyze its context, and anticipate the challenges your Capstone may face (CLO 1, CLO 4).

Two planning tools

SWOT weighs what is inside an organization against what is outside it. Risk planning prepares responses before trouble arrives.

SWOT
Strengths and weaknesses are internal. Opportunities and threats are external. An opportunity is a condition, not the move you make to use it.
Four risk responses
Avoid it (prevent it), mitigate it (limit the damage), transfer it (pay someone else to accept it, as with insurance), or accept it (know what happens if it occurs).

Practice 1: sort the SWOT

Canyon Lark Community Clinic (fictional) wants to shorten waits for primary care appointments. Sort each finding.

Practice 2: choose the risk response

Canyon Lark's project team lists risks to its new scheduling system. Choose the response each plan describes.

Practice 3: find the critical few

Risks are rated for likelihood and impact. A mitigation plan addresses the risks rated high on both. Choose where each belongs.

Takeaway: Define the problem before choosing a solution. Keep internal and external factors apart, treat SWOT as a source of ideas rather than a verdict, and plan responses for the risks that rate high in both likelihood and impact. That is how a planner counts the cost before building.
