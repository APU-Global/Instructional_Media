Week 2 Interactive: How Psychological Theory and Techniques Align With the Bible

Week 2 objective: Connect common counseling techniques to the theories they come from, so you can judge how each one aligns with Scripture.

Every technique rests on a theory

Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) helps people notice inaccurate or maladaptive thoughts and modify them (cognitive restructuring), and uses exposure to face feared situations instead of avoiding them. Person-centered therapy offers unconditional positive regard while the client leads the conversation. Psychoanalytic therapy explores the unconscious through techniques such as free association and dream analysis. Solution-focused therapy uses the miracle question to help clients imagine a future in which their problems no longer affect their lives. Positive psychology studies what makes life worth living, including gratitude.
