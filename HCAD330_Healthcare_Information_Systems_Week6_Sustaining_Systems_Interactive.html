Week 6 Interactive: Sustaining Health Information Systems

Week 6 objective: Identify strategies for implementing healthcare information systems that support sustainability and improved outcomes (CLO 5).

Three kinds of measures

Outcome measure
Shows how the system affects patients, staff, and other users: the result you want.
Process measure
Shows whether the steps of the change are happening as planned and on schedule.
Balancing measure
Watches for problems the change may cause elsewhere, such as extra workload.

Practice 1: outcome, process, or balancing?

A fictional hospital is adding an electronic medication-reconciliation step at discharge to reduce medication errors after patients go home. Label each measure.

DMAIC: five phases of improvement

Define
Name the problem, goal, and team in a project charter.
Measure
Measure the current process, often on a chart.
Analyze
Find the root causes of poor performance.
Improve
Remove or reduce the root causes.
Control
Monitor the improved process to sustain it.

Takeaway: Sustainability is planned, not hoped for. Track outcome, process, and balancing measures; keep monitoring after the project ends; and budget for the whole life of a system, including support, training, and disruptions, not just the purchase price. Use these ideas as you begin your Leadership Plan for IT Integration.
