Welcome to EDUC 505

Exploration of the methodology and rationale for differentiated instruction, and how to design learning experiences suited to the individual student within a general education classroom. You will explore the diversity of needs (language, culture, learning styles, special needs, development, and interests) and apply what you learn to learning experiences and assessment plans.
