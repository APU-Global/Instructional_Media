Week 3 Interactive: Principles and Decision Steps

Week 3 objective: Identify the ethical principle at stake in a professional situation and apply a step-by-step decision model, as Discussion 2 asks you to do for your own project.

Principles first, then a process

Three core principles guide ethical research and practice: respect for persons (autonomy, free and informed consent), concern for welfare (avoiding unnecessary risk, protecting privacy and confidentiality), and justice (treating people fairly and equitably, without unjustly excluding marginalized groups). Codes such as the APA Ethics Code turn these principles into specific standards, and a decision model helps you apply them when a crossroad appears.

Part 1: which principle is most at stake?

Each situation is fictional. Choose the principle that is most directly at stake.

Part 2: name the decision step

A model from the counseling profession (Corey, Corey, and Callanan) has eight steps: identify the problem; identify potential issues; review ethical guidelines; know laws and regulations; obtain consultation; consider courses of action; list consequences; decide. Which step is each fictional intern taking?

Takeaway: When you meet a crossroad in your project, name the principle, find the standard that speaks to it, and walk the steps, including consultation, before you act.
