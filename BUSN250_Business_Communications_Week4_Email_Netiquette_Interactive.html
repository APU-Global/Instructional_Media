Week 4 Interactive: E-mail Fix-It

Week 4 objective: Recognize Virginia Shea's Rules of Netiquette in action and choose the e-mail practices that make a business message clear and professional, as you will in Discussion 4.

Shea's ten rules of netiquette (Chapter 9)

  1. Remember the human on the other side of the electronic communication.
  2. Adhere to the same standards of behavior online that you follow in real life.
  3. Know where you are in cyberspace.
  4. Respect other people's time and bandwidth.
  5. Make yourself look good online.
  6. Share expert knowledge.
  7. Keep flame wars under control.
  8. Respect other people's privacy.
  9. Don't abuse your power.
  10. Be forgiving of other people's mistakes.

Practice 1: which rule is at work?

Each workplace is fictional. Choose the rule each person follows or breaks, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: fix the e-mail

You need to tell your manager about a possible defect in a fictional product that launches next month. Pick the better choice each time.

Takeaway: Write every business e-mail as if it could be forwarded to anyone: a specific subject line, a respectful greeting, three short paragraphs or fewer, no all caps, and no replies sent in anger. Netiquette is the golden rule online.
