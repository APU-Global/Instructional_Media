Week 3 Interactive: Lesson Plan 1 Skills

Week 3 objective: Tell print concepts, phonological awareness, and phonemic awareness apart, practice phoneme manipulation, and build a complete Required Emphases Chart.

Lesson Plan 1 in brief

Print concepts include following words left to right and page by page, knowing that words are separated by spaces, and naming upper- and lowercase letters. Phonological awareness is awareness of the sound structure of spoken language: words, syllables, onsets and rimes, and phonemes. Phonemic awareness, its most advanced part, is the ability to add, delete, or substitute individual phonemes. Your Required Emphases Chart needs 4-6 entries, each with a timestamp, what the teacher did, the foundational skill, and what the skill adds.

Practice 1: print, phonological, or phonemic?

Each classroom moment is fictional. Choose the skill it shows, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: phoneme manipulation

Say each word aloud and make the change. Phonemes are written between slashes, such as /t/.

Practice 3: is this chart entry complete?

Each row comes from a fictional candidate's Required Emphases Chart. Decide whether it is complete or what it is missing.

Takeaway: Lesson Plan 1 needs all four emphases (print concepts, letters, phonological awareness, phonemic awareness), each taught two or three times. When you annotate, give every entry a timestamp, what you did, the skill, and what it adds.
