Week 3 Interactive: Planning and Conducting Stakeholder Interviews

Week 3 objective: Choose an interview structure and recognize planning and interviewing practices that build trust and produce useful information.

What the readings say

CDC Evaluation Brief 17 names three approaches that differ in structure: informal conversational (no predetermined questions), semi-structured (an outline of topics; wording and order can vary), and standardized open-ended (a set protocol of questions and probes in a fixed order). Good interviews start with planning: a clear focus, an interview guide, a chosen set of interviewees, protected confidentiality, and a pilot test. During the interview, build trust, keep a neutral demeanor, stay in control of the direction, and use probes. EPA's Public Participation Guide adds practical steps such as choosing interviewees by clear criteria, meeting in a familiar setting, sharing topics ahead of time, and transcribing notes promptly.
