Week 7 Interactive: Financial Condition Analysis

Week 7 objective: Interpret healthcare financial statements and communicate what they show about an organization's financial health.

Three tools

Trend analysis: percent change = (current year - base year) ÷ base year. Common-size analysis: each income statement line as a percent of revenue, each balance sheet line as a percent of total assets. Ratio analysis: for example, the current ratio (current assets ÷ current liabilities) for short-term liquidity and debt to assets (total liabilities ÷ total assets) for long-term solvency.

Compared with Year 1, is Lakeside's position stronger or weaker on each measure?

Takeaway: One number rarely tells the story. Revenue grew, but liquidity fell, leverage rose, and labor costs grew faster than revenue. Reading trends, common-size percentages, and ratios together, and beside comparable organizations, gives a fair picture of financial condition.
