Week 4 Interactive: Listening and Responding

Week 4 objective: Develop micro-messaging content that protects customer trust by choosing how a brand should respond to positive, neutral, and negative conversations and to online criticism.

How brands respond

Positive talk: keep the conversation going, because expectations change. Neutral talk: “safe is risky”; a brand nobody discusses may be forgotten. Negative talk: treat it as a broken brand promise and a high priority. Respond personally (no blanket replies), transparently, and honestly. If criticism is false, send evidence in a friendly tone and ask for a correction. If it is true, tell your side, try to take it offline, and apologize quickly and sincerely, offering to make amends.

Practice 2: choose the best response

Choose the response that best follows the recovery steps.

Takeaway: Listen to every kind of conversation, respond personally and honestly, correct what is false with evidence, and own what is true with a sincere apology. Gracious replies protect both your reputation and your witness.
