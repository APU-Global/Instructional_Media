About the course

Role of the supply chain and various marketing elements to create value for the consumer. Students consider alternatives to achieve supply chain sustainability and apply advanced communication techniques to foster operational success. For strategic success, a company must establish a sustainable competitive advantage, and one way to implement strategy is to manage the chain of activities a company uses to create consumer value.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

In Week 8 the rhythm shifts: respond by Wednesday and discuss through Friday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required). Weeks 5–7 ask for at least three outside references, and Week 8 at least two.

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
