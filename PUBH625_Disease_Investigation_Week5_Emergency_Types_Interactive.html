Week 5 Interactive: Public Health Emergencies

Week 5 objective: Classify public health emergencies by type and identify how an emergency operations center coordinates the response.

What you need to know

Disaster. A serious disruption of a community or society, with widespread human, material, economic, or environmental losses, that exceeds the community’s ability to cope using its own resources. It can be natural or human-made.
Humanitarian emergency. A disaster resulting in the need for international support (humanitarian assistance) to meet the basic needs of the affected population.
Complex emergency. A humanitarian emergency associated with fragile states or areas of conflict, in which a total or considerable breakdown of authority has occurred.
Emergency operations center. A public health emergency operations center monitors events from many data sources, improves communication between public health and emergency management, coordinates partners, and scales up or down as needed.
