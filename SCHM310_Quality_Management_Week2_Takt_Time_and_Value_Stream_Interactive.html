Week 2 Interactive: Process Management

Week 2 objective: Use process time measures and the value stream mapping sequence to find where a process loses time.

Four times every process improver should know

THROUGHPUT TIME
From the very first operation until the product is completed, including waiting, inspection, rework, and movement.
LEAD TIME
From when the customer's order is received until the product is completed and ready to ship.
CYCLE TIME
The rate at which the operation actually produces each unit: the time between completed units.
TAKT TIME
The pace needed to meet demand: available production time ÷ demand.

Practice 1: Which time is it?

Each workplace is fictional. Choose the time measure each statement describes, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: Calculate takt time

Enter the takt time in minutes per unit, then compare it with the line's current cycle time. If units come off the line more slowly than the takt time, the line cannot keep up with demand.

Practice 3: Put the value stream mapping phases in order

Use the arrow buttons to arrange the four phases from first to last, then check your order.

    Takeaway: Map and walk the current state first with the people who do the work. Throughput and lead time show how long customers wait; cycle time shows the pace you produce; takt time shows the pace demand requires. The gap between them points to the waste a future state map should remove.
