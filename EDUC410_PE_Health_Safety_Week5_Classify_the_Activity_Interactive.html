Week 5 Interactive: Classify the Activity

Week 5 objective: Describe any activity by its main fitness component, energy system, intensity, and pattern, the four questions in Discussion 5.

Your four lenses

Component. Body composition, cardiorespiratory endurance, flexibility, muscular strength (maximal force for short periods), muscular endurance (lesser force for extended periods).
Energy system. Anaerobic energy is fast but lasts only seconds to a few minutes. Aerobic energy sustains longer efforts. The body uses both at once, so name the one that dominates.
Intensity. The talk test: breathing hard but still talking easily is moderate; only a few words before a breath is vigorous.
Pattern. Continuous activity holds a steady effort. Intermittent activity alternates bursts with rest, which is how children naturally play.

Takeaway: One activity can be described four ways. For Discussion 5, name the main component, say whether it is mainly aerobic or anaerobic, use the talk test for moderate or vigorous, and note whether you move continuously or in bursts. Then think beyond the physical: activity also supports mood, friendships, and focus.
