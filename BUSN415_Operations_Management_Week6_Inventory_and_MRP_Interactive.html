Week 6 Interactive: Inventory, MRP, and ERP

Week 6 objective: Identify the forms of inventory in a process, the reasons organizations hold inventory, and the role of MRP, MRP II, the master production schedule, JIT, and ERP.

Why this matters

Inventory control strikes a balance between two threats: losing production time because materials run out, and wasting money by carrying too much stock. Materials can make up about 50 percent of total manufacturing costs, so planning systems that calculate what is needed, and when, matter a great deal.

Takeaway: Know which inventory you hold and why you hold it. MRP turns the master production schedule into material orders, MRP II and ERP connect that plan to the rest of the organization, and without them, shortages and excess stock become far more likely.
