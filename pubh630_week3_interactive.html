Match real programs to the model that fits, then sharpen weak goals into objectives an evaluator can actually measure.

Part 1 · Match the model

Part 2 · Objective doctor

SMART objectives are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, and Time-bound. For each objective, tap the letters that are missing or weak. Some objectives are already strong, so tap nothing for those.

Part 3 · Build your own SMART objective

Start with the weak goal “Improve healthy eating among teens” and turn it into a measurable target.

Remember

Objectives drive evaluation. A measurable objective tells evaluators what evidence to collect. A weak one makes it hard to say whether the program worked.
Think at multiple levels. Ecological models remind you that individual, community, and policy factors often work together, so a program can target more than one level at a time.
