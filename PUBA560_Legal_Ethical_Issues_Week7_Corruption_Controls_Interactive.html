Week 7 Interactive: Avoiding and Detecting Corruption

Week 7 objective: Treat corruption as a governance risk and choose the controls that prevent, deter, and detect it in public finance.

Three streams of control

Prevention
Lowers the likelihood of wrongdoing: culture, codes, training, and controls that reduce opportunity.
Deterrence
Raises the cost and risk for would-be offenders: independent oversight, predictable sanctions, transparency.
Detection & correction
Finds breaches, remedies harm, and strengthens controls: hotlines, analytics, audits, restitution.

Part 2: Audit the City of Millbrook

A new city council in fictional Millbrook asks you to review its finances. For each red flag, choose the control that best answers it, then select Check Part 2.

Takeaway: No single rule stops corruption. Mature integrity programs weave prevention, deterrence, and detection/correction together, measure results, protect whistleblowers, and rely on the certainty of proportionate punishment under the rule of law more than its severity.
