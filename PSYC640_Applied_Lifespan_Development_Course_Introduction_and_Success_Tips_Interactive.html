About the course

Provides a comprehensive review of research, theory, and application of social, cultural, emotional, biological, cognitive, moral, and spiritual development from birth to death with an emphasis on creating optimal functioning in all areas of development. Each week you follow a persona you create through one stage of life in The Game of Life, and you integrate a Christian worldview into the interventions you design.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points. In Week 8 the rhythm moves up a day: respond by Wednesday and discuss through Friday.

INITIAL POST
300 words
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, drawing on the assigned reading and resources.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150 words each
Post at least three substantive responses: further resources, questions of clarification, or context and insight, not simple agreement.
SCHOLARLY SOURCES
Cite scholarly sources where the prompt requires them (for example, two APA-formatted sources in Discussion 7). Posts made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Engagement (10), Critical Thinking (20), Contributions to Discussion (15), and Communication and Collaboration (5), for 50 points.

Welcome aboard: Lifespan development is how we change and grow throughout life, and this course studies it stage by stage, from heredity and birth to death and dying. Each week you will read a chapter of the open textbook, discuss a real-world question about overcoming obstacles and building grit, and help your persona through a new challenge with evidence-based, faith-integrated interventions.
