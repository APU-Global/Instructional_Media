Week 4 Interactive: Understanding Holy Communion

Week 4 objective: Distinguish the main ways churches understand Holy Communion and choose good sources for researching a tradition.

The words at the center

Jesus “took bread, and when he had given thanks, he broke it and gave it to them, saying, ‘This is my body, which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me’” (Luke 22:19, ESV). Churches have understood these words in three broad ways:

  • Presence: emphasizing “This is my body,” some traditions believe the bread and wine are the actual presence of Jesus’ body.
  • Memorial: emphasizing “Do this in remembrance of me,” some believe the elements are symbolic and taken as a memorial.
  • Middle ground: some seek a position between physical presence and symbolism.
Takeaway: Most differences over Communion come from which part of Jesus’ words a tradition emphasizes: “This is my body” (presence), “Do this in remembrance of me” (memorial), or a middle ground. Research a tradition through its own official sources, and remember Paul’s point: whenever the church shares the meal, it proclaims the Lord’s death until he comes (1 Corinthians 11:26).
