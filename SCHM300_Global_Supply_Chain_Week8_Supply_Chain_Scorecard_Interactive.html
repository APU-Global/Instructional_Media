Week 8 Interactive: Measuring Whether You Keep the Customer Promise

Week 8 objective: Calculate inventory turnover, days of supply, fill rate, and on-time delivery; judge them against a referent; and place measures in the balanced scorecard and the triple bottom line.

Key formulas

  • Inventory turnover = cost of goods sold ÷ average aggregate inventory value (higher means inventory moves faster).
  • Days of supply = average aggregate inventory value ÷ annual cost of goods sold × 365 (lower means less inventory sitting in the building).
  • Fill rate = orders filled from stock on hand ÷ total orders.
  • On-time delivery = orders delivered on or before the due date ÷ total orders.
