Activity 1: Which emphasis is this?

Activity 2: Letter-tile word chain

The Dyslexia Guidelines suggest letter tiles that change one sound at a time. Read the prompt, then select the tile that makes the new word. The tile that changes is outlined in red.

Takeaway: Phonics is a two-way street. Students blend phonemes to decode (read) and segment phonemes to encode (spell), then apply the taught patterns in connected, decodable text. Each word change in a chain is a clear moment to timestamp: name the correspondence taught and what it adds.
