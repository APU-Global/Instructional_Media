Week 3 Interactive: Diagnosing a Quality Management System

Week 3 objective: Use basic quality tools to turn observations about the current state into evidence that points to root causes.

The scenario

Northgate Distribution (a fictional company) used a check sheet for four weeks to record every order error customers reported. Its quality team wants to know where to focus first. A Pareto chart ranks problems from most to least frequent and adds a cumulative percentage line, so managers can put resources on the few causes behind most of the problems.

Takeaway: A diagnostic replaces impressions with evidence. Check sheets collect the counts, Pareto charts show the vital few problems, fishbone diagrams organize possible causes, scatter diagrams test relationships, histograms show distributions, and control charts watch a process over time. Fix the root causes behind the biggest bars first.
