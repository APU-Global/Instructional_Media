Week 6 Interactive: Levels of Organizational Culture

Week 6 objective: Sort evidence of an organization's culture into its three levels and see how leaders build culture.

Three levels of culture

Organizational culture is a system of shared assumptions, values, and beliefs that show employees what is appropriate behavior. Drawing on Schein, the reading describes three levels. Artifacts are the visible, tangible elements: office layout, dress, policies, reward systems. Values are shared principles, standards, and goals. Assumptions are taken-for-granted beliefs about human nature and reality, below conscious awareness.

Takeaway: Start with what you can see (artifacts), then ask which values and assumptions explain them. To build the culture you intend, align every lever: founder values, hiring, onboarding, leader example, and rewards.
