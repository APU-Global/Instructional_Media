Week 2 Interactive: Problem, Purpose, Question

Week 2 focus: Telling the three statements apart and testing a research question before you build your Discussion 2 snapshot and Part 1 of the Virtual Research Study.

Key ideas

Select each card to open it.

Activity 1: Why, what, or how?

A fictional student is studying sleep among nursing students who work night shifts. Label each sentence as the problem statement (why), the research question (what), or the purpose statement (how).

Activity 2: Test the research question

Blackstone lists five features of a good research question. Decide what, if anything, is wrong with each draft.

Activity 3: Build your own question

Fill in the three parts. The builder assembles a draft "how" question and a matching purpose statement you can adapt for Discussion 2.

Your draft will appear here.
Takeaway: The problem statement says why the issue matters, the research question says what you will answer, and the purpose statement says how your study will contribute. A strong question is focused, is not yes/no, and looks at a relationship between concepts.
