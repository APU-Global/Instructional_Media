Week 7 Interactive: Policy Options and Clear Communication

Week 7 objective: Assess policy options against CDC's criteria and apply items from the CDC Clear Communication Index to an executive brief.

Two tools from this week

CDC's Policy Analytical Framework describes and rates each policy option, including keeping the status quo, on three criteria: health impact, feasibility, and economic and budgetary impact.

The CDC Clear Communication Index scores materials on items such as one main message at the top, a call to action for the primary audience, the active voice, words the audience uses, chunks with headings, and explaining what experts know and do not know.

Practice 1: Which criterion does this evidence address?

A fictional proposal recommends a city ordinance requiring shade structures at public bus stops. Choose the criterion each piece of evidence speaks to.

Practice 2: What does this draft need?

Each excerpt comes from a fictional one-page executive brief for the city council. Choose the Clear Communication Index item the excerpt fails.

Takeaway: Show decision-makers that you compared options, including the status quo, on health impact, feasibility, and cost. Then lead your Executive Brief and pitch with one main message and a clear call to action, in the active voice and the audience's own words.

Concepts from Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (2024), CDC's Policy Analytical Framework, and the CDC Clear Communication Index and its Full Index Score Sheet (2014). The ordinance, costs, and excerpts are fictional.
