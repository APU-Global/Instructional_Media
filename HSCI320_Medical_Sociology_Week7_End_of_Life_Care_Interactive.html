Week 7 Interactive: End-of-Life Care and Advance Care Planning

Week 7 objective: Use the language of advance care planning and recognize the physical, emotional, spiritual, and practical needs at the end of life.

Why plan ahead?

The National Institute on Aging reports that in one study, people guessed nearly one out of three end-of-life decisions for their loved one incorrectly. Conversations and advance directives help the patient's own wishes guide care, and Chapter 14 reminds us that end-of-life care includes psychological, social, spiritual, and practical support, which may look different across cultures.

Takeaway: Advance care planning keeps the patient's voice at the center when families disagree. Good end-of-life care attends to physical, emotional, spiritual, and practical needs, respects cultural and religious practices, and is built on a relationship with each patient and family.
