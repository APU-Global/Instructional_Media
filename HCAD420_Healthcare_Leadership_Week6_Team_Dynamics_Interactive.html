Week 6 Interactive: Self-Awareness and Team Dynamics

Week 6 objective: Apply leadership principles about self-awareness and team development to improve healthcare operations (CLO 5).

Know yourself, then lead the team

Authentic leadership
Self-awareness, balanced processing, and relational transparency.
Perception
Biases distort how we see ourselves and others; awareness is the defense.
Team stages
Forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning. The leader moves from directing to coaching to delegating.

Practice 1: what stage is the team in?

A fictional hospital team. Identify the Tuckman stage in each moment.

Practice 2: spot the perception trap

Each fictional example shows a bias that can distort a leader's judgment. Name it.

Practice 3: authentic leadership in action

Which component of authentic leadership does each fictional leader show?

Takeaway: Self-aware leaders check their perceptions against evidence, share honestly, and adjust their style to the team's stage of development. Expect storming after any disruption, and lead the team through it.
