Week 5 Interactive: AI and Data Ethics Safeguards

Week 5 objective: Identify the ethical principle at stake when evaluation data is used with AI and choose safeguards that protect participants, as Discussion 5 asks.

Four principles, four mechanisms

Muceku and Muceku (2025) map each principle of ethical data use to an operational mechanism. They are interdependent: transparency enables accountability, and fairness requires privacy protections.

TRANSPARENCY
Algorithmic explainability
FAIRNESS
Bias detection audits
ACCOUNTABILITY
Algorithmic impact assessment
PRIVACY
Data minimization and contextual integrity

Part 2: Which safeguard fits?

When the University of Washington graduate medical education office used AI to review program evaluations, it built in several safeguards. Choose the one that most directly answers each risk.

Takeaway: Evaluations should "safeguard the dignity, rights, safety, and privacy of participants" (CDC, 2024). Redact before you upload, keep a human in the loop, document the process, and assign responsibility. Guarding confidences is part of being trustworthy: "he who is trustworthy in spirit keeps a thing covered" (Proverbs 11:13, ESV).
