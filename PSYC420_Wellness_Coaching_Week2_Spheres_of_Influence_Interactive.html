Week 2 Interactive: Levels of Influence on Wellness

Week 2 objective: Use the ecological perspective to identify the personal, social, community, and societal influences on a person's wellness.

Four levels of analysis

Community psychology's ecological perspective explains thoughts, feelings, and behavior at several levels at once, instead of looking only at the individual. Because everything is connected (the principle of interdependence), a change at one level ripples into the others.

Personal levelCharacteristics of the person, such as personality traits.
Social levelClose relationships, such as family upbringing.
Community levelShared settings and their cultural norms.
Societal levelThe wider society, such as public policies and laws.
