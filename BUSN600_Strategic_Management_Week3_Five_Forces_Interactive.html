Week 3 objective: Judge the strength of each competitive force and decide when Porter’s Five Forces or the Ansoff Matrix fits a strategic question, as Discussion 2 asks.

The five forces at a glance

Five forces analysis estimates how much profit potential an industry offers. The stronger the forces, the weaker the profit potential.

RIVALRY
Competition among firms with similar products.
NEW ENTRANTS
Firms that may join the industry.
SUBSTITUTES
Offerings from other industries that fill the same need.
SUPPLIERS
Providers of the inputs the industry needs.
BUYERS
Purchasers of the industry’s products.
