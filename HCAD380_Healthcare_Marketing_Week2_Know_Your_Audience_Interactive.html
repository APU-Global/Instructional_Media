Week 2 Interactive: Understanding Healthcare Audiences

Week 2 objective: Identify how healthcare marketers segment audiences and which factors influence a health decision.

Four ways to slice a market

Behavioral: what benefits people want and how they use the service. Demographic: age, income, family life cycle, education, ethnicity. Geographic: region, size of town, population density. Psychographic: activities, interests, values, lifestyles. Behind every choice sit situational, personal, psychological, and societal influences.

Takeaway: Good tailoring starts with knowing your audience. Combine behavioral, demographic, geographic, and psychographic bases, and remember that time, life stage, past experience, family, and culture all shape whether someone seeks care.
