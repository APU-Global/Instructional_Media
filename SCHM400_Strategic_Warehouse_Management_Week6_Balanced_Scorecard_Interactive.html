Week 6 Interactive: KPIs and the Balanced Scorecard

Objective: Organize fulfillment center KPIs into the four balanced scorecard perspectives, write SMART objectives, and weigh the ethics of measuring workers.

Four perspectives

CustomerSatisfaction and retention
Internal processesProduction, innovation, future productivity
Learning and growthEmployee satisfaction and retention; information systems
FinancialOperating costs, return on investment

Part 1: Build the scorecard

Summit Fulfillment is a fictional e-commerce fulfillment center. Place each measure in the balanced scorecard perspective it belongs to.

Part 2: Is the objective SMART?

SMART objectives are specific, measurable, attainable, realistic, and time bound. Decide whether each objective meets the test.

Part 3: Measure people justly

Choose the best answer.

Takeaway: A KPI earns its place only if it tracks a strategic goal. The balanced scorecard keeps customer, internal process, learning and growth, and financial measures in view together; SMART objectives turn them into action; and just, honest measurement protects the people behind the numbers.
