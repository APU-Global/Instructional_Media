Week 3 Interactive: Conflict Patterns and the Fear Cycle

Week 3 objective: Recognize destructive conflict patterns and practice responses that interrupt the fear cycle.

Why fights repeat

Fields and Burns describe hurt feelings as awakened fears: a spouse pushes a hot button, a relational fear (rejection, disconnection, failure) wakes up, and the reaction pushes the other spouse's button. Wiley names four destructive patterns that often appear in conflict:

  • Escalation: each reply is more negative than the last, and intensity rises quickly.
  • Withdrawal (stonewalling): one partner "checks out" while the other wants to stay engaged.
  • Negative interpretation: believing the worst about the partner's motives.
  • Put-down: an insult, ridicule, or sarcasm aimed at the partner's character.

Takeaway: Most repeated fights are not really about the dishes or the bill. Look for the fear underneath, notice your own pattern, and choose one specific response you can practice.
