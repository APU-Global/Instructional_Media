Week 2 Interactive: SWOT Analysis and Root Causes

Week 2 objective: Classify organizational analysis findings with a SWOT and choose questions that uncover root causes from employees.

The four quadrants

  • Strengths: internal, positive attributes of the business.
  • Weaknesses: internal, negative attributes of the business.
  • Opportunities: external, positive factors that could help the business.
  • Threats: external, negative factors that could hurt the business.

Ask first: is this about the business itself (internal) or its environment (external)? Then: does it help or hurt?

Practice 1: sort the findings

Ridgeline Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle shop whose sales have dropped for two years. Choose the quadrant for each finding from its organizational analysis, then select Check my answers.

Practice 2: ask the employees

A SWOT lists conditions; employees can explain them. For each finding, choose the question most likely to uncover a root cause.

Takeaway: A SWOT sorts findings into internal strengths and weaknesses and external opportunities and threats. It shows what is happening; the people closest to the work help explain why. Involving them while the problem is still being diagnosed also builds support for the change that follows.
