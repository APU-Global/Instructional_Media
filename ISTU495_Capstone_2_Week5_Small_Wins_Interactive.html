Week 5 Interactive: Challenge the Process

Week 5 focus: Break a change into stages and small wins, understand why people resist, and set goals that keep momentum going, for Discussion 5 and Capstone Part 5.

Three ideas to use

Lewin’s model: unfreeze (prepare people), change (carry out the plan), refreeze (make it the new normal). Small wins: breaking a large change into phases creates smaller targets, and visible progress keeps people going. SMART goals: specific, measurable, aggressive, realistic, and time-bound, with feedback along the way.

Practice 1: Which stage is this?

Each organization is fictional. Choose the stage of Lewin’s model each action belongs to.

Practice 2: Why are they resisting?

Resistance is feedback. Choose the most likely reason behind each comment.

Practice 3: Is this small win a SMART goal?

Choose SMART goal or the element that is missing.

Takeaway: “One hop at a time” is a leadership strategy. Prepare people before you change anything, listen to resistance for what it tells you, and break the path into small, SMART wins that people can see and celebrate.
