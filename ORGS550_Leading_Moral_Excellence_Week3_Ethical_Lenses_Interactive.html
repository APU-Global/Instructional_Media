Week 3 Interactive: Ethical Perspectives and Leadership Theories

Week 3 objective: Recognize consequence-based, duty-based, and character-based reasoning, and match leader behavior to transformational, servant, authentic, and leader-member exchange theories.

The lenses

  • Consequences (consequentialism): whether an act is right depends only on its consequences.
  • Duty or rule (deontology): act on a rule you could will as a universal law, and never treat people merely as a means.
  • Character (virtue ethics): ask what an honest, fair, or generous person would do.

Normative leadership theories add the leader's role: transformational (charisma, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, individualized consideration), servant (serving the needs of others), authentic (self-aware and true to one's values), and leader-member exchange (trust-based relationships with each follower).

Takeaway: No single lens catches everything. Test a decision by its consequences, by the duties it honors or breaks (could it be a universal rule? does it use anyone merely as a means?), and by the character it builds, and pair that reasoning with a leadership style that serves and respects followers.
