Clara's Case Notes:

Analysts — including psychologist Irving Janis, who coined the term "groupthink" partly from this case — point to a breakdown in Evaluating Options. Dissenting concerns were minimized to preserve group harmony, and risks weren't seriously stress-tested.

Clara's Core Takeaway

"Six situations. Six different breakdowns. But one thread runs through all of them: a structured process only works if every stage gets taken seriously — especially the uncomfortable ones, like raising doubts or challenging the group's momentum."

As you move into your own Signature Assignment this week, you won't have hindsight like we just did. But you can build in something a lot of these teams were missing: a pause to ask, "Which stage are we actually in right now — and are we doing it well?"
