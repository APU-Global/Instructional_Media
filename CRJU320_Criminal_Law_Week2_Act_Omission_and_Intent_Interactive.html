Week 2 Interactive: Act, Omission, and Intent

Week 2 objective: Identify when failing to act is a crime, and match conduct to the Model Penal Code mental states.

The building blocks

With exceptions, every crime has a criminal act (actus reus), a criminal intent (mens rea), and concurrence of the two. The act must be voluntary. An omission to act is criminal only when the law imposes a duty through a statute, a contract, or a special relationship (some states add a duty to rescue someone you put in peril). Takeaway: Ask two questions about any situation: Was there a voluntary act, or an omission where the law imposed a duty? And what did the person know or intend? From purposely down to negligently, the less awareness, the lower the culpability, and strict liability crimes need no intent at all.
