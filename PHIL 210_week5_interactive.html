The Categorical Imperative Lab

Some actions are right or wrong by nature, whatever their consequences. Put four proposed actions through Kant's two tests, then face a case where two duties collide.

Part 1 · Choose an action to test

Test 1 · Could everyone do this?

Imagine the rule behind your action becoming a universal law. Press the button and watch the crowd.

Test 2 · Does it respect the person?

Kant also asks whether you treat people as ends in themselves, never merely as means.

Verdict

Notice: the verdict never asked whether the outcome would turn out well. Duty creates rules that uphold moral actions regardless of consequences.

Part 2 · When duties collide

Rules bring clarity and protect persons, but they can conflict. You promised a friend to keep a secret. The secret turns out to be a plan to seriously harm someone else. Which duty will you honor?
