Week 2 Interactive: Read the Statements

Week 2 objective: Know what each government financial statement tells a leader, compute a program's net cost, and classify fund balance.

Two lenses on one government

GOVERNMENT-WIDE STATEMENTS

Accrual basis, long-term view. The Statement of Net Position (assets + deferred outflows = liabilities + deferred inflows + net position) and the Statement of Activities (expenses by function, program revenues, and the net cost each function leaves for general revenues).

FUND STATEMENTS

Modified accrual basis, fiscal accountability: did the government collect and spend money as taxpayers directed? The governmental funds balance sheet (assets + deferred outflows = liabilities + deferred inflows + fund balance) and the statement of revenues, expenditures, and changes in fund balances.

Governmental GAAP sorts fund balance into five classes by how strongly its use is restricted. Choose the class for each (fictional) item.

Takeaway: Government-wide statements show whether the overall position is improving and what services cost; fund statements show whether money was collected and spent as directed. Unassigned general fund balance is one of the most closely watched numbers, and leaders keep asking the "Goldilocks" question: too large, too small, or just right?
