Association Is Not Causation

Regression can show that two variables move together, and it can predict. It cannot, on its own, prove that one causes the other. Move the points and see what r and the line really tell you.

Part 1 · Move the points

Think of x as hours studied and y as exam score. Drag any point (or focus it and use the arrow keys). The correlation coefficient r ranges from −1 to 1; values near 0 indicate little linear relationship.
