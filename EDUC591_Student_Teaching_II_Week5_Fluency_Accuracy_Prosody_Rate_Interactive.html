Week 5 Interactive: Text Reading Fluency

Week 5 objective: Identify which part of fluency a reader needs, sort the six syllable types, and choose phrasing that supports meaning.

Lesson Plan 3 in brief

The California Dyslexia Guidelines define oral reading fluency as "the accuracy, speed, and prosody (intonation and meaningful phrasing)" of a student's reading. The standards ask students to read on-level text "with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression on successive readings." Knowing the six syllable types lets students chunk long words, and syntax is "critical for learning to read with prosody" because prosody means grouping words into meaningful phrases.

Practice 1: accuracy, prosody, or rate?

Each note comes from a fictional fluency check. Choose the part of fluency it describes.

Practice 2: sort the syllable types

Choose the syllable type for each word or syllable.

Practice 3: phrase it

Slashes (/) mark where a reader pauses. Choose the reading that groups words into meaningful phrases.

Takeaway: In Lesson Plan 3, plan a moment for accuracy, prosody, and rate, use syllable types and morphology for longer words, and have students reread so they can phrase for meaning.
