Part 2 · Verify the AI output

AI-supported tools can assist with information and decisions, but their output is a starting point to verify with credible sources, and human oversight is an important safeguard. An AI assistant gave this (fictional) summary about a clinic's telehealth program. Decide how to treat each statement.

“The Riverside clinic offers telehealth visits for all patients. It has 220 beds. Telehealth lets patients receive care remotely. The program is covered by every Medicaid plan in the state. Patients should expect a short wait.”
