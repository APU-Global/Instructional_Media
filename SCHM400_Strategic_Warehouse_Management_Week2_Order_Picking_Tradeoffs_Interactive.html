Week 2 Interactive: Order Picking Trade-Offs

Objective: Identify the four planning decisions that drive picking travel and evaluate the trade-offs between speed, safety, and flexibility.

Four decisions that drive travel

LayoutBlocks, picking aisles, and cross aisles
RoutingThe tour and the retrieval sequence
BatchingGrouping or splitting customer orders
Storage assignmentWhich item goes in which location

Part 1: Which planning decision is it?

Ridgeline Supply is a fictional online retailer with a picker-to-parts warehouse. For each change the manager is considering, choose the planning problem it belongs to, then select Check my answers.

Part 2: Weigh the trade-off

Each question describes a trade-off from this week's research. Choose the best answer.

Takeaway: Travel is the biggest target in picker-to-parts picking, and four decisions shape it: layout, routing, batching, and storage assignment. Every improvement trades something (speed, safety, flexibility, or cost), so name the trade-off and design for conditions that change.
