Week 5 Interactive: Digital Metrics

Week 5 objective: Calculate common web and campaign metrics from raw counts and use them to decide what to improve first.

The formulas

  • Click-through rate = clicks ÷ impressions
  • Bounce rate = single-page visits ÷ entry pages
  • Page views per visit = page views ÷ visits
  • Conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors who started
  • Cost per acquisition (CPA) = ad cost ÷ acquisitions it produced

Takeaway: Counts become useful when you turn them into ratios and tie them to a goal. Read each step of the funnel, not just the overall rate: the step that leaks most is where a better landing page, clearer copy, or a test will pay off first.
