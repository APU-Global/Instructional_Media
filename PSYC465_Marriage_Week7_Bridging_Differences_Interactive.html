Week 7 Interactive: Resolving Cultural and Religious Differences

Week 7 objective: Match cultural and religious differences in marriage to the problem resolution strategies in this week's reading.

Four problem resolution strategies

Hammond places the root of most disagreements in each partner's values, beliefs, needs, and wants, and offers four strategies:

  • Negotiate a win-win: an outcome in which both partners' core needs are met.
  • Agree as a gift: freely yield on something, with no strings attached. Use it rarely, so no one becomes a martyr.
  • Live with differences: accept a difference that will not change and let it add richness to the relationship.
  • Change yourself: redefine what something means to you instead of asking your spouse to change.
