Week 8 Interactive: Ethical Decision-Making in AI-Enabled Hiring

Week 8 objective: Spot fair and risky screening practices and apply a step-by-step ethical decision process to an AI-enabled hiring tool.

Practice 1: fair screening or a risk?

A selection test should fit the job, predict performance, and not discriminate. Decide whether each fictional screening practice supports fair selection or raises a fairness or legal risk.

Practice 2: walk a decision through the ethical process

Fictional case: Northgate Logistics is offered "ClearView," software that scores facial expressions in asynchronous video interviews and screens out the lowest scorers before any person reviews them. You must decide whether and how to use it. Choose the best response at each step.

Step 1: Assess the situation

Step 2: Identify the stakeholders

Step 3: Weigh the alternatives

Step 4: Decide and monitor

Takeaway: Faster screening is not automatically fairer. Every selection test should fit the job, predict performance, and avoid protected characteristics, and an ethical decision moves through clear steps: assess the situation, identify the stakeholders, weigh the alternatives (including the newspaper test), decide, and monitor the outcomes. Because employees look to leaders for cues, the leader who adopts the tool owns its fairness.
