About the course

This course is one of a two-course capstone experience for students in the Bachelor of Interdisciplinary Studies program. In this class, students draw from their accumulated education and professional experience to create a solution or improve a process for a situation in their workplace, ministry, or interest area, integrating insights from both of their emphases. Weekly discussions connect faith integration with Christian leadership.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points (in Week 8: respond by Wednesday, discuss through Friday). Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
