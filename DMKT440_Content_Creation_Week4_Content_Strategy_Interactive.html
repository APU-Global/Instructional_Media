Week 4 Interactive: Building a Content Strategy

Week 4 objective: Explain how individual pieces of content fit a content marketing strategy, and draft the five building blocks of a strategy for a brand.

One piece is not a strategy

No matter how good a single piece of content is, it needs to be woven together with others. The course's Content Marketing Strategy Template asks five questions: your goals, who you'll help and how, the topics you'll cover, your formats and publishing frequency, and your promotional channels. Stokes adds that formats should match objectives and that a strategy balances stock and flow content.

Practice 1: stock or flow?

Stokes advises balancing stock content (bigger assets that cost more and age well) with flow content (quick, lower-cost pieces). Sort each piece for a fictional bakery.

Practice 2: match the format to the objective

Different formats do different jobs along the customer journey. Choose the objective each fictional piece serves best.

Practice 3: draft a one-page content strategy

Fill in the five steps of the course's Content Marketing Strategy Template for a fictional business (or the brand you audited). The checklist updates as you type and checks completeness only; the quality of the ideas is yours to judge.

    Takeaway: A strategy starts from goals and an audience, then chooses topics, formats, and channels that serve them. Balance lasting stock content with quick flow content, and commit the plan to the Lord (Proverbs 16:3).
