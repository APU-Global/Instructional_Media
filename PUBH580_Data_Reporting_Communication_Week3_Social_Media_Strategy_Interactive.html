Week 3 Interactive: Social Media Campaign Strategy

Week 3 objective: Apply CDC's six-step social media strategy and key message criteria to a campaign plan.

The scenario

Hope Valley Heart Ministry, a fictional Christian charity, has hired you to plan a spring campaign inviting older adults to free blood pressure checks. Interviews with the director and church volunteers come first (open-ended questions, with probes when an answer opens a new lead). Then you build the plan.

Practice 1: put the strategy steps in order

Use the arrow buttons to move each step. Then select Check my order.

    Practice 2: is it a strong key message?

    The Playbook says key messages are few (limit 3), short (1 to 2 sentences), memorable, focused, consistent, and tailored to the audience. Judge each draft.

    Practice 3: match the platform

    Use the Playbook's platform descriptions (figures as printed in the 2018 Playbook) to pick the best fit.

    Takeaway: Strategy runs audience → platforms → goals → voice and tone → content strategy → calendar. Keep to three or fewer short, tailored key messages with an action step, and choose platforms where your audience already has its conversations.

    Steps, key message criteria, and platform descriptions from CDC and ATSDR, Health Communication Playbook (2018), "Identify Key Messages and Talking Points," "Social Media Platforms and Tools," and "Social Media Strategies and Calendars"; interview approach from Wolff et al., "Collecting and Analyzing Qualitative Data," Chapter 10 of The CDC Field Epidemiology Manual (2024). The charity and messages are fictional.
