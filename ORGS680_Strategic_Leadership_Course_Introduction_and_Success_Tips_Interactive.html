About the course

Exploration of advanced topics in strategic leadership concepts using a cross-disciplinary case study approach. Students develop and enhance their strategic decision-making skills by proposing solutions to complex scenarios in for-profit and non-profit sectors with the goal of assisting organizations in gaining and sustaining their competitive advantages in the marketplace.

Welcome aboard: Strategic leaders set a vision, turn it into plans people carry out, communicate so stakeholders buy in, and make sound decisions when the future is uncertain. In this course you assess and plan your own strategic leadership development, build an innovation matrix for a real industry, and share a leader’s transition story in a Podcase or TED Talk, while evaluating strategy from a Christian worldview. Start your action plan conversations in Week 1, and ask questions in Ask the Prof whenever you need help.
