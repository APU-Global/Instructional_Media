Week 3 Interactive: Local System Pressure Points

Week 3 objective: Identify which agencies serve your community and recognize the local factors that create pressure points in a criminal justice system.

Explore: who polices your community?

Select an agency type to see its jurisdiction and typical responsibilities.

Select a card.

Practice 1: which agency handles it?

Each situation is fictional. Choose the agency type most likely to handle it.

Practice 2: name the local factor

Local agencies’ effectiveness is shaped by local factors. Choose the factor at work in each fictional county or city.

Apply: trace your own pressure point

Use these prompts to plan Discussion 3. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Takeaway: Know which agency serves each part of your community and who its leaders answer to. Then look for the local factors (funding, staffing, community trust, political priorities, and crime patterns) that create strain, and trace how that strain spreads to courts, corrections, and the community.

Agency descriptions from the Week 3 Overview “Understanding the Different Levels of Policing” and local factors from Local, State, and Federal Criminal Justice Systems (CRJU 490 course media, Azusa Pacific University Global, n.d.). Scenarios, places, and agencies in the practice items are fictional.
