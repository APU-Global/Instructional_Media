Week 4 Interactive: Kotter Stage Sorter

Week 4 objective: Use Kotter's eight stages and Lewin's three-stage model to analyze a change, as Change Initiative Assessment Parts 2A and 2B ask you to do for the Fortis case.

Part 1: Name the Kotter stage

A fictional regional credit union, Riverbend Credit Union, is moving its members from branch paperwork to a mobile banking platform. Match each action to Kotter's eight stages, as summarized in Saylor's Organizational Behavior.

Part 2: Kotter inside Lewin

Lewin's model has three stages. Which stage does each action belong to?

Takeaway: Lewin gives the map; Kotter adds the leadership work inside it. Remove obstacles, plan short-term wins, and build on them. “You and the people with you will certainly wear yourselves out, for the thing is too heavy for you. You are not able to do it alone.” (Exodus 18:18, ESV).
