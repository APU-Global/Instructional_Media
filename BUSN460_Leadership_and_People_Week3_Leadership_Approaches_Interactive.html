Week 3 Interactive: Contemporary Leadership Approaches

Week 3 objective: Distinguish transformational, transactional, servant, and authentic leadership by what leaders actually do.

Four contemporary approaches

Transformational leaders align employees' goals with the leader's, using charisma, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, and individualized consideration. Transactional leaders exchange resources for the right behaviors through contingent rewards and active or passive management by exception. Servant leaders serve the needs of others, develop employees, and care for the wider community. Authentic leaders stay true to themselves through self-awareness and integrity.

Practice: name the approach

Choose the approach each fictional leader is using, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Research supports using both transformational and transactional methods, but not passive management by exception. Servant leadership adds an explicit focus on ethics, community, and self-sacrifice, and authentic leadership rests on self-awareness and integrity.
