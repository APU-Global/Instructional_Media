Week 4 Interactive: From Data to Decisions

Week 4 objective: Propose data-driven solutions for improving patient care and operational efficiency (CLO 3).

Three ideas to apply

Data
Facts and statistics in their raw form. Alone, data tells you very little.
Information
Data that is processed and presented so it becomes meaningful: data with relevance and purpose.
Knowledge
The insight people bring from experience and expertise that turns information into stronger decisions.

The analytics toolkit

Canned report
A regular summary in a fixed format.
Ad hoc report
A report users build on the fly, choosing fields and ranges.
Dashboard
A graphical, heads-up display of key performance metrics.
Data mining
Computers search large data sets for hidden patterns and build models.

Practice 2: which tool fits the need?

Choose the business intelligence tool each leader needs.

Before you trust a model

Data mining works only when (1) the data is clean and consistent and (2) the patterns in it reflect current and future trends. Beware of random flukes, test a model on data it was not built from, and staff the team with information technology, statistics, and business knowledge.

Practice 3: use it or be cautious?

Decide whether each analysis is ready to use (with ongoing monitoring) or calls for caution.

Takeaway: Data becomes useful only when it is turned into information and combined with knowledge. Match the tool to the question, and before acting on any analysis, including one from an AI tool, ask whether the data is clean and consistent, whether the pattern still holds, and whether it makes sense to people who know the work.
