Week 3 Interactive: Formative Assessment and Feedback

Week 3 objective: Distinguish formative from summative assessment and choose feedback that helps students learn and helps you teach.

Two purposes, one goal

Formative assessment (assessment for learning) is a process used during instruction that provides feedback to adjust teaching and learning; it is "not a tool or an event." Summative assessment (assessment of learning) gauges achievement after learning, such as at the end of a unit or year. Good feedback answers three questions: Where am I going? Where am I now? What are my next steps?

Part 1: formative or summative?

Each classroom is fictional. Decide how the evidence is being used.

Part 2: choose the stronger feedback

For each piece of fictional student work, choose the comment that does more to move learning forward.

Takeaway: Plan where you will gather evidence during the lesson, how students will show what they know in more than one way (talking, drawing, manipulatives, writing, home language), and what specific next step each comment will give.
