What is MATH 125?

College Algebra is primarily a study of functions (linear, quadratic, polynomial, inverse, exponential and logarithmic) and their graphs. You will also solve equations and inequalities, work with matrices, and explore sequences and series.

Prerequisite: appropriate placement test score, SAT 540 / ACT 23 math, or MATH 099 Intermediate Algebra.
