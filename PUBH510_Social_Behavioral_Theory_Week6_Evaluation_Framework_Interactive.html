Week 6 Interactive: Evaluating Programs for High-Risk Populations

Week 6 objective: Apply the steps of program evaluation and recognize threats that make findings about special populations hard to trust.

The six steps of the original CDC framework

Engage community members and other affected parties · Describe the program · Focus the evaluation questions and design · Gather credible evidence · Develop and justify conclusions · Use findings and share lessons learned. Four standards test the work: utility, feasibility, propriety, and accuracy.

Practice 1: which step?

A fictional county program helps people leaving jail connect with health care. Match each activity to its step in the framework.

Practice 2: spot the threat

Each fictional evaluation has a problem. Choose the threat to internal validity it shows.

Takeaway: With high-risk and special populations, the strongest design is not always the most ethical one. Engage the people affected, choose a feasible design that does not withhold needed help, and plan for history, selection, and attrition before you claim the program worked.
