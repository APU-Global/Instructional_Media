Week 2 Interactive: Character Strengths and Virtues

Week 2 objective: Connect the 24 VIA character strengths to the six core virtues and apply the research on signature and lesser strengths to your VIA results.

The VIA classification

Peterson and Seligman proposed six core virtues found across the writings of philosophers and spiritual leaders. Character strengths are the distinguishable routes to each virtue:

  • Wisdom and knowledge: cognitive strengths that involve gaining and using knowledge.
  • Courage: emotional strengths that use the will to reach goals in the face of opposition.
  • Humanity: interpersonal strengths of "tending and befriending" others.
  • Justice: civic strengths that underlie healthy community life.
  • Temperance: strengths that protect against excess.
  • Transcendence: strengths that forge connections to the larger universe and provide meaning.
