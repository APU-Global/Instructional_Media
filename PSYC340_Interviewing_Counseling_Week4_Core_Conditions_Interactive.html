Week 4 Interactive: The Person-Centered Relationship

Week 4 objective: Tell reflective, accepting responses apart from responses that judge or direct, and choose replies that show empathy, unconditional positive regard, and genuineness, as Discussion 4 and Partner Roleplay #1 ask.

Quick guide

Accurate empathy
Understand the client’s personal frame of reference and convey it back through reflective listening.
Unconditional positive regard
Warmth and acceptance. Never condemn or criticize the client for what they have done or thought.
Genuineness
A genuine, egalitarian relationship in which the counselor is nonjudgmental and empathetic.

Person-centered therapy is non-directive. The counselor does not try to change thoughts or behavior directly and offers no judgment or interpretation.

Score: 0

Takeaway: Empathy is a skill you can see and hear: reflect the client’s meaning and feeling, accept the person without condemning, and stay genuine. Research suggests that what counts is whether the client experiences you as empathic.
