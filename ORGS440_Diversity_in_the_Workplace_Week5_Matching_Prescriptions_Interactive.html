Week 5 Interactive: Prescribing Treatments

Week 5 objective: Match DEI and belonging treatments to the root causes they address, and weigh the evidence on diversity training.

Fit the treatment to the cause

A diversity program should answer a few guiding questions first: What problem is it meant to solve? How will we know it is effective? Does its content reflect the goals to be achieved? Options from this week’s readings include:

  • Broaden recruiting sources: post openings in many places and do not rely only on word of mouth or referrals.
  • Formal mentoring: assign mentors so everyone has one, since informal mentoring tends to pair people who are alike.
  • Set pay schedule: set pay for new and promoted employees instead of leaving it to each manager.
  • Diverse screening committee: insist on diverse screening panels and extra outreach for open positions.
  • Manager accountability: track diversity goals and tie part of managers’ evaluations or bonuses to them.
