Practice 2: Trust and rights

Decide whether each fictional practice builds trust and respects rights, or crosses the line.

Takeaway: Federal agencies investigate and prosecute, but they rely on state and local partners to share information, detect threats, and respond first. Partnerships work only with community trust, which grows when agencies focus on behaviors, not appearance, and always respect constitutional rights.
