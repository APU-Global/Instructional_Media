About the course

The understanding and application of research, theories, and strategies in personnel selection, training, and development. The topics include such areas as individual differences, recruitment, selection, placement, appraisal, feedback, and measurement, assessing bias in selection, training methods, learning and development programs, evaluation, employment legislation, and organizational design issues necessary for planning evaluation and improvement strategies. Along the way you look at human resource management from a Christian worldview, including a theology of work.

Projected total

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 50 points. Discussions 1–7 follow the rhythm above; Discussion 8 is “Respond by Wednesday. Discuss through Friday.” Here are the course’s discussion requirements.

INITIAL POST
250–300 words
Answer every part of the week’s prompt, backed by your textbooks and current research.
RESPONSES TO PEERS
150–250 words each
Respond to a minimum of three peers in an exchange of ideas that continues through the second deadline.
SOURCES
A minimum of one APA-formatted source, where required. Posts and responses made after the academic week do not count toward your grade.

Discussions are graded with the syllabus discussion rubric: Engagement (10), Critical Thinking (20), Contributions to Discussion (15), and Communication and Collaboration (5), for 50 points.

Welcome aboard: To manage others, you first have to know yourself.
