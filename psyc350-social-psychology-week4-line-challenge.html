The Line Challenge

Would you go along with the group?

In the 1950s, Solomon Asch asked people to judge which of three lines matched a standard line. The task was easy. The twist: everyone else in the room was in on it.

You will join a group of six “participants.” They answer out loud, one at a time, and you answer last. Just say what you see.

9 short trials. Answer honestly. There are no points and nothing is graded until the end.

Listen to the group…

Which comparison line (A, B or C) is the same length as the standard line?
