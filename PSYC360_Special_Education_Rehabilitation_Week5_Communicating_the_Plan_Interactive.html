Week 5 Interactive: Communicating the Plan

Week 5 objective: Communicate a learning plan clearly to families, students, and school staff, and listen before you advise.

The laws behind the conversation

Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act (1973) bars disability discrimination in programs that receive federal funding. The ADA (1990) extends protections to employment and public facilities. IDEA focuses on education: a free, appropriate education, due process, fair evaluation, the least restrictive environment, and an individualized educational program. Whatever the law, the plan only works if every stakeholder understands it, so adjust your words for each listener.

Choose the law each situation describes.

Practice 2: Who is this message written for?

Part 3 of the Individual Learning Plan asks you to present one plan three ways. Each message is about a fictional student named Eli. Choose the audience it fits best.

Practice 3: Listening or advising too soon?

A parent says, "I'm worried the teachers just see Eli as a problem." Decide whether each reply is active listening or a jump to advice.

Takeaway: Know which law applies, remove barriers such as jargon and inflexible meeting times, change your register for each audience, and listen before you advise.
