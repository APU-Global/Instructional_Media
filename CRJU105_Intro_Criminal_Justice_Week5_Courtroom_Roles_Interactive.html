Week 5 Interactive: Court System Roles and Sentencing

Week 5 objective: Identify the roles of judges, prosecutors, defense attorneys, and court staff, and recognize the limits courts have placed on capital punishment.

The courtroom workgroup

Eisenstein and Jacob (1977) coined the term courtroom workgroup for the cooperative relationship among prosecutors, defense attorneys, and judges, who work together to resolve most cases efficiently. Court staff, such as clerks, bailiffs, indigency verification officers, and release assistance officers, keep the courthouse running. Because most cases end in a plea rather than a trial, the key player in many cases is the prosecutor rather than the judge.

Takeaway: The judge is the face of the courtroom, but outcomes are a team effort: prosecutors choose cases and shape pleas, defense attorneys protect the accused at every critical stage, and court staff keep the process moving. On the death penalty, courts decide what the Eighth Amendment allows, while questions like deterrence and morality are left to policy and conscience.
