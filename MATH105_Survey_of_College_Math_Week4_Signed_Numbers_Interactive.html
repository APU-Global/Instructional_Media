Week 4 Interactive: Signed Numbers and the Real Number Line

Week 4 objective: Add, subtract, multiply, and divide signed numbers using the order of operations, and classify real numbers as rational or irrational.

Rules to keep in view

  • Subtracting a negative is adding: a − (−b) = a + b. The opposite of a negative is positive: −(−a) = a.
  • A product of two negatives is positive. An even number of negative factors gives a positive product; an odd number gives a negative product.
  • Parentheses set the base of an exponent: (−3)⁴ = 81, but −3⁴ = −81.
  • Order of operations: grouping symbols (including absolute value), exponents, multiplication and division left to right, then addition and subtraction left to right.

Practice 2: where does it live?

Choose the most specific set for each number. Whole numbers are 0, 1, 2, 3, …; integers add the negatives; rational numbers can be written as a/b with integers a and b, b ≠ 0; irrational numbers cannot.
