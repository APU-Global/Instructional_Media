Week 3 Interactive: Group Development and Cohesion

Week 3 focus: Discussion 3 asks how biblical principles on small groups apply to workplace teams. Use this activity to recognize where a team is in its development and whether its cohesion is helping, so you can say where principles such as encouraging and building one another up fit.

Tuckman’s five stages at a glance

1Forming. Polite, guarded, testing the rules and the leader.
2Storming. More authentic and argumentative; power and tasks are contested.
3Norming. Ground rules, roles, and goals agreed; cohesion rises.
4Performing. Work gets done, and the group examines how it works.
5Adjourning. The group closes, ideally with a debrief and celebration.
!Not always linear. Disruptions can send a group back to storming (punctuated equilibrium).

Part 1: Which stage is this?

Each team is fictional. Choose the stage each moment best illustrates, then select Check my answers.

Part 2: Is cohesion helping?

Cohesion is the degree of camaraderie in a group. Decide whether it is helping this group or has become a risk.

Takeaway: Groups commonly move through forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning, though disruptions can send them back to storming. Cohesion usually supports performance, but only when the group’s norms favor the task; too much cohesion can silence dissent. In Discussion 3, connect a biblical principle to a specific stage or cohesion problem you would expect in a workplace team.
