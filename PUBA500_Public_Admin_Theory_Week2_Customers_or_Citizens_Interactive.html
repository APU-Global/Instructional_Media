Week 2 objective: Place new public management in the history of public sector reform and recognize the shifts toward citizen-centric government.

Four eras of public management

  • Mid-1960s to late 1970s: rational, hierarchical planning and cost-benefit analysis; science and expertise will produce progress.
  • Late 1970s to late 1990s: new public management; business techniques to improve efficiency; "better management" as the solution to many problems.
  • Late 1990s to 2010: no dominant model; governance, networks, partnerships, "joining up," transparency, and trust.
  • 2010 onwards: participatory democracy; open data as the default for government information.

Timeline from Pollitt and Bouckaert (2011), with a fourth wave added by the authors Betchoo cites (Chapter 14).
