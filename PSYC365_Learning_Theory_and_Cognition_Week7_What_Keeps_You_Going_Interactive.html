Week 7 Interactive: Motivation, Setbacks, and Perseverance

Week 7 objective: Recognize motivation theories in everyday situations and identify explanations for setbacks that keep people going.

What the readings say

Foundations of Learning Theories links motivation to self-efficacy, our belief that we can complete a task, which shapes our ability to stay on task and build grit and resilience. Attribution theory sorts explanations for success and failure by locus (internal or external), stability, and controllability; blaming too little effort, an internal and controllable cause, leads to more effort next time than blaming bad luck. Mastery goals focus on growth and hold up better under challenge than performance goals, which focus on looking competent. Arousal theory says we seek an optimal level of stimulation, and the overjustification effect shows that external rewards can reduce motivation a person already had.
