Week 3 Interactive: Vision, Goals, and Performance

Week 3 objective: Translate a public health vision into a mission, measurable objectives, strategies, and action steps.

The scenario

Fresh Start Eastside is a fictional coalition working to reduce diabetes risk in a neighborhood with few places to buy fresh produce. Its planning team drafted the statements below using the Community Tool Box VMOSA process.

  • Vision: the dream, the ideal conditions.
  • Mission: what the group will do and why.
  • Objective: how much of what will be accomplished by when.
  • Strategy: how the objectives will be reached.
  • Action step: who will do what by when, with what resources.

Practice 1: which VMOSA layer?

Choose the layer each draft statement belongs to.

Practice 2: what type of objective?

The Tool Box names three types of objectives. Classify each Fresh Start objective.

Practice 3: what is missing from SMART + C?

Each draft objective fails one SMART + C criterion. Choose the main gap.

Takeaway: A vision inspires; objectives make it accountable. Write each objective as how much of what by when, compared with a baseline, and check it against SMART + C before you build your budget and evaluation plan.

VMOSA layers, objective types, and SMART + C from Nagy and Fawcett, Section 1. An Overview of Strategic Planning or "VMOSA" and Section 3. Creating Objectives (Community Tool Box, Chapter 8, University of Kansas). Fresh Start Eastside and its people are fictional.
