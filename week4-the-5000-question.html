    Common law vs. civil law

    Legal systems shape how contracts are written, how disputes are settled, and where you look for the rules. Mexico, where GlobalTech is headed, has a civil-law tradition. The U.S. is a common-law system.

    Common law

    • Judicial decisions and precedent are a major source of law.
    • Adversarial process: lawyers argue opposing sides before a neutral judge or jury.
    • Examples: United States (most states), United Kingdom, India.
    • Tendency: long, explicit contracts, because courts interpret the contract’s words.

    Civil law

    • Comprehensive written codes are the primary source of law.
    • Judges apply the code and often play a more investigative role.
    • Examples: Mexico, Germany, France, Japan.
    • Tendency: contracts can rely on the code to fill gaps, so know the code and use local counsel.
