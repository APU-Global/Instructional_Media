Week 4 Interactive: The AI Speechwriter Check

Week 4 objective: Recognize Cialdini's principles of persuasion and the fallacies to remove from an AI-drafted speech, as in Discussion 4.

Six principles of persuasion (Cialdini)

  • Reciprocity: people feel they should give back when someone gives first.
  • Scarcity: people value what is rare or may disappear.
  • Authority: references to experts and expertise build credibility.
  • Commitment and consistency: people follow through on what they have written or signed.
  • Consensus: people follow the lead of peers.
  • Liking: people are drawn to those who like them and seem similar.

Practice 1: name the principle

An AI tool drafted lines for a fictional speech launching a global clean-water initiative. Choose the principle each line uses.

Practice 2: catch the fallacy

Ethical persuasion avoids false logic. Choose the fallacy in each AI-suggested line.

Takeaway: Persuasion moves people; ethics decides how. Use the six principles only when they are true, remove fallacies, never pretend certainty, and cut any AI line you do not believe yourself.
