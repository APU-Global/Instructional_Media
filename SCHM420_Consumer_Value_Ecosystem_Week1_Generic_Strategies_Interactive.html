Week 1 Interactive: Generic Strategies and Best Value Supply Chains

Week 1 objective: Identify the generic strategy behind a firm's value proposition and recognize the four measures a best value supply chain balances.

Key terms

Porter's generic strategies rest on two choices: the source of advantage (low cost or uniqueness) and the scope (a broad market or a narrow segment). That gives cost leadership, differentiation, focused cost leadership, and focused differentiation. A rare firm offers both low prices and desirable unique features (best-cost); a firm that offers neither is stuck in the middle.

A best value supply chain balances four measures: speed (cycle time from start to finish), quality (reliability of supply chain activities), cost (lower expenses, or more customer benefit at the same cost), and flexibility (responsiveness to changes in customers' needs).
