Week 5 Interactive: Policy Advocacy Skills

Week 5 objective: Propose strategies for advocating public health policy to address social determinants of health.

Three terms

Advocacy educates about an issue and its impact on individuals and communities.
Direct lobbying is communication with legislators, their staff, or officials who help write legislation that (1) refers to specific legislation and (2) reflects a view on it.
Grassroots lobbying tries to influence the opinions of the general public about specific legislation, reflects a view on it, and includes a call to action.

Tax-exempt status sets limits on lobbying, but much public health advocacy is not lobbying at all (Martin, 2012).
