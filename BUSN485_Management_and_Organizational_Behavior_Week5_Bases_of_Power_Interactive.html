Week 5 Interactive: Bases of Power

Week 5 objective: Identify the base of power a person is using and think about how people are likely to respond.

Six bases of power

Power is the ability to influence the behavior of others to get what you want. Building on French and Raven, the reading names six bases. Legitimate: from a role or position. Reward: the ability to grant pay, perks, attractive assignments, or praise. Coercive: the ability to take something away or punish noncompliance; it works through fear. Expert: knowledge and skill. Information: access to specific information. Referent: others like, respect, and want to be like the person (charisma).

Practice: Which base of power?

Each person is fictional. Choose the base of power each one is mainly using, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Three bases come largely from a position (legitimate, reward, coercive) and three from the person (expert, information, referent). Power that rests on expertise and respect does not disappear when the title does. As the Week 5 overview notes, Jesus' influence did not come from a title.
