Week 3 Interactive: Conflict-Handling Styles

Week 3 objective: Identify the five conflict-handling styles and judge when each one fits the situation.

Two dimensions, five styles

Conflict-handling styles differ in how assertive (pressing your own concerns) and how cooperative (attending to the other side’s concerns) they are. Avoiding is low on both; accommodating is cooperative but unassertive; competing is assertive but uncooperative; compromising sits in the middle; collaborating is high on both. There is no single right style: successful people match the style to the situation.

Practice: which style is this?

Each workplace is fictional. Choose the style each person is using, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Keep disagreements focused on the task, not the person. Avoid trivial fights, compete only when something unethical or harmful is at stake, and reach for collaboration when both sides’ concerns matter.
