Step 4: Lean and green distribution

Decide whether each statement is true or false, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: The product picks the mode: value, weight, distance, and urgency decide between truck, rail, air, water, pipeline, and intermodal. A weighted factor rating makes location trade-offs visible, and the weights matter: leave environmental fit out and a different site can win. Lean, green distribution such as crossdocking cuts storage, handling, and delay.
