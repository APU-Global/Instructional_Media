Week 5 Interactive: Infrastructure and Security

Week 5 objective: Classify internal and external security threats by the CIA principle they endanger and match each to an effective control.

The CIA triad

Confidentiality
Only those allowed to see information can access it.
Integrity
Information has not been altered and truly represents what is intended.
Availability
Authorized people can access information in an appropriate timeframe.

Threats come from inside (fraud, misuse of access, back doors) and outside (malware, spoofing, denial of service, phishing and pretexting), and some come from accidents. Organizations layer technical, physical, and administrative controls.

Practice: six security incidents

Organizations are fictional. Answer all three questions for each incident, then select Check my answers.

Takeaway: Map every threat to confidentiality, integrity, or availability, ask whether it comes from inside or outside, and layer controls: authentication and access control, encryption, firewalls and IDS, backups, physical security, separation of duties, and training.
