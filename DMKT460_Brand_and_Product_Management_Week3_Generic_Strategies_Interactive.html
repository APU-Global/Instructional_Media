Week 3 Interactive: Porter's Generic Strategies

Week 3 objective: Identify the generic strategy a product follows and judge whether its strategy fits the product.

Two decisions, four strategies

Porter's framework turns on two choices: the source of advantage (low cost or uniqueness) and the scope (a broad market or a narrow segment). Cost leadership offers acceptable quality to a broad market at a low price. Differentiation offers unique, desirable features that a broad market will pay a premium for. Focused cost leadership offers low prices relative to rivals within a narrow market, and focused differentiation offers specialized features to a narrow market. A firm that is neither unique enough nor cheap enough is stuck in the middle.
