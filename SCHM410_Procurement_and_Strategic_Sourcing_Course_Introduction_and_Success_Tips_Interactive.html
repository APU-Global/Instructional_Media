About the course

Emphasis on the importance of negotiations and contract management. Students examine the role of procurement within the supply chain process as well as the selection, evaluation, and leverage of supplier relationships for the most cost effective, optimal procurement solutions. Because modern supply chains are complex, purchasing managers must also become procurement specialists who keep evaluating the costs and gaps in the chain to improve it.

Respond by Thursday. Discuss through Saturday.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required). Week 5 asks you to connect procurement ethics with Christian values.

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
