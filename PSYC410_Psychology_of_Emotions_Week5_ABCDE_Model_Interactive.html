Week 5 Interactive: The ABCDE Model

Week 5 objective: Use the ABCDE model to identify the beliefs behind an adverse event and dispute them with evidence.

The five steps

A: Adversity
What happened (Discussion 5 calls this the activating event).
B: Beliefs
What you told yourself about it.
C: Consequences
How you felt and acted because of those beliefs.
D: Disputation
Evidence against the beliefs and other ways to see the event. Ask: What is the evidence? What else could explain this? Is it as bad as I am making it? Is dwelling on it useful?
E: Energization
How you feel once the beliefs have been disputed (Discussion 5 calls this the effective new approach).

Practice 2: optimistic or pessimistic explanation?

Pessimists explain bad events as internal, stable, and global; optimists explain them as external, variable, and specific. For good events the pattern reverses. Classify each explanation.

Takeaway: Events do not create consequences directly; beliefs about them do. Catch the belief, treat it as if someone else had said it, and argue back with evidence and specific, changeable explanations. That is how you move from consequences to energization.
