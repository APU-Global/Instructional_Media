Week 4 Interactive: Crisis Communication Readiness

Week 4 objective: Apply issues management and the Entergy hurricane lessons to judge whether an agency is ready to communicate in a crisis.

Key ideas from the reading

  • Issues scanning, monitoring, and analysis is arguably the most important phase: once an issue reaches the public policy arena, the organization loses control of how it is defined.
  • Entergy turned lessons from Hurricane Ivan into a new plan: a cross-functional review, supervisor talking points and meetings, and the IEStormNet website for employees and families.
  • During Katrina it moved its command center early and used backup channels (daily CEO e-mails, an information line, printed newsletters, radio announcements) when phones failed.
  • Lessons: employees are the most important public; keep detailed plans and test them with frequent drills; get communicators to a pre-equipped command center quickly.

    Takeaway: Crisis communication is won before the crisis. Scan for issues, turn past lessons into a written plan, start with your own employees, prepare backup channels, and drill the plan until everyone knows it.
