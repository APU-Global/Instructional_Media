Week 2 Interactive: Forecasting Methods

Week 2 objective: Calculate simple and weighted moving averages, an exponential smoothing forecast, and the mean absolute deviation, then identify demand patterns and forecasting methods.

Why this matters

Demand forecasts drive production scheduling, capacity, inventory, and workforce planning. Quantitative methods use past demand when patterns are expected to continue; qualitative methods rely on judgment when data are scarce, such as for a new product. Your Forecasting Method and Analysis assignment asks you to graph historical trends and use them to inform operational strategy.

Takeaway: Moving averages smooth recent demand, weighted averages and exponential smoothing respond faster to change, and MAD tells you how far off a forecast runs on average. Always check the demand pattern first: trend, seasonality, and cycles call for different methods than stable, random demand.
