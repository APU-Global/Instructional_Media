Week 6 objective: Define a metric for success and the tools to measure it, as Discussion 6 and Capstone Project Part 3 ask.

Relative change vs. percentage points

The Community Tool Box framework’s Drive Smart example explains that a decline from 10% to 8% is a 20% relative decrease, or a decrease of 2 percentage points.

RELATIVE CHANGE
(new − baseline) ÷ baseline × 100. From 10% to 8%: (8 − 10) ÷ 10 = −20%.
PERCENTAGE POINTS
new − baseline. From 10% to 8%: 2 points lower.
