Week 7 Interactive: Measuring Campaigns

Week 7 objective: Relate social media analytics to campaign effectiveness by separating objectives, goals, KPIs, and targets, spotting vanity metrics, and calculating funnel conversion rates.

The vocabulary

Objective: a desired outcome of a campaign. Goal: the defined action you want people to take. KPI (key performance indicator): a metric that shows whether an objective is being achieved. Target: a specific numeric benchmark. A single impressive number with no context, such as “5,000 fans,” is a vanity metric; trends over time and engagement (likes, comments, shares) tell you more.

Takeaway: Start with the objective, choose KPIs that show progress toward it, set targets, and watch trends and funnel steps, not trophies. Honest measurement shows where to improve.
