The timing mismatch

Move the slider to an age. The limbic system, which regulates emotion and reward, is linked to the hormonal changes of puberty (on average puberty begins at about 10–11 for girls and 11–12 for boys). The prefrontal cortex, which supports impulse control, planning, and decision making, does not fully develop until the mid-20s.

Takeaway: Adolescence is a sensitive window: the reward system matures about ten years before the prefrontal cortex, half of mental illness begins by age 14, and family adversity, peers, gender, sleep, and recent stressors shape who struggles. The same brain is also highly adaptive. When you explain higher rates in Discussion 4, combine the biological timing with the social context.
