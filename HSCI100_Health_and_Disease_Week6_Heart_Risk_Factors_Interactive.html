Week 6 Interactive: Heart Risk Lab

Week 6 objective: Separate modifiable from non-modifiable cardiovascular risk factors and build an evidence-based action plan (CLO 1, CLO 4).

Practice 1: Can it be changed?

Discussion 6 asks you to identify modifiable and non-modifiable risk factors. Sort each one.

Practice 2: Heart facts

Decide whether each statement is true or false.

Practice 3: Build an action plan

Case (fictional): Marcus, 52, is an accountant who sits most of the day and feels constant deadline pressure. His father had a heart attack at 58. Marcus smokes about half a pack a day, eats fast food most weekdays, and has several drinks most evenings. His doctor says his blood pressure is high.

Check the steps that belong in a plan to reduce Marcus's risk, then check your plan.

    Takeaway: You cannot change your genes, age, or background, but you can change smoking, drinking, activity, diet, and how you handle stress. A DASH-style diet and regular exercise lower blood pressure.
