Water, Source to Tap

Safe drinking water is one of the foundations of a healthy community, and keeping it safe takes treatment, testing, and many agencies working together. Follow water from its source to the tap, see what can go wrong, and sort contaminants by the harm they cause.

Part 2 · Quick illness or lasting harm?

Water can carry microbes, chemicals, and metals. Microbial contamination causes acute illness; chemical contaminants, such as lead, may cause long-term harm. Sort each contaminant.

Acute illnessSickness that appears soon after exposure
Long-term harmEffects that build up or appear over years

Part 3 · Standards, agencies, and disasters

Many agencies, one goal. Water is a useful public health topic because in many communities several public agencies must work together to keep it free of pollution and other contaminants. Understanding how they cooperate is essential for public health professionals.
