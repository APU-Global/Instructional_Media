About the course

Examination of financial management principles and practices. Students analyze concepts related to corporate finance, investments, and capital markets within a global business context. The course also focuses on financial statement analysis, long-term financial planning, and implementation of organizational performance measures. You will also apply legal and ethical guidelines and a Christian worldview to financial decisions.

Each of the eight discussions is worth 40 points. Here is what a complete week of discussion looks like.

INITIAL POST
250–350 words
or a 1–3 minute video/audio post. Use 2–3 quotations or ideas from the week’s reading and videos.
TWO COLLEAGUE RESPONSES
125–200 words each
or 30 seconds to 2 minutes each.
YOUR VOICE
Write in an authentic, personal voice and credit your sources (APA format is not required).

Discussions are graded with a 3-criterion rubric worth 15, 15, and 10 points (40 total).
