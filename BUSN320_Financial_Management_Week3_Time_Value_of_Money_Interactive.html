Week 3 Interactive: Time Value of Money

Week 3 objective: Use future value, present value, net present value, and internal rate of return to decide whether money is worth more now or later.

Explorer: watch money compound

Future value = PV × (1 + R/p)n × p, where R is the annual rate, n the years, and p the compounding periods per year. Change the inputs and watch the value grow.

Future value

Grey bar: today's amount. Red bars: value at the end of each year.

Practice: five decisions

Use the formulas, a calculator, or a spreadsheet. Enter dollar answers to the cent (within $1 is accepted), then select Check.

Takeaway: Time discounts value. A higher rate, more years, or more frequent compounding raises future value and lowers present value. A project is worth doing when its NPV, at the return you could earn elsewhere, is above zero; equivalently, when its IRR beats that rate.
