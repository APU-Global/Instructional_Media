Week 5 Interactive: Applying PEN-3

Week 5 objective: Classify cultural factors with the PEN-3 model and choose health messages that start from a community’s strengths, as the Week 5 reading recommends for COVID-19, Ebola, and HIV.

Three domains, nine factors

PEN-3 (first published in 1989) has three domains, each with three factors. The examples in this activity come from the authors’ table applying PEN-3 to COVID-19, Ebola, and HIV.

Cultural identity
Person · Extended family · Neighborhood
Relationships and expectations
Perceptions · Enablers · Nurturers
Cultural empowerment
Positive · Existential · Negative

Part 1: Perceptions, Enablers, or Nurturers?

Each item comes from the authors’ table. Choose its factor in the relationships and expectations domain, then select Check Part 1.

Part 2: Positive, Existential, or Negative?

The authors’ key: positive factors are to be promoted, existential factors to be recognized, and negative factors to change. Classify each item, then select Check Part 2.

Part 3: Which message fits PEN-3?

The messages below are practice examples written for this activity. Choose the one that better follows the authors’ advice, then select Check Part 3.

Takeaway: Begin with what a community does right. PEN-3 asks you to promote positive factors, recognize existential ones, and change only what is negative, and to address individual and community risk. Use it when you design the health education strategy for Part 3 of your plan.
