PSYC 520 | 8-Week Course

Social and Cultural Psychology

Explore how culture, identity, and systems shape the way we think and work with others, and plan how you will succeed in this course.

What You'll Learn

By the end of this course you will be able to do the following.

1. Apply Culture and Diversity Models

Use established models to understand culture and diversity in real settings.

2. Examine Emotional Intelligence

Look at professional situations through the lens of emotional intelligence.

3. Communicate Ethically and Professionally

Share information in ways that are ethical and professional.

4. Manage Perceptions of Stereotypes

Use cultural humility and openness to manage perceptions of stereotypes.

5. Evaluate Cultural Identity

Evaluate your understanding of cultural identity, including race, gender, sexuality, geography, age, and faith.

Success Tips

1
Start each week with the Overview. Read the week's learning outcomes and due dates first.
2
Post early in discussions. An early initial post leaves time for thoughtful replies to classmates.
3
Build the Cultural Exploration Paper in parts. It is the largest part of your grade, so work on each part as it is assigned.
4
Reflect honestly. Self-awareness is a core skill, so use the interactives to notice your own assumptions.
5
Ask for help early. Contact your instructor with questions, and visit the Professor Corner and Career Corner for support.
