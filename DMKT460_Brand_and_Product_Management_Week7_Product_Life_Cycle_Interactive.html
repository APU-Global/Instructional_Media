Week 7 Interactive: The Product Life Cycle

Week 7 objective: Identify a product's life-cycle stage from market signals and choose the marketing approach that fits it.

Four stages

Introduction: high marketing costs, low profits, building awareness and trial; pricing often uses penetration (low) or skimming (high). Growth: rising sales and profits, competitors enter, promotion stresses why this brand is better, and distribution expands. Maturity: sales level off, most buyers are repeat customers, competition is intense; firms modify the market, product, or strategy to extend it. Decline: sales fall; firms cut costs, harvest, divest, or move customers to newer products.

Practice 2: match the message to the stage

Takeaway: Know which season your product is in. Each stage calls for different objectives and a different marketing mix, from awareness and trial at launch, to differentiation in growth, to extension in maturity, to harvesting or transition in decline.
