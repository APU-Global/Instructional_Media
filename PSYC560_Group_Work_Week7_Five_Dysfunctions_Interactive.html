Try it with your Signature Assignment group

The Reader’s Guide suggests a Personal Histories exercise to start building trust: each member shares where they grew up, how many siblings they have and where they fall in the birth order, and their worst job as a kid. It helps team members slowly get to know one another and begin to break down barriers.

Takeaway: Lencioni’s five dysfunctions build on one another: absence of trust, fear of conflict, lack of commitment, avoidance of accountability, and inattention to results. Trust begins with the willingness to admit weaknesses and mistakes, and healthy teams keep working to answer “yes” to the five questions. In Discussion 7, name the dysfunction you have seen most and a specific step you would take to address it.
